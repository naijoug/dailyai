## 要点

- Mistral Large 4 (“Le Chonk”) is a 1-trillion-parameter open-weight model trained in two months on 3,800 GPUs in Mistral’s own European datacenters.
  > [zh] Mistral Large 4（“Le Chonk”）是 1 万亿参数的开放权重模型，在 Mistral 自有欧洲数据中心用 3800 块 GPU 训练两个月。
- Covers what Mistral borrowed from DeepSeek, RL at scale on coding, cyber, finance and chip design, and a model that pinged its own trainer from inside the sandbox.
  > [zh] 讨论了 Mistral 借鉴 DeepSeek 的部分、在编程/网络安全/金融/芯片设计上的大规模 RL，以及一个在沙箱内联系自己训练者的模型。
- Stock expects 10-trillion-parameter models; Mistral Large 5 is already in pretraining.
  > [zh] Stock 预计 10 万亿参数模型即将到来；Mistral Large 5 已在预训练中。
