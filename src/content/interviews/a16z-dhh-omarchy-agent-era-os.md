## 要点

- DHH argues the agent era is reopening a window in computing that has been closed for decades: the chance to build a new operating system people actually want to use; Omarchy is his agent-first Linux distribution, designed around a computer you can modify simply by asking.
  > [zh] DHH 认为 Agent 时代重新打开了计算领域关闭了几十年的一扇窗：做一个人们真正想用的新操作系统；Omarchy 就是他的「Agent 优先」Linux 发行版，围绕「开口就能修改的电脑」来设计。
- The command lines, config files and detailed error messages that once made Linux intimidating now make it unusually well suited to agents.
  > [zh] 曾让 Linux 显得难以接近的命令行、配置文件和详细报错，如今反而让它格外适合 Agent 操作。
- Software development has become cheap enough, he says, to challenge decades-old platform lock-in; if code becomes abundant, taste, craft and the willingness to obsess over the final 2% matter more, not less.
  > [zh] 他认为软件开发已经便宜到足以挑战几十年的平台锁定；代码变得充裕之后，品味、手艺和死磕最后 2% 的意愿反而更重要。
- The conversation also covers tiny teams wielding the productive capacity of thousands of people, open models and competition at the frontier, and the return of hacker, demo and pseudonymous internet culture.
  > [zh] 节目还谈到小团队拥有上千人的生产力、开源模型与前沿竞争，以及黑客文化、demo 文化和匿名互联网文化的回归。
