## 要点

- 刘松铭说，去年底创业时就看到基础大模型可能进入具身大脑的规划和调度层（System 2），所以 LiberAI 从第一天起就做全栈：精细操作能力、触觉和力觉的数据采集与训练，以及机器人本体。
  > [en] Liu says that when he founded the company late last year he already saw foundation models moving into the planning and scheduling layer of robot brains (System 2), so LiberAI was full-stack from day one: dexterous manipulation, touch and force data collection and training, and its own robot body.
- 节目详谈刚发布的世界动作模型 Liber-0 的三项验证：零样本长程任务、数据效率实验和全栈优化协同；它用 220 万小时人类经验预训练，并讨论了为什么数据手套采到的数据能超过遥操作数据。
  > [en] The episode walks through three validations of the newly released world action model Liber-0: zero-shot long-horizon tasks, data-efficiency experiments and full-stack co-optimization; it was pretrained on 2.2 million hours of human experience, and they discuss why glove-captured data can beat teleoperation data.
- 硬件为模型而设计：机器人本体 LEO 有能弯、能扭的腰和能转的手腕，让人类操作经验更容易迁移；预训练分三个阶段，从「看」走到「做」。
  > [en] The hardware is designed for the model: the LEO robot has a waist that bends and twists and wrists that rotate, making human manipulation experience easier to transfer; pretraining runs in three stages, from "seeing" to "doing".
- 谈到 GPT-6 Astra 带来的冲击，他的判断是：Astra 擅长理解和规划，不擅长强接触任务和「最后一毫米」；基模做 System 2，创业公司要啃 System 1 的硬骨头，从模型走到商业价值中间还隔着场景基础设施。
  > [en] On the shock from GPT-6 Astra, his view is that Astra is good at understanding and planning but not at contact-rich tasks and the "last millimeter"; foundation models take System 2 while startups take on the hard System 1 problems, and scene infrastructure still separates models from commercial value.
- 不到一年拿了 6 轮融资，公司却选在北京清河附近更远、更旧、更便宜的老工业园区；他强调资本纪律、风险管理和对硬件与场景的敬畏。
  > [en] After six funding rounds in under a year, the company still chose an older, cheaper industrial park near Qinghe in northwest Beijing rather than Wudaokou; he stresses capital discipline, risk management and respect for hardware and real-world settings.
