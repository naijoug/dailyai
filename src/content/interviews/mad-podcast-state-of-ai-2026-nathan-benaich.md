## 要点

- AI is now building AI: Claude leads 26% of Anthropic's own model R&D, up from under 1% in February, but Benaich thinks "research taste" is the wall.
  > [zh] AI 已经在造 AI：Claude 主导了 Anthropic 自身 26% 的模型研发（2 月时不到 1%），但 Benaich 认为“研究品味”才是瓶颈。
- He now ranks cyber risk above existential risk, citing OpenAI's evaluation agents ending up inside Hugging Face's systems, and predicts a frontier model's weights will get stolen.
  > [zh] 他现在认为网络安全风险比生存风险更紧迫，举了 OpenAI 评测 Agent 闯进 Hugging Face 系统的例子，并预测会有前沿模型权重被盗。
- Catching up to OpenAI or Anthropic from scratch costs about $1 trillion; $50B of US data-center projects are stalled and all 27 EU countries together have fewer GPUs than one top US cluster.
  > [zh] 从零追上 OpenAI 或 Anthropic 约需 1 万亿美元；美国有 500 亿美元数据中心项目受阻，欧盟 27 国的 GPU 总数还不如美国一个顶级集群。
- On agents: whether models will absorb the harness, why he won't give a personal agent his credit card, Codex beyond coding for knowledge work, and a solo GP whose agent ran 12 expert interviews overnight.
  > [zh] Agent 话题：模型会不会吞掉 harness、他为何不肯把信用卡交给个人 Agent、Codex 走出编程用于知识工作，以及一位单人 GP 的 Agent 一夜跑完 12 场专家访谈。
