## 要点

- Silver and Espeholt plan an agent that starts knowing nothing and discovers science, maths and language by direct experience instead of pre-training.
  > [zh] Silver 与 Espeholt 计划打造一个从零开始、通过直接经验而非预训练去发现科学、数学和语言的 Agent。
- Silver argues human-language reasoning is a ceiling, not a foundation, and that chain-of-thought monitoring as alignment is reaching its limits.
  > [zh] Silver 认为人类语言推理是天花板而非地基，用思维链监控做对齐已接近极限。
- His forecast: strong evidence within a year, superintelligence in three.
  > [zh] 他的预测：一年内出现有力证据，三年内实现超级智能。
