## 要点

- Carina went from math competitions in Guangzhou to math and physics at MIT, a Rhodes Scholarship and Stanford's JD/PhD program, which she left to start Axiom; she met her co-founder in a coffee shop.
  > [zh] Carina 从广州的数学竞赛起步，读了 MIT 数学和物理，拿到罗德奖学金，进入斯坦福法学博士/哲学博士联读项目后退学创办 Axiom；她和联合创始人是在咖啡馆认识的。
- Axiom focuses on verified code generation, using Lean for mathematical proofs, and brings together people from mathematics, machine learning and hardware.
  > [zh] Axiom 专注可验证的代码生成，用 Lean 写数学证明，团队由数学、机器学习和硬件背景的人组成。
- Her vision of "quantitative superintelligence" is systems that generate new conjectures, develop theories and connect mathematical reasoning to practical software.
  > [zh] 她说的“量化超级智能”，是能提出新猜想、发展理论，并把数学推理和实用软件连起来的系统。
- The episode also covers how verification differs from a benchmark score, the gap between benchmark marketing and real capability, open versus closed models, physical systems that need stronger guarantees, and her timeline for capable AI agents.
  > [zh] 节目还谈到验证和跑分有什么不同、跑分宣传和真实能力的差距、开源与闭源模型、需要更强保证的物理系统，以及她对强能力 AI Agent 何时到来的判断。
