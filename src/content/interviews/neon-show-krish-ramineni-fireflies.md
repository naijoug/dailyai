## 要点

- Ramineni and Sam Udotong started Fireflies ten years ago, before “AI note taker” was a category; per the show notes they built it into a billion-dollar company on a $5 million seed and didn’t touch their $14 million Series A until they were profitable.
  > [zh] Ramineni 和 Sam Udotong 十年前创办 Fireflies，那时「AI 会议记录」还不是一个品类；据节目简介，他们靠 500 万美元种子轮做成了 10 亿美元估值的公司，在盈利之前没有动用 1400 万美元的 A 轮资金。
- The notes say Fireflies sits in meetings at 75% of Fortune 500 companies and has 20 million monthly users across 100 countries, built by a fully remote team in 20 countries; the episode also says it is used by 1 in 3 Indian unicorns.
  > [zh] 简介称 Fireflies 进入了 75% 的财富 500 强公司的会议，月活用户 2000 万、覆盖 100 个国家，团队完全远程、分布在 20 个国家；节目还提到三分之一的印度独角兽在用它。
- The product has grown from a meeting note taker into an AI teammate that triages your inbox, takes dictation and searches across hundreds of work apps; Ramineni’s “value buyer” goal is for one subscription to replace a dozen other AI tools, like a Costco membership that pays for itself.
  > [zh] 产品已从会议记录扩展为 AI 队友：整理收件箱、听写、在数百个工作应用里搜索；Ramineni 以「价值型买家」自居，目标是一个订阅能顶掉十几个其他 AI 工具，就像第一个月就回本的 Costco 会员。
- Chapters also cover going multi-product, why a note taker is building hardware, “interface is overrated,” saying no to growth-stage VCs, the hardest part of building in AI today, and who the 1% of AI winners will be.
  > [zh] 其他章节包括：走向多产品、会议记录公司为什么做硬件、「界面被高估了」、拒绝成长期 VC、如今做 AI 最难的部分，以及谁会成为 AI 赢家里的那 1%。
