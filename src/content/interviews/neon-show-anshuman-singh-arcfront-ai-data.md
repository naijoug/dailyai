## 要点

- Per the show notes, Arcfront went from $0 to around $70M in monthly revenue in 11 months with just $2M raised, working with four of the five frontier labs. It got in by building RL environments for one lab, where a single environment can take a dedicated team two to four weeks, and scaled by hiring many engineers from the Scaler network.
  > [zh] 据节目简介，Arcfront 只融了 200 万美元，11 个月里从零做到月收入约 7000 万美元，客户覆盖五家前沿实验室中的四家。它从给一家实验室搭 RL 环境切入（一个环境往往要一个专职小团队做两到四周），再靠从 Scaler 体系大量招工程师快速扩张。
- He estimates labs will spend $15–20B on data and research this year and argues it is not a gold rush that ends when labs stop spending: as harnesses outside the model companies hold the context and connectors, switching models gets easier, no model stays sticky, and labs must keep investing to stay at the frontier.
  > [zh] 他估计今年各实验室在数据与研究上的投入约 150–200 亿美元，并认为这不是实验室一收手就结束的淘金热：当上下文和连接器都留在模型公司之外的 harness 里，换模型越来越容易，没有哪个模型有黏性，实验室只能持续投入才能留在前沿。
- Labs are now targeting jobs that combine high GDP value with easy verification. Because enterprise context is scattered across email, Slack, Drive and finance tools, they are buying operating data from large companies, such as years of emails, documents and SaaS exports, to use both as pre-training data and as material for RL tasks.
  > [zh] 实验室现在瞄准的是「GDP 价值高、又容易验证」的工作。由于企业里的上下文散落在邮件、Slack、Drive 和财务工具里，它们开始向大公司购买运营数据（多年的邮件、文档和 SaaS 导出），既当预训练数据，也用来构造强化学习任务。
- He is sitting out enterprise post-training for now, says margins are above 50% but cash flow lags because labs take about 30 days to verify delivered work, and would not build an AI app today because even a winner ends up fighting OpenAI and Anthropic head on. If starting now he would look at infrastructure optimization, physical AI for data center operations, and AI-era cybersecurity.
  > [zh] 他暂时不做企业后训练，称毛利率超过 50%，但现金流有滞后，因为实验室要花约 30 天验收交付的工作；他现在不会去做 AI 应用，因为即便做成了也要和 OpenAI、Anthropic 正面竞争。如果今天重新创业，他会看基础设施优化、用于数据中心运维的物理 AI，以及 AI 时代的网络安全。
