## 要点

- 推动转型前，他先和部门负责人聊「能力放大以后能多做哪摊事」，让团队在转型里看见自己的新地盘，而不是只把 AI 当成省人工、抢饭碗的工具。
  > [en] Before pushing change, he asks department heads what extra territory they could take on with amplified capability, so teams see their own opportunity rather than treating AI as a headcount cut.
- 「反向咨询」：从业务材料里反查缺口，例如四套系统讲同一件事却字段、口径各异，先让 AI 把「摆不上台面说的」问题显出来，再由管理层决定是否疏通。
  > [en] "Reverse consulting": mine business materials for gaps—say, four systems describing the same thing with different fields and definitions—let AI surface what nobody will say out loud, then let management decide whether to fix it.
- 外部团队走后企业要能接着做，他强调培养内部 FDE，让企业里的人能继续开发其他项目；人才盘点则看任务和能力如何在人与 AI 之间迁移，人逐渐集中到最需要人的环节。
  > [en] After outside teams leave, the company must keep going, so he emphasizes growing internal FDEs who can build the next projects; talent reviews track how tasks shift between people and AI, with people concentrating where they matter most.
- 起步建议：别漫无目的地梳理流程，先从「钱从哪进、从哪出」找场景，再找公司里已经长出来的试点苗子；他也提醒，项目快做成时才可能出现「最后一刀」式的阻力。
  > [en] Where to start: don't map processes aimlessly—follow where money comes in and goes out, then find pilots already sprouting inside the company; he warns the strongest resistance may appear only when a project is about to succeed.
