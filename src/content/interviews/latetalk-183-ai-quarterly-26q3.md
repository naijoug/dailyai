## 要点

- 个人 AI 助理又一轮尝试：OpenAI Dots、Instinct、Muse 等，为何此刻爆发——Computer Use 成熟、推理成本下降，但也碰上生态高墙。
  > [en] Another wave of personal AI assistants (OpenAI Dots, Instinct, Muse): why now — mature computer use and cheaper inference — and the ecosystem walls they hit.
- 多智能体的一体两面：OpenAI 用一万个 Agent、88 小时挑战 Navier–Stokes，而不受控的协作也带来了 Hugging Face 攻击事件。
  > [en] Two faces of multi-agent systems: OpenAI used 10,000 agents for 88 hours on Navier–Stokes, while uncontrolled collaboration led to the Hugging Face incident.
- GPT-6 Astra 之后通用模型直接成为机器人策略模型，具身领域既兴奋又害怕。
  > [en] After GPT-6 Astra, general models are becoming robot policy models directly — exciting and unsettling for embodied AI.
- 对比两家前沿厂商增速：OpenAI ARR 猛增，以及 Anthropic 上市材料里的大客户、云渠道与 5180 亿美元承诺。
  > [en] Comparing frontier-lab growth: OpenAI’s ARR surge versus Anthropic’s IPO filing — big customers, cloud channels and $518B in commitments.
