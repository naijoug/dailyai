## 要点

- Muse was built to feel like messaging, not an AI app, with a custom avatar, a three-layer status display, an ideas surface and a generative feed.
  > [zh] Muse 被设计成像发消息而非 AI 应用：自定义形象、三层状态显示、灵感面板和生成式信息流。
- The three-layer status system accommodates different levels of technical expertise; generative UI is shipped in a consumer product.
  > [zh] 三层状态系统照顾不同技术水平的用户；生成式 UI 已在消费级产品中落地。
- Demos include turning years of Instagram saves into a shopping catalog and tracking a golf game shot by shot.
  > [zh] 演示包括把多年的 Instagram 收藏变成购物目录，以及逐杆记录高尔夫比赛。
