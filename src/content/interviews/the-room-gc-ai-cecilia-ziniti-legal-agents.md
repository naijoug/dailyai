## 要点

- A former tech-company counsel (Yahoo trust and safety, Cruise), Cecilia says a pre-ChatGPT language model convinced her legal work could change, leading her and co-founder Bardia to build a tool for in-house legal teams.
  > [zh] 曾在 Yahoo 信任与安全团队、Cruise 等科技公司做法务的 Cecilia 说，一个 ChatGPT 之前的语言模型让她确信法律工作会被改变，于是和联合创始人 Bardia 为企业内部法务团队做了这款工具。
- She covers finding early customers by following unexpected market signals, fundraising, what an investor called "violent product-market fit," and staying focused when a new model launch shakes the market.
  > [zh] 她讲了如何顺着意料之外的市场信号找到早期客户、融资经历、投资人口中的「猛烈的 PMF」，以及新模型发布冲击市场时如何保持专注。
- The second half turns to agents: moving from AI interfaces to agents that complete multistep legal tasks, why legal agents need trust, accountability and a record of their work, and who is responsible when an agent acts on your behalf.
  > [zh] 后半段聚焦 Agent：从 AI 界面走向能完成多步骤法律任务的 Agent，法律 Agent 为何需要可信、可追责并留下工作记录，以及 Agent 代你行事时责任归谁。
- She also sketches what a legal team could look like in 2029 and the remaining gaps in trust, data protection and judgment.
  > [zh] 她还描绘了 2029 年法务团队可能的样子，以及在信任、数据保护和判断力上仍存在的差距。
