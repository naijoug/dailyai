## 要点

- Madheswaran argues AI won't just cut law firms' costs: it can make an entirely new class of cases economically viable and expand access to legal representation.
  > [zh] Madheswaran 认为 AI 不只是给律所降本，还能让一整类原本不划算的案件变得可行，扩大普通人获得法律代理的机会。
- Eve automates plaintiff law from the first client call through settlement.
  > [zh] Eve 把原告律师业务从首次客户来电到和解的全流程自动化。
- Some firms using Eve have raised the number of clients each attorney can manage from roughly 100 to 500.
  > [zh] 部分使用 Eve 的律所，每位律师可管理的客户数从约 100 个提升到约 500 个。
- With a16z GP Alex Rampell, he also discusses lawyers shifting toward higher-value advocacy, agents eventually negotiating with other agents, and what happens to the legal system when bringing a case becomes dramatically cheaper.
  > [zh] 他还和 a16z 合伙人 Alex Rampell 聊到律师转向更高价值的辩护工作、Agent 之间未来可能直接谈判，以及起诉成本大降后法律体系会怎样变化。
