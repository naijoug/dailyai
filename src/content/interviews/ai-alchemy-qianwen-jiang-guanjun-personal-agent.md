## 要点

- 节目以一个测试案例开场：千问的 AI 助理替用户争取到 70 元航班延误现金赔偿；讨论的问题是办成一次之后，还要过哪些关，用户下次才敢继续把事交给它（录制于 10 月 27 日，电话代办案例来自当时的测试）。
  > [en] The episode opens with a test case: Qwen’s assistant won a user ¥70 in cash compensation for a delayed flight; the question is what else it must get right after one success before users dare hand it the next errand (recorded Oct 27; the phone-errand case came from testing at the time).
- 模型会出错，系统要兜底：替用户打电话涉及场景、身份与授权，哪些操作必须由用户确认是重点话题。
  > [en] Models make mistakes, so the system has to backstop them: phoning on a user’s behalf raises questions of scenario, identity and authorization, and which actions require user confirmation is a central topic.
- 个性化与记忆要权衡记什么、记多少；从尝鲜到常用，要同时顾及能力上限、体验下限和成本。
  > [en] Personalization and memory mean deciding what and how much to remember; going from novelty to habit requires balancing the capability ceiling, the experience floor and cost.
- 后训练用来对齐真实用户、让常见任务做得更稳；节目还谈到主动提醒与「产品宪法」、生态和长期目标等暂时做不到的事，以及云电脑的成本取舍和未来半年的模型升级。
  > [en] Post-training is used to align with real users and make common tasks more reliable; the conversation also covers proactive reminders and a “product constitution,” what is still out of reach (ecosystem, long-term goals, companionship), cost trade-offs of cloud computers, and model upgrades over the next six months.
