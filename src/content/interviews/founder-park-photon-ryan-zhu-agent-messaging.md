## 要点

- 把 Agent 接进 iMessage、WhatsApp 和电话，要兼容少有文档的协议、自建机房、对接运营商。Ryan 估计 Agent 公司自建至少要六到八个月，连 OpenAI 这样的公司也用第三方方案；Photon 今年 4 月开始商业化，只卖标准化 API，一条 Business 线路每月 250 美元、约覆盖 200 到 400 个用户，收入主要来自企业客户，开发者贡献不到一成。
  > [en] Wiring agents into iMessage, WhatsApp and phone calls means handling sparsely documented protocols, running your own data centers and working with carriers. Ryan estimates an agent company needs at least six to eight months to build this itself, and says even companies like OpenAI use a third party. Photon began charging in April with only a standardized API; a Business line costs $250 a month and covers about 200 to 400 users, with revenue mostly from enterprises and under 10% from individual developers.
- Personal Agent 是平台上增长最快的一类：四五月 Hermes 等带来第一波需求，9 月 Instinct 等一批产品集中出现后再次加速，现在已占 Photon 客户的四到五成。
  > [en] Personal agents are the fastest-growing category on the platform: Hermes and others drove the first wave in April and May, Instinct and a batch of others accelerated it again in September, and they now make up 40–50% of Photon's customers.
- 从消息管道看到的问题：多数 Personal Agent 留存不高，用户试一两周就不用了；工作日用得多、周日最少，大家主要拿它办事；所谓「主动性」多半只是收到邮件就发条通知，像「一个 notification 机器人」，真正的主动应是在后台过滤掉大部分冗余信息、只把关键部分交给用户。
  > [en] What the pipe reveals: most personal agents retain poorly, with users dropping off after a week or two; usage peaks on weekdays and bottoms out on Sundays because people mainly use them for errands; and most "proactivity" is just a message whenever an email arrives, "a notification bot." Real proactivity, he says, means filtering out most redundant information in the background and surfacing only what matters.
- 用户在分化：在一个 Agent 那里流失的人，换到另一个可能成为重度用户。健康类、拓展生意和人脉的 Agent 受欢迎，替人社交、打发时间的表现一般；留存最好的反而是「AI 男友」「AI 女友」类陪伴产品。他认为 IM 仍会是 Personal Agent 最主要的入口之一，App 主要承接看 Canvas 等复杂界面的重度用户。
  > [en] Users are splitting: someone who churns from one agent may become a power user of another. Health agents and ones that grow your business or network do well, while agents that socialize for you or kill time do not; the best-retaining category is "AI boyfriend/girlfriend" companions. He still sees IM as one of the main entry points for personal agents, with apps serving power users who need complex views like a canvas.
- 下一步不再横向接更多渠道（iMessage、WhatsApp、Slack、电话已覆盖大部分场景），而是在约半年内纵向做四件事：利用端到端加密识别发消息设备的身份认证、把工具调用日志与用户在消息端的真实行为结合的分析、对话内原生支付，以及以 Agent 的手机号和邮箱为底座的记忆。他认为优势主要来自生态位置而非技术。
  > [en] Rather than adding channels (iMessage, WhatsApp, Slack and voice cover most cases), Photon plans to build four layers within about six months: identity that uses end-to-end encryption to recognize which device sent a message, analytics that join tool-call logs with real user behavior on the messaging side, native in-conversation payments, and memory anchored on the agent's phone number and email. He says the edge comes mainly from ecosystem position, not technology.
