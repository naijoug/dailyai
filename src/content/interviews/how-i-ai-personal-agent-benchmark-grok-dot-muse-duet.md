- Four personal agents (Grok Bot, OpenAI's Dot, Meta Muse and Anthropic's Claude Duet) are tested on the same nine real tasks, with a scoreboard and final scores.
  > [zh] 四个个人 Agent（Grok Bot、OpenAI 的 Dot、Meta Muse、Anthropic 的 Claude Duet）完成同样九个真实任务，给出计分板和最终得分。
