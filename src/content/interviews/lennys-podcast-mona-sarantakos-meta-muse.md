## 要点

- Mona Sarantakos is VP of Product at Meta Superintelligence Labs and has led product on Muse since day one; the show notes call Muse the #1 app in the App Store and “arguably the fastest-growing AI consumer product of all time.” This is her first podcast interview.
  > [zh] Mona Sarantakos 是 Meta 超级智能实验室产品副总裁，从第一天起负责 Muse 的产品；节目简介称 Muse 是 App Store 第一的应用，「可以说是史上增长最快的消费级 AI 产品」。这是她第一次上播客。
- The origin story: how a weekend running OpenClaw on a Raspberry Pi turned into Muse, and why the team waited seven months to launch even though it started before most competitors.
  > [zh] 起源故事：一个周末在树莓派上跑 OpenClaw，如何演变成 Muse；以及团队明明比多数竞争对手更早起步，为什么还是等了七个月才发布。
- How Nat Friedman, Alex Wang and Mark Zuckerberg each left their imprint on the product, and how the placeholder avatar Jolly became an icon.
  > [zh] Nat Friedman、Alex Wang 和扎克伯格各自在产品上留下了什么印记，以及占位头像 Jolly 如何变成了标志。
- How the team uses “agent swarms” to surface user insights at scale, the use case nobody on the team predicted, and the two levers that actually move activation for an AI agent.
  > [zh] 团队如何用「Agent 集群」大规模挖掘用户洞察，一个团队谁都没料到的使用场景，以及真正能推动 AI Agent 激活的两个杠杆。
- Her view on the number-one thing she now looks for in PMs.
  > [zh] 她如今招产品经理时最看重的一点。
