## 要点

- 录制当天是 Fable 5.2 发布次日。第一个 Aha Moment 是 RSI（递归自我改进）：用 Kimi 加速 Kimi、用模型优化模型，具体落在 Kernel 优化和推理加速上，并谈到 NVIDIA B300 和 OpenRSI。
  > [en] It was recorded the day after Fable 5.2 launched. His first aha moment is RSI (recursive self-improvement): using Kimi to speed up Kimi and models to optimize models, concretely in kernel optimization and inference acceleration, with mentions of NVIDIA B300 and OpenRSI.
- 评测是关键：能被评估才能被优化。这一段的简介关键词包括 SGLang-Omni、「80M 语音模型」和「一个周末性能提升 1700%」；他也谈到 Benchmark 在客观性、自动化、低成本与主观审美之间的取舍，以及指标偏科。
  > [en] Evaluation is the key: what can be evaluated can be optimized. Keywords in the show notes for this segment include SGLang-Omni, “an 80M speech model” and “a 1700% performance gain in one weekend”; he also discusses the trade-offs benchmarks make between objectivity, automation, low cost and subjective taste, and lopsided metrics.
- 开源项目管理：在 Vibe Coding 和廉价智能时代，BDFL 式治理、Code Review 和一致的代码风格标准为什么更重要。
  > [en] Open-source project management: why BDFL-style governance, code review and consistent style standards matter more in the era of vibe coding and cheap intelligence.
- Computer Use 与交互式 AI：Agent 评测、KV Cache 与 Prefix Sharing、Phone Use；原生全双工与 VAD → ASR → LLM → TTS 级联方案的对比，以及「TTS as a Tool」。
  > [en] Computer use and interactive AI: agent evaluation, KV cache and prefix sharing, phone use; native full-duplex versus the cascaded VAD → ASR → LLM → TTS pipeline, and “TTS as a Tool”.
- 语音输入法 Voxt 让他每天输出约 4 万字（ASR + LLM、本地推理、MLX）；最后用《大明王朝1566》《万历十五年》等历史读物谈权责对等与开源项目中的权力和责任。
  > [en] The Voxt voice input method lets him produce about 40,000 characters a day (ASR + LLM, local inference, MLX); he closes by using histories such as The Ming Dynasty 1566 and 1587, a Year of No Significance to talk about matching power with responsibility, including in open-source projects.
