## 要点

- Tenev frames Robinhood’s mission as expanding Ownership: from zero-commission stocks to tokenization of privates, Robinhood Chain, Trump accounts, prediction markets, and AI-era access to private companies.
  > [zh] Tenev 把 Robinhood 的使命概括为扩大「所有权」：从零佣金炒股，延伸到私募代币化、Robinhood Chain、Trump 账户、预测市场，以及 AI 时代普通人接触私有公司的通道。
- Agentic Trading / Robinhood Agents let customers run AI agents that research and trade in a dedicated Agentic account (also via MCP for external agents); chapters highlight impacts of agentic trading and an agentic credit card.
  > [zh] Agentic Trading / Robinhood Agents 让用户在独立 Agentic 账户里用 AI Agent 做研究与下单（也可经 MCP 接外部 Agent）；章节还谈到 Agent 交易影响与 Agent 信用卡。
- Harmonic, Tenev’s mathematical superintelligence startup, aims to decentralize new discoveries and inventions through formally verified reasoning (Aristotle for code/hardware proofs).
  > [zh] Tenev 创办的数学超级智能公司 Harmonic，想用形式化可验证推理（Aristotle 可证明软硬件正确性）去中心化新发现与发明。
- Also covers prediction markets as monetizing knowledge, safeguarding financial infrastructure for higher HFT volumes, and leadership under market volatility.
  > [zh] 还谈到用预测市场把知识货币化、为更高频交易量守住金融基础设施，以及在市场波动中保持冷静的领导力。
