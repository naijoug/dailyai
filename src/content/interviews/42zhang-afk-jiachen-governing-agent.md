## 要点

- 曲凯对谈 Afk.surf 创始人贺嘉琛：他把 Personal Agent 用到 2B 领域，并给这些企业训练私有模型，做法接近节目前几期聊过的 Personal Agent 与 RLaaS 的结合。
  > [en] Qu Kai talks with Afk.surf founder He Jiachen, who applies personal agents to B2B and trains private models for those companies—a mix of the show's earlier threads on personal agents and RLaaS.
- 嘉琛原本研究深度学习与天体物理学，2020 年从博士阶段退学创业，创办开源文档协作与知识库产品 AFFiNE，一度做到 700 万用户、Product Hunt 年度第四；后来又与尤雨溪等人一起孵化开发者工具公司 VoidZero，被 Cloudflare 收购。
  > [en] He previously researched deep learning and astrophysics, left his PhD in 2020, founded the open-source docs and knowledge-base product AFFiNE (7 million users, fourth in a Product Hunt year-end list), and later helped incubate developer-tools company VoidZero with Evan You and others, which Cloudflare acquired.
- 他现在做的 To B Governing Agent 产品 Afk.surf，希望企业能真正接管并持续学习自己运行的各个环节，在服务不同员工的同时，对齐整个组织的业务、工作方式和使用偏好；和许多仍在讲故事、还没看到明确正反馈的团队不同，他们已经有了几个大客户。
  > [en] His current to-B governing agent, Afk.surf, aims to let a company take over and continually learn its own operations, serving different employees while aligning with the organization's business, ways of working, and preferences; unlike many teams still telling the story without clear feedback, they already have several large customers.
- 时间轴还复盘了 2026 年起 Agent 不再像软件、近两年产品形态剧烈变化后收敛到的新趋势，以及 Personal Agent 的发展脉络；并讨论 To C 是否仍是平台型机会而 To B 不是、Afk 如何拿到大客户、企业协作该有的共识，以及 Langchain / Dify / n8n 是否过时、应用公司为何自己训模型、AI 会否端到端消灭过去的各种逻辑、模型两三年内是否普惠，还有 AI 游戏的 timing。
  > [en] The chapter list also recaps how agents stopped resembling software in 2026, a violent shift in product form that has recently converged, and the trajectory of personal agents; they discuss whether consumer personal agents are still a platform opportunity while to-B is not, how Afk won large customers, shared assumptions for enterprise collaboration, whether LangChain, Dify and n8n are outdated, why an application company trains its own models, whether AI may end-to-end erase prior logic, whether models become widely accessible within two or three years, and whether the timing for AI games has arrived.
- 嘉琛也被认为擅长组织人才：AFFiNE 前产品负责人后来做的 AirBuild 在 X 上小火了一把，前 CTO 如今在 Cloudflare 担任 Principal Engineer，还有做出海的朋友都熟悉的生姜，有「聚是一团火，散是满天星」的感觉；节目最后也聊了招人经验。
  > [en] He is also described as strong at assembling talent: AFFiNE's former head of product later made AirBuild, which got a burst of attention on X, the former CTO is now a principal engineer at Cloudflare, and there is also Jiang, well known among going-global founders—scattered like stars after burning together as a fire. The episode ends on how he recruits people like that.
