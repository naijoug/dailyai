## 要点

- Luttwak calls the recent wave of vulnerabilities and agentic attacks "Black October" and sees it as a turning point: AI is not only helping defenders find bugs but giving attackers the speed and expertise to exploit them faster than many companies can respond.
  > [zh] Luttwak 把近期这波漏洞和 Agent 攻击称为「黑色十月」，认为这是安全行业的转折点：AI 不只帮防守方找漏洞，也让攻击者有了足够的速度和专业能力，利用漏洞的速度快过许多公司的响应速度。
- The conversation breaks down PHP2Shell and why AI is collapsing the time between vulnerability discovery and exploitation, and what happens as open-source models put increasingly sophisticated offensive capabilities in more hands.
  > [zh] 节目拆解了 PHP2Shell，讨论 AI 为何把从发现漏洞到被利用的时间压缩到极短，以及开源模型让越来越强的攻击能力落到更多人手里之后会怎样。
- He argues startups are becoming attractive targets, especially when they hold valuable AI credentials, and that yesterday's secure architecture may no longer be secure as models improve.
  > [zh] 他认为初创公司正成为诱人的目标，尤其是手里握有高价值 AI 凭据的公司；而且随着模型变强，过去被认为安全的架构可能已经不再安全。
- On defense, companies should continuously attack their own systems, automate investigation and patching wherever possible, design infrastructure assuming sophisticated attackers will get in, and rethink security stacks built for a slower era; Wiz's blog post "The Rise of Agentic Attacks" is linked as companion reading.
  > [zh] 防守上，企业应持续攻击自己的系统，尽可能把调查和打补丁自动化，按「高水平攻击者一定会进来」来设计基础设施，并重新审视为更慢时代打造的安全体系；节目附上 Wiz 博客文章《The Rise of Agentic Attacks》作为延伸阅读。
