## 要点

- 90% 和 100% 的委托差别很大：她的主 agent「AI 余一」跑通链路后把经验和代码交给监控 bot，她只跟这一个 agent 说话，它是幕僚长而不是干活的。
  > [en] Delegating 90% versus 100% is a huge gap: her lead agent hands working code and know-how to monitoring bots, and she only talks to that one agent, which acts as chief of staff rather than a worker.
- 在 Manus，人人都是 AI 重度用户：反馈进 Slack 后由 AI 定级、找到负责人、翻出历史记录；入职时先和内部机器人聊。
  > [en] At Manus everyone is a heavy AI user: feedback in Slack is triaged by AI, routed to an owner and linked to past reports; new hires first chat with an internal bot.
- 用 Manus 2.0 一句提示词就能出好视频，她打算把攒了一年的 harness 全删掉重跑，让 AI 自证哪些该留。
  > [en] With Manus 2.0 one prompt produced a strong video, so she plans to delete a year's worth of harness and make the AI prove which parts are worth keeping.
- 工程交给 AI 后，人类的活是打磨那一句定义好坏和验收标准的提示词。
  > [en] Once AI handles the engineering, the human job is honing the one prompt that defines what good looks like and how to accept the work.
- 招实习生先看作品，再给候选人 Manus 积分，用一两天和 AI 一起做出东西。
  > [en] For interns, portfolios come first, then candidates get Manus credits to build something with AI in a day or two.
