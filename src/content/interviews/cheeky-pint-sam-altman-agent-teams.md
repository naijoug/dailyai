## 要点

- Models keep getting better at finding vulnerabilities and people have only started pointing them at all public code; Altman expects many more serious bugs than even experts anticipate.
  > [zh] 模型找漏洞的能力还在快速提升，而人们才刚开始拿它扫遍公开代码；Altman 认为会发现比专家预期还多的严重漏洞。
- He is pessimistic that a sandbox can contain a model far smarter than any security researcher, so a better security model and trust in alignment are the priority.
  > [zh] 他不太相信沙箱能关住远比任何安全研究员聪明的模型，当务之急是更好的安全模型和对对齐的信任。
- Chain-of-thought and test-time compute let you throw whole agent teams at one problem thousands of times in parallel; OpenAI's Navier-Stokes proof used agent teams, and the gap between power users and typical corporate use keeps widening.
  > [zh] 思维链和测试时算力让你能把整支 Agent 团队成千上万次并行地砸在一个问题上；OpenAI 的 Navier-Stokes 证明就用了 Agent 团队，重度用户和普通企业用法的差距持续拉大。
- He argues swarms mirror the robustness of a pluralistic society, which is why he prefers many agents keeping each other in check over a single-brained superintelligence.
  > [zh] 他认为 Agent 群体像多元社会一样稳健，因此更倾向许多 Agent 相互制衡，而不是单一大脑的超级智能。
- Personal agents are "a small number of ideas away" from mainstream; with agentic commerce inside ChatGPT, a business's moat is brand and fulfillment rather than its HTML or app. Also: Codex going down during an internal incident, and why customers prefer token pricing.
  > [zh] 个人 Agent 离主流只差少数几个想法；当购物在 ChatGPT 里由 Agent 完成，企业的护城河是品牌和履约，而不是网页或 App。另谈到内部故障时 Codex 宕机，以及客户为何偏好按 token 计价。

## 金句

- 「I hate the word 'agent swarms'. Let's call them 'agent teams'.」
  > [zh] “我讨厌‘Agent 蜂群’这个词，叫它们‘Agent 团队’吧。”
- 「I suspect that teams of agents have very interesting scaling law properties, but also variance in alignment properties.」
  > [zh] “我猜 Agent 团队有非常有趣的规模定律特性，但对齐特性上也会有差异。”
- 「I think this is a small number of ideas away from a super mainstream thing.」
  > [zh] “我觉得（个人 Agent）离真正的超级主流只差少数几个想法。”
