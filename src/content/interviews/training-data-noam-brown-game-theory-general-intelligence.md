## 要点

- Brown built the first AIs to beat top poker professionals, then had a “crisis of faith”: game theory assumes everyone is rational, but the Diplomacy bots that won were the ones trained on human data to model how people actually behave in the messy real world.
  > [zh] Brown 做出了第一批击败顶级职业扑克选手的 AI，随后经历了一场「信仰危机」：博弈论假设所有人都理性，但在《外交》游戏里获胜的机器人，恰恰是用人类数据训练、能模拟人们在混乱现实中真实行为的那些。
- That pushed him to OpenAI and a bet on reasoning, models that think before acting, at a time when much of the field believed a bigger pre-trained model would solve everything; the work became o1. The episode also asks whether chain of thought is the right way to scale test-time compute.
  > [zh] 这让他加入 OpenAI，押注「推理」，也就是先思考再行动的模型；当时很多人相信只要预训练模型更大就能解决一切。这项工作后来成为 o1。节目还讨论了思维链是不是扩展测试时计算的正确方式。
- He asks every new model to build a pokerbot as a personal test, and discusses where GPT-6 Astra still falls short and why, in audience Q&A, models still can’t build a great pokerbot on their own.
  > [zh] 他会让每个新模型写一个扑克机器人作为自己的测试，并谈到 GPT-6 Astra 仍有哪些不足；在观众问答中，他解释了为什么模型目前还不能独立做出优秀的扑克机器人。
- He calls the present the “centaur period” of human plus AI, argues that human progress has always been one giant recursive self-improvement loop, and gives his most controversial take: promising students should drop out and join the labs now.
  > [zh] 他把当下称为人加 AI 的「半人马时期」，认为人类进步一直就是一个巨大的递归自我改进循环，并给出他最有争议的观点：有潜力的学生现在就该退学加入实验室。
