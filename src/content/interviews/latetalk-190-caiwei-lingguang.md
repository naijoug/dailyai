## 要点

- 灵光 1.0 于 2025 年 11 月 18 日发布，2.0 在 10 月 27 日全量上线，定位转为 Personal Agent。蔡伟回应「什么火做什么」的质疑：从 1.0 起主线就是 To C、提升个人在工作和生活中的效率，Claude Code 与 OpenClaw 带来了新启发，而「主动式」是关键——真正的助理会持续跟进你关心的事。
  > [en] LingGuang 1.0 launched on November 18, 2025, and 2.0 rolled out fully on October 27 as a personal agent. Responding to the charge of chasing trends, Cai Wei says the through-line since 1.0 has been consumer productivity in work and life; Claude Code and OpenClaw brought new inspiration, and proactivity is the key—a real assistant keeps following up on what you care about.
- 节目复盘了半年开发：春节到 4 月先判断做独立产品还是 feature、模型能力是否到位，开发中踩过框架的弯路，要定义什么是「好」体验并守住安全与隐私底线；他把做好个人 Agent 归结为智能、服务供给、安全可信三要素，还讨论了常驻 Agent 用云主机方案的实际成本。
  > [en] The episode reviews six months of development: from Spring Festival to April the team weighed standalone product versus feature and whether models were ready, then took detours on frameworks, had to define what a "good" experience is, and held a floor on safety and privacy. He boils a good personal agent down to intelligence, service supply and trustworthiness, and discusses the real cost of always-on agents running on cloud VMs.
- 产品表达上，灵光把「事项」而不是对话作为基本单位；发布后留存超出预期，但被吐槽「打不透部门墙」，蔡伟认为背后是服务供给问题。用户也从一二线白领扩展到更多元的人群。
  > [en] As a product, LingGuang makes tasks rather than conversations the basic unit. Retention since launch has beaten expectations, but users complain it "can't break through departmental walls," which Cai attributes to limited service supply. Its users are also spreading beyond white-collar workers in top-tier cities.
- 他更相信个人 Agent 会成为国民级应用，但从 Instinct 和 Muse 得到的启发是：个人 Agent 不会只有一个入口，所有前端应用和超级 App 被一个统一入口颠覆是小概率事件，也不是他设想中 AI 与人最好的相处方式。
  > [en] He believes personal agents will become national-scale apps, but takes from Instinct and Muse that there won't be just one entry point; a single unified front end displacing every app and super-app is a low-probability outcome, and not how he thinks AI and people should coexist.
- 壁垒在上下文、迁移成本与服务生态，他还谈到先发窗口期有多长、豆包手机代表的另一类个人 Agent，以及下一个里程碑：托管事项数再上一个数量级，让 AI 从「帮你盯」走向「帮你管」、承担更多责任。
  > [en] The moats are context, switching costs and the service ecosystem. He also discusses how long the first-mover window lasts, the Doubao phone as a different kind of personal agent, and the next milestone: another order of magnitude in tasks under management, moving AI from watching things for you to managing them and taking on more responsibility.
