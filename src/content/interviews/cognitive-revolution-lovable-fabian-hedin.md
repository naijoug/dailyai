## 要点

- Scale by Lovable’s own numbers: over 70 million projects, 1.2 million new ones a week, more than a billion monthly visits to apps built on it, about 400 full-time staff, around 800,000 paying customers and a latest public ARR of $600 million (the show notes disclose Lovable is a current sponsor).
  > [zh] 按 Lovable 自己的口径：平台上已建超过 7000 万个项目，每周新增 120 万个，这些应用每月访问量超过 10 亿；公司约 400 名全职员工、约 80 万付费客户，最新公开 ARR 为 6 亿美元（节目说明中披露 Lovable 是当前赞助商）。
- His core thesis is cost: building software is now about 100x faster and cheaper, so long-tail niche apps get built; a contractor prototype that once took weeks and thousands of dollars now takes a day and twenty dollars, and eight out of ten users arrive intending to create value for others or make money.
  > [zh] 他的核心观点是成本：做软件快了、便宜了约 100 倍，于是大量长尾小众应用被做出来；过去外包做原型要几周、几千美元，现在一天、二十美元就够；十个用户里有八个带着为别人创造价值或赚钱的目的而来。
- Security is the longest section: Lovable scans every project for free, keeps hosting up to date and bumps vulnerable dependencies automatically, moving from “email you to click Fix” to fixes users never touch, made reliable by the unit tests and browser checks it writes while building; its scanners formally verify whether database row-level security rules leave any data reachable and feed results back to the agent until the data is provably inaccessible.
  > [zh] 安全是最长的一段：Lovable 免费扫描每个项目、保持托管设施更新、自动升级有漏洞的依赖，正从「发邮件让你点修复」走向用户无感的自动修复，靠的是构建时顺带写好的单元测试和浏览器检查；扫描器还会形式化验证数据库行级安全规则是否留有可访问的数据，并把结果反馈给 Agent，直到证明数据不可访问。
- No model picker by design: Lovable A/B tests models in production and with LLM judges offline, sends complex work to frontier models and simple edits like recoloring a button to fine-tuned open-source models, and he insists routing only makes things faster and cheaper, never worse.
  > [zh] 刻意不给模型选择器：Lovable 在线上做 A/B 测试、离线用 LLM 评审评估模型，复杂任务交给前沿模型，像改按钮颜色这样的简单修改交给微调过的开源模型；他强调路由只会让响应更快更便宜，不会降低质量。
- Beyond building, Lovable is adding SEO and ad tools and starting to let its agent draft replies to customers in WhatsApp and Instagram DMs until owners trust it to handle them; new projects default to TanStack Start for server-side rendering so Google and AI crawlers can index them, and he argues SaaS survives the “SaaSpocalypse” by serving agents rather than humans.
  > [zh] 除了搭应用，Lovable 还在加入 SEO 和广告工具，并开始让 Agent 在 WhatsApp 和 Instagram 私信里替店主起草客户回复，直到店主放心交给它处理；新项目默认改用支持服务端渲染的 TanStack Start，方便谷歌和 AI 爬虫收录；他认为 SaaS 要撑过「SaaS 末日」，就得转向服务 Agent 而不是人。

## 金句

- 「The major thing that has changed is that it's a hundred times faster and it's a hundred times cheaper than before to build software.」
  > [zh] 「最大的变化是，做软件比以前快了一百倍、便宜了一百倍。」
- 「We'll actually never route you in a way where we would lower the quality.」
  > [zh] 「我们的路由永远不会以降低质量为代价。」
- 「That, I think, is the critical thing for these SaaS companies to transition to, where they're not serving humans anymore, they're serving agents.」
  > [zh] 「我认为这是 SaaS 公司必须完成的关键转变：它们服务的不再是人，而是 Agent。」
