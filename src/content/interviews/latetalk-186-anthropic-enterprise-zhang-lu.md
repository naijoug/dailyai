## 要点

- 讨论 Anthropic 从模型供应商走向企业智能体平台的可能路径，这是嘉宾判断而非已确定的行业结局。
  > [en] The conversation explores Anthropic’s potential evolution from model supplier to enterprise agent platform, a guest assessment rather than a settled industry outcome.
- 张璐认为旧工作流、监管和成本可能使企业改造耗时多年，金融、法律和医疗不一定复制 coding 的增长速度。
  > [en] Zhang argues that legacy workflows, regulation and cost can make adoption take years; finance, law and healthcare may not grow like coding.
- 节目关注后台智能体和高监管行业的垂直机会，并把更明确的监管视为部分企业部署的前提。
  > [en] It examines back-office agents and regulated-industry opportunities, noting that some enterprises need clearer regulation before deploying.
