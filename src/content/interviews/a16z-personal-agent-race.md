## 要点

- David Pawlan has tested dozens of assistants on real tasks — email, travel booking, financial admin.
  > [zh] David Pawlan 在邮件、订行程、财务杂务等真实任务上测试了数十款助理。
- The most useful agents may become invisible: checking you into flights, finding refunds, filing reimbursements.
  > [zh] 最有用的 Agent 可能变得“隐形”：帮你值机、找退款、报销。
- Open questions: app vs text thread vs voice vs wearable, how much autonomy consumers will grant, and what happens when agents transact with agents.
  > [zh] 待解问题：App、短信线程、语音还是可穿戴？消费者愿放多少自主权？Agent 与 Agent 交易时会怎样？
