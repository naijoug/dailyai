## 要点

- According to the show notes, he moved home life out of AI chats, which drift and forget, into Claude Code working on a folder of Markdown files that he views in Obsidian.
  > [zh] 据节目说明，他受够了 AI 聊天会跑偏、会忘事，把家务管理搬进 Claude Code，让它维护一个 Markdown 文件夹，自己用 Obsidian 查看。
- The demos cover shopping for car insurance with it (the notes say AI made him "a sharper insurance shopper") and planning repairs, plus what Claude told him to photograph first when building the knowledge base.
  > [zh] 节目演示用它比价买车险（节目说明称 AI 让他成了「更精明的保险买家」）和安排维修，以及搭知识库时 Claude 建议他先拍哪些东西。
- He shares one household brain with his wife through a shared to-do file, and puts priority tasks and a "recent wins" section on a DAKboard wall display.
  > [zh] 他和妻子共用这个「家庭大脑」和一份共享待办文件，并把优先任务和「最近完成」栏目放到 DAKboard 墙面显示屏上。

  > [zh] 节目还聊到哪些家务适合交给 AI、系统数据不同步时怎么办，以及让学生反过来教 AI 的「翻转家教」聊天机器人，好让孩子保持自己思考。
