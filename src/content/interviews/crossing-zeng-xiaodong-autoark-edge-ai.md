## 要点

- 曾晓东认为把端侧模型当作云端模型的「缩水版」「妥协产物」的认知可能全错了；节目介绍，这家 50 人团队把千亿大模型塞进普通笔记本，跑出超过 2 亿元的 ARR。
  > [en] Zeng argues the view of on-device models as "shrunken" or "compromise" versions of cloud models may be entirely wrong; the episode notes the 50-person team fit a 100B-plus model onto ordinary laptops and reached over ¥200M in ARR.
- 商业现实：AI 硬件是一次性销售，云端 Token 账单却每月持续产生，所以硬件卖得越多反而可能亏得越多，这是端侧模型的商业动因。
  > [en] The business reality: AI hardware is a one-time sale while cloud token bills recur every month, so selling more hardware can mean losing more money, which is the commercial case for on-device models.
- 技术拆解近期开源、能在 30G 内存消费级电脑上流畅运行的千亿级原生端侧模型 Edge0-145B；他强调端侧原生的本质是模型架构与推理引擎必须推倒重来、联合设计。
  > [en] A technical breakdown of Edge0-145B, the recently open-sourced 100B-plus on-device-native model that runs smoothly on consumer computers with 30 GB of memory; he stresses that being on-device native means redesigning model architecture and inference engine together from scratch.
- 组织上，50 人的 AI Native 团队支撑 200 多种模型部署，经验是把模型公司当成代码流水线来跑。
  > [en] Organizationally, a 50-person AI-native team supports deployments of 200-plus models, and the lesson is to run a model company like a code pipeline.
