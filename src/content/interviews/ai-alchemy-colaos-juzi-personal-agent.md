## 要点

- 模型降价打开窗口期：半年前做 agent 要用 Opus 级模型，如今 flash 级模型就能做好看邮件、订机票酒店、操作软件，token 成本只有当时的几十分之一。
  > [en] Cheaper models open a window: six months ago agents needed Opus-class models; now flash-class models handle email, bookings and software operation at a few percent of the cost.
- 橘子的单用户账：补贴用户 token 的成本已低于获客成本，是一个质变节点。
  > [en] Juzi’s per-user math: subsidizing users’ tokens now costs less than acquiring them — a qualitative turning point.
- 放弃卷办公，转向工作之外的个人 agent，帮用户通过副业、自媒体找到“第二资产曲线”。
  > [en] Leaving the crowded office space to build a personal agent for life outside work, helping users find a ‘second income curve’ via side gigs and content.
- 踩坑：99% 的人没有日历，大部分人也没有“支使别人干活”的肌肉。
  > [en] Lessons: 99% of people don’t use a calendar, and most lack the habit of delegating work.
