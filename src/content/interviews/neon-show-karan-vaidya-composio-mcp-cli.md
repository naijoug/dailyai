## 要点

- Vaidya and Soham Ganatra started Composio in 2023 trying to build a “Devin for integrations”; it was too early because the models were not good enough, but the work showed every agent still needs to reach Slack, Gmail, Salesforce and the rest of the work stack.
  > [zh] Vaidya 与 Soham Ganatra 在 2023 年起步时，想做的是「集成版 Devin」；当时模型还不够好，项目本身太早，但他们由此看到：每个 Agent 仍然必须接到 Slack、Gmail、Salesforce 以及知识工作者日常使用的其余工具栈。
- Today Composio connects agents to more than 1,000 apps, and agents themselves build and repair most of those integrations (show notes also point to a chapter on 50,000 tools). An early Composio benchmark showed accuracy jumping from 20% to 80% just by improving the tools an agent uses.
  > [zh] 如今 Composio 让 Agent 接到 1,000 多个应用，而且其中大多数集成是由 Agent 自己搭建和修复的（章节里还提到 5 万个工具）。早期基准显示，只改进 Agent 所用的工具，准确率就能从 20% 升到 80%。
- The company claims more than 100K developers use it every day, about 20 million tool calls a day, customers including AWS, Zoom and Glean building on top, 7x revenue this year, and a chase toward $20M ARR, with double-digit Fortune 500 logos after restarting enterprise monetization.
  > [zh] 节目称每日活跃开发者超过 10 万、每天约 2,000 万次工具调用，AWS、Zoom、Glean 等在其之上做产品；今年收入增至 7 倍，并在追 $2,000 万 ARR，重启企业变现后已有两位数的 Fortune 500 客户。
- He argues a smarter model is only half the answer: as companies mix models from different labs, they need to own logins, context, and the skills agents learn over time, and many enterprises will want to run their own models. After coding agents, knowledge work is “20–50x bigger.” There is also a chapter on how he would fix MCP, and an opening segment “Did CLI Kill MCP?”
  > [zh] 他认为更强的模型只是答案的一半：企业会混用不同实验室的模型，真正要掌握的是登录、上下文，以及 Agent 随时间学到的 skills，而且不少企业会选择自托管模型。编码 Agent 之后，知识工作的市场「大 20–50 倍」。节目还有「他会如何改进 MCP」以及开篇的 “Did CLI Kill MCP?”。

  > [zh] 「如今 Composio 让 AI Agent 连接到 1,000 多个应用，而且这些集成大多由 Agent 自己搭建和修复。」
