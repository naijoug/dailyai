## 要点

- Cosign is building a professional network around who is willing to vouch for you, aiming to surface relationship and reputation signals that traditional networks leave locked away—and eventually make that graph usable by AI agents as well as humans.
  > [zh] Cosign 正围绕「谁愿意为你背书」搭建职业网络，目标是把传统网络锁住的关系与声誉信号显出来，并最终让人和 AI Agent 都能用上这张图。
- David Booth and Dhruv Gupta introduce the Cosign Awards, which recognize operators, companies and products based on nominations from people who know the work firsthand.
  > [zh] David Booth 与 Dhruv Gupta 介绍了 Cosign Awards：凭熟悉当事人工作的人的提名，表彰出色的运营者、公司与产品。
- Looking ahead, they sketch professional networking where your personal agent can talk to a company's agent, check for a coincidence of intent, and only surface the opportunity when there is a real fit.
  > [zh] 他们设想未来的职业社交：你的个人 Agent 先与公司侧 Agent 对话，判断是否存在「意图巧合」，只有真正匹配时才把机会推给你。
