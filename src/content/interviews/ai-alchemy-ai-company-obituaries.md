## 要点

- 渠道变成竞品：Chegg 的流量原本来自 Google，现在题目输进去 AI 直接答了；Tailwind 用的人更多了，但 AI 去翻文档时会略过与主业无关的内容，附加服务就没人买了。
  > [en] Channels turn into competitors: Chegg’s traffic came from Google, and now AI answers the question directly; Tailwind has more users than ever, but AI reads the docs and skips everything unrelated to the task, so nobody buys the add-ons.
- SaaS 财报还在涨、股价先死，「一篇博客可以让你的公司跌个几十一百亿美金」；拥抱 AI 是必须的，但对手最多落后三到六个月，大家都降本增效三倍，整个生意盘子会缩到原来的三分之一。
  > [en] SaaS revenue is still rising while the stocks die first—“one blog post can knock tens of billions of dollars off your company”; embracing AI is mandatory, but rivals are at most three to six months behind, and if everyone gets three times more efficient the whole market shrinks to a third.
- 徐文浩认为 Linear 的上涨也是「虚假繁荣」：效率最高的团队一周干完未来三个月的活，没法提前排计划、拆 ticket、开站会，项目管理这个产品形态本身可能消失。
  > [en] Xu Wenhao calls Linear’s rise a “false boom” too: the most efficient teams do three months of work in a week, leaving no room to plan ahead, split tickets or hold stand-ups, so project management as a product category may disappear.
- Neo Cloud 像 2000 年铺光纤，一定会死一批；Coding 赛道里 Cursor、Augment、Devin、Windsurf、Lovable 等走向各异；从模型公司买 token 再卖给用户的 Agent 公司拿到的是「二手贵的 token」，像当年按零售价买车再原价卖出的 carorder.com。
  > [en] Neoclouds are like laying fiber in 2000 and a batch of them will die; in coding, Cursor, Augment, Devin, Windsurf, Lovable and others met very different fates; agent startups that buy tokens from model companies and resell them are getting “second-hand, expensive tokens”, like carorder.com buying cars at retail and selling them at the same price.
- Agent 接管入口后，携程、Airbnb 还剩什么仍有分歧：Airbnb 创始人认为「逛」本身是旅行乐趣的一部分，任鑫则认为足够了解你的 Agent 也能给出更好的逛法；给个人的建议是加入头部那家有泡沫的公司，给公司的建议是留一半钱别花完。
  > [en] They disagree on what is left for Ctrip and Airbnb once agents own the front door: Airbnb’s founder says browsing is part of the joy of travel, while Ren Xin thinks an agent that knows you well could browse better for you; their advice is for individuals to join the leading, bubbly company and for companies to keep half their cash in reserve.
