## 要点

- 系列不做大而全的新闻盘点，而是请一线创业者、研究员和投资人各讲 10 个真正触动他们的 AI 瞬间，本期嘉宾是「屠龙之术」主播庄明浩。
  > [en] Instead of a comprehensive news recap, the series asks frontline founders, researchers and investors for the 10 AI moments that truly moved them; this episode's guest is Zhuang Minghao of the Tulong Zhishu podcast.
- 产品与模型线：OpenClaw 刷屏后留下了什么、Seedance 2.0 让字节赢的是否只是模型、Sora 2 关闭后谁在受益、Codex 与 ChatGPT 合并该如何理解。
  > [en] Products and models: what OpenClaw left behind after going viral, whether ByteDance won only on the model with Seedance 2.0, who benefits after Sora 2 shut down, and how to read the Codex–ChatGPT merger.
- 一篇 Anthropic 关于 RSI（递归自我改进）的文章让他「毛骨悚然」；他还谈到模型竞争的「斩杀线」、Qwen 小模型把能力下放端侧带来的旧机会重做，以及 App Store 收入变化对软件护城河的冲击。
  > [en] An Anthropic piece on RSI (recursive self-improvement) left him unsettled; he also covers the "kill line" in model competition, Qwen small models pushing capability on-device and reopening old opportunities, and what App Store revenue shifts mean for software moats.
- 收尾时他谈到 Opus 5.5 让他只想说「毁灭吧」，以及在 AI 天天造奇观之后，反而越来越想要一点粗糙的人味。
  > [en] He closes on why Opus 5.5 made him want to say "just destroy it all," and why, after a year of daily AI spectacle, he increasingly wants a bit of rough human texture.
