## 要点

- 曲凯与 Ambi 创始人莫子皓（前 Plaud 合伙人兼中国区 CEO）聊几个月做 Personal Agent 软硬件产品的实践：成本到底多高、人们有多大需求。
  > [en] Qu Kai talks with Ambi founder Mo Zihao (ex-Plaud partner and China CEO) about months of building a hardware+software personal agent: how costly it is and how real the demand is.
- 讨论“涉及名和利的需求就可做，因为算得清 ROI”，以及 AI Coworker 为何没有想象中发展得快。
  > [en] They discuss why needs tied to fame and money are workable because ROI is measurable, and why AI coworkers have moved slower than expected.
- 简评 Town、Grok Bot、Muse、Instinct 等产品，并讨论 Ambi 如何与 Muse 这样的对手竞争、很多产品的 Proactive 是否做错了。
  > [en] Quick takes on Town, Grok Bot, Muse and Instinct, how Ambi competes with rivals like Muse, and whether many products get proactivity wrong.
- 结尾抛出一个没有答案的问题：为什么国内跑出来的 AI 产品这么少，以及现在是否已到能跑出亿万级用户产品的 timing。
  > [en] It closes on an open question: why so few Chinese AI products break out, and whether the timing for a hundred-million-user product has arrived.
