## 要点

- The framing: the revolution makes software cheaper, more reliable and easier to produce at scale (and produces a lot of slop), while the renaissance makes it affordable to build something personal and spend human time refining how it feels; both can happen in the same project, and Thariq argues the factory frees you up for the renaissance rather than replacing it.
  > [zh] 他的框架是：革命让软件更便宜、更可靠、更容易规模化生产（也产出大量垃圾），文艺复兴则让做出个人化的东西、花人力打磨手感变得负担得起；两者可以出现在同一个项目里，他认为软件工厂是腾出你去做文艺复兴，而不是取代它。
- Get the feedback loop back: much slop exists because the human never dogfoods it. In his fighting-game demo he edits the size of a rock in a `/human` folder and immediately tries the throw, and a Mac Dock that switched modes taught him he cared more about noticing when he got distracted, because a model can build something technically perfect that you never use when you never used it while building it.
  > [zh] 把反馈回路找回来：很多垃圾内容是因为人从来没自己试用过。在他的格斗游戏演示里，他在 `/human` 文件夹里改一块石头的大小并立刻试一下投掷；那个会切换模式的 Mac Dock 则让他发现自己更在意的是「察觉自己分心了」——模型能做出技术上完美、你却从不使用的东西，因为你在做的过程中就没用过它。
- On limits he says he is not bottlenecked by tokens and maybe not even by intelligence but by ambition, and by ways of working with the model, which is why he built a scripting-language layer for building the game; he was also surprised that models do front-end work the way humans do, for example by using Figma through computer use.
  > [zh] 谈到瓶颈，他说自己不受 token 限制，甚至也未必受智能限制，而是受限于雄心，以及与模型协作的方式，这也是他为做游戏搭了一层脚本语言的原因；他还惊讶于模型做前端的方式越来越像人，比如通过电脑操作去用 Figma。
- On decision models he thinks harnesses are under-utilizing them right now; the show notes describe a decision model choosing from actions a designer defines, and he also discusses personal software, which is hard because you can't tell whether an idea is good or whether you will actually use it.
  > [zh] 谈到决策模型，他认为现在的 harness 没有充分利用它们；节目简介把它描述为模型从设计者定义的动作里做选择；他还谈了个人软件：很难，因为你说不准一个点子好不好、自己会不会真的用。
- On adoption he notes model uptake is the fastest of anything, yet not much has changed in GDP or the labor market because software is hard, and on autoresearch he questions whether having a model improve itself completely headlessly is even the best option and who decides which experiments deserve the compute. Host swyx says OpenAI leaders reacted to Thariq's move by saying it is time to reinvent Codex, which he stresses is his own reading, not Thariq's.
  > [zh] 谈到采用，他指出模型的普及速度前所未有，但 GDP 和劳动力市场并没有多大变化，因为软件本来就难；谈到 autoresearch，他质疑让模型完全无人值守地自我改进是否真是最佳选择，以及谁来决定哪些实验值得占用算力。主持人 swyx 说 OpenAI 的负责人对 Thariq 加入的反应是「该重做 Codex 了」，他强调这是自己的解读，不是 Thariq 的说法。
