## 要点

- Finding almost no one outside engineering using AI for real work, Ezra and fellow PMs turned his personal Claude Code setup into PM OS, a shared repo of 31 skills that every PM at Figma now uses—kept as a repo, not a product.
  > [zh] 发现工程部门以外几乎没人用 AI 真正干活后，Ezra 和几位 PM 把他个人的 Claude Code 配置做成了 PM OS：一个含 31 个 skill 的共享仓库，如今 Figma 所有 PM 都在用——刻意做成仓库而非产品。
- The repo uses skills, hooks and private sub-repos to keep 60 to 70 PMs in sync, including a "chief of staff" setup and a skill he runs six times a day to tame Slack; skills were built, roasted and whittled down over time.
  > [zh] 仓库靠 skill、hook 和私有子仓库让 60–70 位 PM 保持同步，包括「幕僚长」式配置和他每天跑六次、用来驯服 Slack 的 skill；这些 skill 经过反复打磨和删减。
- He bug bashes with Codex—an agent that catches things he never would—and built AutoXP, an automated growth PM that runs experiments with one human checkpoint and found a big win on its very first experiment.
  > [zh] 他用 Codex 做 bug bash，Agent 能发现他自己发现不了的问题；还做了 AutoXP，一个只留一个人工检查点、自动跑实验的「增长 PM」，第一次实验就带来了大收益。
- He also moved from the terminal to a local web front end and built a real-time writing assistant with Jev that took over every meeting he was in for two weeks.
  > [zh] 他还从终端转向本地网页前端，并用 Jev 做了一个实时写作助手，有两周时间接管了他参加的每一场会议。
