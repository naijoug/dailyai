## 要点

- Josh Gruenstein argues robotics is a systems problem spanning AI, hardware, manufacturing and the work itself, not just a model problem.
  > [zh] Josh Gruenstein 认为机器人是横跨 AI、硬件、制造与工作本身的系统问题，而不只是模型问题。
- Tutor deploys robots into real businesses, brings in human "tutors" when needed, and turns that work into training data that makes the robots more capable.
  > [zh] Tutor 把机器人部署进真实企业，需要时由人类「导师」介入，再把这些工作变成训练数据，让机器人越来越能干。
- With a16z's Martin Casado and Raghu Raghuram, he discusses Tutor's 100-robot data factory, robots starting at roughly $16 an hour, advances in spatial reasoning, and what flexible robotic labor could mean for American manufacturing.
  > [zh] 他与 a16z 的 Martin Casado、Raghu Raghuram 聊到 Tutor 的 100 台机器人数据工厂、约每小时 16 美元起的机器人、空间推理的进展，以及灵活机器人劳动力对美国制造业意味着什么。
