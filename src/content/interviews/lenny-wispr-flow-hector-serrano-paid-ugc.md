## 要点

- Hector joined Wispr Flow, an AI voice-to-text tool, at 19 before product-market fit; over the past year the company grew revenue 30x and raised at a $2 billion valuation.
  > [zh] Hector 19 岁时加入 AI 语音转文字工具 Wispr Flow，当时产品尚未达到 PMF；过去一年公司营收增长 30 倍，并以 20 亿美元估值融资。
- He built a roster of 50 creators who drive 500 to 600 paid user-generated content ads every month; creators are paid a cut of ad spend and the best earn over $13,000 a month—a DTC playbook adapted for SaaS.
  > [zh] 他组建了 50 位创作者的团队，每月产出 500–600 条付费 UGC 广告；创作者按广告投放额分成，最好的月入超过 1.3 万美元——这是把 DTC 品牌的打法搬到 SaaS。
- The episode covers when to start paid ads, a step-by-step playbook for a paid-UGC group, why the best marketers are "allergic to AI" output, and why YouTube packaging matters more than production quality.
  > [zh] 节目还谈到何时开始投放付费广告、从零搭建付费 UGC 团队的步骤、为何最好的营销人对 AI 产出「过敏」，以及为何 YouTube 的包装比制作质量更重要。
