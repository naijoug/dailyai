## 要点

- Supabase, the open-source Postgres platform, now has 13 million users and spins up more than a million databases every week.
  > [zh] 开源 Postgres 平台 Supabase 目前有 1300 万用户，每周新建超过 100 万个数据库。
- Roughly 70% of Supabase's new activity now comes from agents, and the company has been rebuilding itself around that new kind of user.
  > [zh] Supabase 约 70% 的新增活动已来自 Agent，公司正围绕这类新"用户"重建自己。
- Copplestone argues personal software may become as common as personal documents once everyone can build.
  > [zh] Copplestone 认为，当人人都能写软件，个人软件可能会像个人文档一样普遍。
- The conversation with Garry, Harj and Jared covers how to design products that agents actually want to use, and why knowing what to build may matter more than knowing how to build it.
  > [zh] 与 Garry、Harj、Jared 的对话还谈到如何设计 Agent 真正愿意用的产品，以及为什么"知道该做什么"可能比"知道怎么做"更重要。
