## 要点

- Serval is an AI platform for enterprise IT where anyone can build automations, while permissions are safeguarded by strict deterministic approvals and an audit trail.
  > [zh] Serval 是面向企业 IT 的 AI 平台，人人都能搭自动化，同时用严格的确定性审批和审计日志守住权限。
- Stauch says AI was the big unlock for attacking ServiceNow as the system of record for enterprise automation and service management, and he is betting coding agents keep getting better.
  > [zh] Stauch 认为 AI 是挑战 ServiceNow 这一企业自动化与服务管理“记录系统”的关键突破，他押注编码 Agent 会持续变强。
- ProServal embeds automation engineers directly with customers for ongoing support.
  > [zh] ProServal 团队把自动化工程师直接派驻到客户那里，提供持续支持。
- Also covers why personal agents fall short in enterprise, token maxing and ROI, why the "SaaS-pocalypse" may look more like consolidation, and why background agents are still a moonshot in enterprise software.
  > [zh] 还谈到个人 Agent 在企业里的局限、token 用量与 ROI、所谓“SaaS 末日”为何更像整合，以及后台 Agent 为何在企业软件里仍是登月级目标。
