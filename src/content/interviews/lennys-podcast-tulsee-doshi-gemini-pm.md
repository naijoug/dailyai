## 要点

- Tulsee leads product for Google's frontier AI models, including Gemini and multimodal models such as Nano Banana, Genie, Veo and Lyria, and compares model development to Snape's potions class: not just engineering.
  > [zh] Tulsee 负责 Google 前沿 AI 模型的产品，包括 Gemini 以及 Nano Banana、Genie、Veo、Lyria 等多模态模型；她把模型开发比作斯内普的魔药课，不只是工程问题。
- The episode covers Google's full-stack frontier AI strategy and what product managers actually do inside a frontier AI lab.
  > [zh] 节目讨论了 Google 的全栈前沿 AI 战略，以及产品经理在前沿 AI 实验室里实际做什么。
- She lays out a 60-20-20 framework for balancing what works now against what's almost working, so teams can ship ahead of the curve.
  > [zh] 她给出「60-20-20」框架，用来平衡「现在已经可用」和「快要可用」的能力，让团队能超前交付。
- Practical segment on how to write your first eval, and why evals and "vibes" both matter when judging models.
  > [zh] 实操部分讲如何写出第一个评测，以及评估模型时为何评测数据和「体感」都不可少。
- Her headline argument: great PMs are becoming more valuable again in the AI era.
  > [zh] 她的核心观点：在 AI 时代，优秀产品经理的价值重新上升。
