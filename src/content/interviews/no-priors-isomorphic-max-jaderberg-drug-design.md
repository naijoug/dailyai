## 要点

- Isomorphic wants to turn medicine from a process of discovery into one of precision engineering, shifting discovery onto computers with predictive AI models that reach experimental accuracy.
  > [zh] Isomorphic 想把医药从「靠发现」变成「精密工程」：用能达到实验级精度的预测 AI 模型，把药物发现搬到计算机上。
- Jaderberg treats AlphaFold as the first proof that a deep learning model can generalize in biology, and argues that models compounding what they learn generalize better with scale, raising both the odds of success and the speed of real clinical trials.
  > [zh] Jaderberg 把 AlphaFold 看作深度学习模型能在生物学里泛化的第一个证明；他认为模型不断累积所学、随规模扩大泛化更好，能同时提高真实临床试验的成功率和速度。
- Isomorphic builds drug design engines that are agnostic of disease area, target class and modality, rather than betting on one disease; the episode also covers its datasets, rethinking endpoints, automating biology labs, and internal programs versus partnerships.
  > [zh] Isomorphic 做的是不限病种、靶点类别和药物形态的药物设计引擎，而不是押注单一疾病；节目还谈到它的数据集、重新思考研发终点、自动化生物实验室，以及自研管线和合作的取舍。
- He expects in-silico models to move past the need for animal testing, and accurate molecule engineering to make multidrug cocktails across complex biological pathways feasible, improving both lifespan and healthspan.
  > [zh] 他预计计算机模型会逐步摆脱对动物实验的依赖；精确的分子工程能让横跨复杂生物通路的多药组合变得可行，从而延长寿命和健康寿命。
