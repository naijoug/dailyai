## 要点

- Innovaccer CTO Ankit Maheshwari still runs an OpenClaw “Jarvis” on a Mac Mini. He started with the approvals that were making him the bottleneck at work, then kept going until the same system was ordering lunch, calling coworkers who had not replied on Slack, negotiating restaurant reservations, and turning on the AC before he gets home.
  > [zh] Innovaccer 的 CTO Ankit Maheshwari 现在仍在一台 Mac Mini 上跑着 OpenClaw 版「Jarvis」。他先从让自己成为工作瓶颈的审批入手，后来同一套系统开始帮他点午餐、打电话催没回 Slack 的同事、谈餐厅预订，以及在到家前打开空调。
- Show notes highlight soul files and guardrails as what makes the setup trustworthy, and a mid-flight moment that tested those guardrails. He split voice calling into its own subagent; a chapter list mentions a health-coach subagent, email triage and a master soul, and that one global agent hallucinated on personas, which he fixed by using well-scoped subagents instead of a single agent for everything.
  > [zh] 节目简介把 soul file 和护栏当作这套系统可信的关键，并提到一次飞行途中的经历考验了这些护栏。他把语音外呼拆成独立的子 Agent；章节目录里还有健康教练、邮件分拣和主 soul，并提到一个包打天下的全局 Agent 会在人设上出现幻觉，于是改用职责清晰的子 Agent，而不是让一个 Agent 什么都做。
- He is pressure-testing GLM 5.3 Flash against a $500–$600-a-month Anthropic bill, asking whether a local model can cut that cost, and what GLM 5.3 Flash can and cannot do. Other tools named in the notes include Bland AI, Navan, Coupa, DoorDash Meal Manager, Claude Code, Codex, Slack, ElevenLabs and Twilio.
  > [zh] 他正在拿 GLM 5.3 Flash 去压测每月 500 到 600 美元的 Anthropic 账单，看本地模型能不能把费用打下来，以及 GLM 5.3 Flash 擅长和不擅长什么。简介里还提到 Bland AI、Navan、Coupa、DoorDash Meal Manager、Claude Code、Codex、Slack、ElevenLabs 和 Twilio 等工具。
- A recurring rule in the episode is to automate every “1% annoyance,” and he says he just talks to OpenClaw like a new hire.
  > [zh] 节目里反复提到一条规则：把每一个「1% 的烦心事」都自动化；他说自己就像对新入职的同事一样，直接跟 OpenClaw 说话。
