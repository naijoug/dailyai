## 要点

- Final Round AI 的第一笔订阅来自一位离面试只剩十分钟的用户：Michael 随口报了每月 99 美元，对方马上付款，这让团队看清实时面试助手值得单独做。
  > [en] Final Round AI's first subscription came from a user ten minutes before an interview: Michael quoted $99 a month off the cuff and the user paid at once, which convinced the team a real-time interview assistant was worth building on its own.
- 候选人用 AI 把面试答得越来越漂亮后，企业想看他们实际怎么做事；团队搭了虚拟工作空间做试工并用 AI 分析过程，结果有 AI 实验室上门想买试工的桌面录屏，团队最终选择自己做数字员工。
  > [en] Once candidates used AI to ace interviews, employers wanted to see how they actually worked; the team built a virtual workspace for work trials analyzed by AI, AI labs then asked to buy the desktop recordings, and the team chose to build digital workers themselves instead.
- Hellyeah 和专业投放团队合作，把何时调预算、如何在平台间分配的经验做成 AI 能执行的流程，产品要接触客户代码和归因数据；徐文浩追问企业凭什么开放这些权限、为什么不自己做投放。
  > [en] Hellyeah works with professional media-buying teams to turn their judgment on when to shift budgets and how to split them across platforms into workflows AI can run, which means touching customers' code and attribution data; host Xu Wenhao presses on why companies would grant that access instead of doing it in-house.
- 对「Claude 自己下场投广告怎么办」两人分歧明显：徐文浩认为大模型公司迟早接手投放，Michael 认为这会让更多客户看到这个市场的价值。
  > [en] They split sharply on what happens if Claude starts running ads itself: Xu thinks model labs will eventually take over ad buying, while Michael thinks it will show more customers the value of the market.
- 团队内部让机器人向 Slack 同步进度、用异步更新取代每日站会，同事各自的 Agent 会在群里互相跟进；原面试产品仍有团队维护并为新业务提供现金流。
  > [en] Internally, bots post progress to Slack, async updates replace daily standups, and colleagues' agents follow up with each other in group chats; the original interview product is still maintained and funds the new bet.
