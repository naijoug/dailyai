## AI 热点
1. **Google DeepMind announces Genie 4, its most realistic and interactive world model**: steer scenes in real time with natural language (events, camera, weather, new characters), with natively generated audio including ambient sound, music and real-time character dialogue; Gemini models provide reasoning for multi-step events, scenes stay consistent for about two minutes (double Genie 3), and outputs carry SynthID. It is a research preview for trusted testers, coming to Google AI Ultra in Project Genie and early-access Gemini API / AI Studio partners; Google admits details still drift over long sessions. 17K+ likes. https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2115114028338962672 | https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/
> [zh] **Google DeepMind 发布 Genie 4，迄今最真实、最可交互的世界模型**：可用自然语言实时操控场景（事件、镜头、天气、新角色），并原生生成环境音、音乐和角色实时对话；Gemini 模型负责多步事件的推理，场景可保持约两分钟一致（Genie 3 的两倍），生成内容带 SynthID 水印。目前仅向受信测试者开放研究预览，之后进入 Google AI Ultra 的 Project Genie 和 Gemini API / AI Studio 早期合作方；官方承认长时间运行细节仍会漂移。1.7 万+ 赞。
2. **Anthropic shares early results from using Claude in drug discovery**: over the past few months its scientists partnered with six organizations developing new medicines, and it has started in-house drug discovery; per the blog summary, Claude generated about 35K antibody designs in roughly a month, coordinated 11 outside labs for a one-person biotech and caught 7 manufacturing issues human reviewers missed. The work is mostly preclinical, with an in-house program that could reach human trials as soon as late 2028. 6.7K likes, 2.1K bookmarks. https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2115178675050307942
> [zh] **Anthropic 公布 Claude 参与新药研发的早期成果**：过去几个月其科学家与六家新药研发机构深度合作，并启动了自己的药物发现项目；据博客摘要，Claude 约一个月生成约 3.5 万个抗体设计，替一家单人生物科技公司协调 11 家外部实验室，还发现了 7 处人工审查漏掉的生产问题。工作大多仍处于临床前，自有项目最快 2028 年底进入人体试验。6700 赞、2100 收藏。
3. **宝玉：Axiom 用几十个 AI agent 24 天在 Lean 里核完「有限单群分类定理」**：这个散落在 500 多篇论文、约 1.5 万页的证明被改写成约 4310 万行可机器检查的代码；定理本身成立，但 AI 发现 Walter 1986 年「不平衡群定理」证明引用的一个说法有反例，Axiom 在原作者 Lyons、Solomon 指点下由 AI 写出新证法（35 页论文）补上。其余多为笔误类问题；Lean 保证推导正确，但定理陈述是否翻译准确仍需人核，目前尚无外部独立复核。https://x.com/dotey/status/2115145479222665228
> [en] **Baoyu: Axiom used dozens of AI agents to verify the Classification of Finite Simple Groups in Lean in 24 days**: the ~15,000-page proof spread over 500+ papers was rewritten as about 43.1M lines of machine-checkable code; the theorem holds, but the AI found a counterexample to a claim cited in Walter's 1986 proof of the Unbalanced Group Theorem, and with guidance from original contributors Lyons and Solomon, Axiom's AI wrote a new proof (a 35-page paper) to patch it. The rest were mostly typo-level issues; Lean guarantees the derivation, but whether the theorem statement is faithfully translated still needs human review, and there is no external independent check yet.
4. **Google's TPUs will run on a hyperscale cloud outside Google for the first time, via Oracle**: Oracle deploys capacity from calendar 2027 and supplies power, cooling and networking, linking TPU clusters to Oracle databases and storage; Google claims 8th-gen TPUs deliver up to 12.6 PFLOPS FP4 per chip, and its open-source TorchTPU stack runs existing PyTorch code plus vLLM and SGLang without model rewrites. https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2115228163408314757
> [zh] **谷歌 TPU 首次在谷歌之外的超大规模云上运行，合作方是 Oracle**：Oracle 从 2027 年起部署算力，负责电力、散热和网络，把 TPU 集群直连 Oracle 数据库与存储；谷歌称第 8 代 TPU 单芯片 FP4 算力最高 12.6 PFLOPS，开源 TorchTPU 栈可直接运行现有 PyTorch 代码及 vLLM、SGLang，无需改写模型。

## 有趣用法和干货
1. **宝玉：Anthropic 工程经理 Felix Rieseberg 面试不再考编程题，「冲劲胜过资历」**：理由是 Claude 已比人更会做这类题；他给新人五条建议：盯业务影响、想一个大胆点子、用一个周末做出原型、一个原型只放一个好点子、用好新人视角；Claude Code 桌面版就是两位新同事周末做出原型、约八天后上线的。400 赞、420+ 收藏。https://x.com/dotey/status/2115218749888634988
> [en] **Baoyu: Anthropic engineering manager Felix Rieseberg drops coding questions from interviews — "hunger beats tenure"**: Claude now does these better than people; his five tips for newcomers: focus on business impact, pick one bold idea, build a prototype in a weekend, keep one good idea per prototype, and use your fresh perspective; the Claude Code desktop app started as two new hires' weekend prototype and shipped about eight days later. 400 likes, 420+ bookmarks.
2. **宝玉：Anthropic 平台工程负责人 Katelyn Lesse 谈上千个 Agent 协作的四个工程难题**：调度（Agent 有状态、空闲不定，Kubernetes 不适配，可参考 Google 开源的 Agent Substrate）、身份与授权（临时凭证可借用，授权基本空白）、安全隔离（一个被提示注入的 Agent 会经共享记忆传染同伴，需严格沙箱）、审计与总闸（不可篡改日志加一键全停）。https://x.com/dotey/status/2115229306490126432
> [en] **Baoyu: Anthropic platform engineering lead Katelyn Lesse on four engineering problems for thousands of collaborating agents**: scheduling (agents are stateful with unpredictable idle time, a poor fit for Kubernetes; see Google's open-source Agent Substrate), identity and authorization (short-lived credentials help, authorization is largely unsolved), isolation (one prompt-injected agent can infect peers via shared memory, so strict sandboxes), and audit plus a kill switch (tamper-proof logs and one-step shutdown).
3. **meng shao：Hugging Face「工业界怎么训练 Agent」线下分享整理**：Training Agents 课程（轨迹 SFT → 蒸馏 → GRPO → RL 环境）的延续；要点包括评测要测「模型 + harness + 运维」而非裸模型、RL 中 Agent 会在沙箱里找参考答案或改评分逻辑作弊、约 9 万个公开 RL 环境过滤后只剩约 800 个较干净，约 1% 的作弊题会污染整轮训练。PPT 与课程均免费。https://x.com/shao__meng/status/2115249578882826742 | https://huggingface.co/buckets/burtenshaw/training-agents-irl
> [en] **meng shao: notes from Hugging Face's in-person "how industry trains agents" sessions**: a sequel to the Training Agents course (trajectory SFT → distillation → GRPO → RL environments); takeaways include evaluating "model + harness + ops" rather than the bare model, agents in RL finding reference answers in the sandbox or tampering with graders, only ~800 fairly clean environments left after filtering ~90K public RL environments, and ~1% of hackable tasks polluting a whole training run. Slides and course are free.
