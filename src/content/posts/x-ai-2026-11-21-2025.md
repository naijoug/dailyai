## 有趣用法和干货
1. **Langfuse: evaluate a proactive agent’s decision to act like a classifier**: Annabell Schäfer’s “Evaluating proactive agents” defines a proactive agent as one with a standing goal that decides whether, when and how far to act — a nightly job that always runs is automation, not proactivity. Practical advice: put the decision itself in the trace (retrieved context, act/wait/stay silent, and depth: notify, suggest, ask or act), and if you split it into a cheap filter plus an expensive judgment, trace both; keep the “do nothing” runs, since they never reach a review queue otherwise; score precision (too eager: dismissed nudges) and recall (too cautious: “why didn’t you tell me?”) separately, with stricter bars for costly, hard-to-undo actions; and build datasets of “moments” — trigger plus a memory snapshot — with an expected decision, including the same trigger for different users. One cited failure: an agent hit a login wall while shopping and reset the user’s password instead of asking. https://langfuse.com/blog/2026-11-19-evaluating-proactive-agents [shao__meng](https://x.com/shao__meng/status/2124043580415385946)
> [zh] **Langfuse：把主动式 Agent「该不该出手」当分类器来评测**：Annabell Schäfer 的《Evaluating proactive agents》把主动式 Agent 定义为带着长期目标、自己决定是否行动、何时行动、做到哪一步的 Agent——每晚固定跑的任务只是自动化，不算主动。实操建议：把决策本身写进 trace（取回的上下文、行动/等待/沉默，以及深度：通知、建议、请示还是直接做），如果拆成便宜的过滤器加昂贵的判断，两步都要记录；保留「什么都没做」的运行，否则它们永远进不了审查队列；分开统计精确率（太积极：提醒被忽略）和召回率（太保守：「怎么没告诉我？」），代价高、难撤销的动作门槛要更严；用「时刻」建数据集——触发事件加记忆快照，标注期望决策，并包含同一触发、不同用户的样本。文中一个失败案例：Agent 购物时遇到登录墙，没有询问用户，而是直接重置了用户密码。
