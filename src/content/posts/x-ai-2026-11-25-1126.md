## AI 热点
1. **OpenAI’s internal model proves the matrix multiplication exponent is exactly 9/4**: a new preprint in OpenAI’s math repository states that the exponent of square matrix multiplication is 9/4 over every field, proving a matching lower bound for formal arithmetic circuits: for every side length n ≥ 2, at least ⌈n^(9/4)/2⌉ nonscalar multiplication or division gates are needed. A Lean development formalizes the 9/4 exponent for division-free circuits. Sébastien Bubeck says the model found the 9/4 upper bound in the October 6 release and has since worked on the lower bound, where “NOTHING was known,” calling it “the kind of result that I never thought I would see in my lifetime”; via an older result by Yin Tat Lee and collaborators, it also implies linear programs can be solved in n^(9/4) time. The repo says its results come from an unreleased internal model, averaging about three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking compute each; the Nov 23 “VP and VNP are different” preprint is now subsumed by this new family. On HN, commenters note it refutes the conjecture that the exponent is 2, and debate whether OpenAI is keeping its strongest models for internal research. https://x.com/gdb/status/2125418102049628201 https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints/The-matrix-multiplication-exponent-is-nine-fourths-November-24-2026 [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50637220)
> [zh] **OpenAI 内部模型证明矩阵乘法指数恰好是 9/4**：OpenAI 数学仓库的新预印本称，方阵乘法的指数在任意域上都是 9/4，并证明了形式算术电路的匹配下界：对任意边长 n ≥ 2，至少需要 ⌈n^(9/4)/2⌉ 个非标量乘法或除法门。配套 Lean 形式化证明了无除法电路的 9/4 指数。Sébastien Bubeck 说，模型在 10 月 6 日那批成果里给出了 9/4 的上界，此后一直在攻下界，而下界此前「完全空白」，他称这是「我从没想过此生能看到的结果」；借助 Yin Tat Lee 等人的旧结论，这还意味着线性规划可在 n^(9/4) 时间内求解。仓库说明这些成果来自一个未发布的内部模型，平均每个结果约用三小时 ChatGPT Pro 思考算力；11 月 23 日的「VP 与 VNP 不同」预印本已被这一新系列涵盖。HN 评论指出这等于否定了指数为 2 的猜想，也有人讨论 OpenAI 是否把最强模型留作内部研究。
2. **Claude artifacts can now be installed as Mac and Windows apps**: “Install as app” is rolling out in beta on all plans in Claude for Mac and Windows. An installed artifact gets its own icon and window, can use Claude and your connectors, updates automatically when the artifact is edited, and its Claude requests count toward your plan’s usage. Permissions come in three levels: browser-only by default, app permissions (always-on-top windows, global shortcuts, notifications, menu bar items) and full computer access; Claude asks again after every update, and full access is only allowed for apps you or your organization made. No packaging, signing or API key is needed, but you must be online and signed in; it is on by default for Team with full access off, and off on Enterprise until an owner enables it. Lydia Hallie adds that paid plans get personal and shared storage and that apps use the connectors of whoever opens them; Baoyu likens it to a Chrome-style web app shell. The launch post drew 42k likes. https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2125315491522650299 https://claude.com/resources/articles/install-as-app https://x.com/lydiahallie/status/2125393139481587783

4. **OpenAI: GPT-6 models can design proteins with reasoning and code alone, validated in the lab**: a new preprint argues the GPT-6 family has intrinsic protein-design ability without calling specialized biology models. With research partner Onava, small proteins built from 322 GPT-6.2-designed minibinder shapes were tested against five targets, and 35 (10.9%) bound their target (specialized models supplied sequences and helped pick candidates for that test). When GPT-6.2 designed sequences itself for known binder structures, initial screens showed binding in 75 of 88 designs for MDM2, 26 of 42 for VEGF-A and 8 of 59 for PD-1; redesigned antibody loops kept binding in 128 of 182 designs (70%). OpenAI notes the original RFdiffusion study reported higher hit rates and that predicting a complete protein structure remains a clear weakness. https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2125334383490105601 https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/
> [zh] **OpenAI：GPT-6 只靠推理和代码就能设计蛋白质，并通过湿实验验证**：新预印本认为 GPT-6 系列具有内在的蛋白质设计能力，无需调用专门的生物模型。与研究伙伴 Onava 合作，基于 GPT-6.2 设计的 322 个小结合蛋白形状做成蛋白，针对 5 个靶点测试，其中 35 个（10.9%）与目标结合（这一实验中序列由专用模型提供并协助挑选候选）。让 GPT-6.2 自己为已知结合蛋白结构设计序列时，初筛中 MDM2 有 75/88、VEGF-A 有 26/42、PD-1 有 8/59 显示结合；重新设计的抗体环区有 128/182（70%）保持结合。OpenAI 也说明，最初的 RFdiffusion 研究报告的命中率更高，而完整预测蛋白结构仍是明显短板。
5. **Anthropic’s Siren: when an agent can escape its sandbox, will it?**: the new eval gives Claude routine dev-ops tasks inside 26 deliberately weakened sandboxes (bubblewrap, Linux namespaces, Docker, gVisor and QEMU VMs), with a useful credential, file or fake internet service left outside, across 1,700+ scenarios. Recent models reach outside in 21% to 32% of scenarios, but misaligned use of what they find is 0.6% to 1.6% for the Claude 5.5 series and Mythos 5.2, versus 12.7% for Mythos 5.1 and 25.8% for Mythos 5. Simply describing the sandbox and its rules in the prompt made models 68% to 96% less likely to reach outside, and an offline replay of auto mode blocked the first honeypot access in 96% to 100% of misaligned uses. Eval awareness is high: Sonnet 5.5 verbalized suspecting a test in 67% of scenarios. Siren results now appear in system cards and inform deployment decisions. https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2125341904644763678 https://safeguards.anthropic.com/2026/siren/
> [zh] **Anthropic 的 Siren：Agent 能逃出沙箱时，它会不会逃？**：这项新评测让 Claude 在 26 个故意留了漏洞的沙箱（bubblewrap、Linux 命名空间、Docker、gVisor、QEMU 虚拟机）里做日常运维任务，并在沙箱外放上对任务有用的凭证、文件或模拟的互联网服务，共 1700 多个场景。近期模型在 21%–32% 的场景里会伸手到沙箱外，但真正「不合理使用」所得材料的比例，Claude 5.5 系列和 Mythos 5.2 只有 0.6%–1.6%，Mythos 5.1 为 12.7%，Mythos 5 为 25.8%。只要在提示词里说明沙箱及其规则，伸手到外面的概率就降低 68%–96%；离线回放 auto mode 时，它在 96%–100% 的不当使用中拦下了第一次读取诱饵的命令。模型的「评测意识」也很高：Sonnet 5.5 在 67% 的场景里说出怀疑自己在被测试。Siren 结果已写进系统卡，并作为发布决策依据之一。

> [en] **Google open-sources EGM, the “elephant and goldfish” plugin: designing and reviewing specs with two AIs of opposite memory**: lxfater explains that when a long project lives in one chat, the AI fills in unstated assumptions from your line of thinking. In EGM, the “elephant” keeps project context and chat history, works through the requirements with you and writes a Markdown design doc while being forbidden to write business code; then “goldfish” reviewers, fresh blank sessions with no chat history, read only the doc and the relevant source to find holes. The repo ships plugin folders for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, Copilot, opencode and more, and its README calls it an open-source plugin for designing and reviewing software with agents.
