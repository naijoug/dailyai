## 有趣用法和干货
1. **Rohan Paul: agents with long-term memory almost never truly forget**: a Tsinghua paper builds BEMULA (50 long synthetic chats, 135 forget requests, each with a no-request twin) and finds no setup across 6 memory systems and 3 backbones passed more than 1.5% of forget requests end to end; after a forget request, systems blocked at most 35.7% of simple extraction prompts like role-play or yes/no guessing. Takeaway: if your agent remembers users, build and test a real delete path—a confirmation message doesn’t mean the data is gone. https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2117929277614149670
> [zh] **Rohan Paul：有长期记忆的 Agent 几乎做不到真正「忘记」**：清华的论文构建了 BEMULA（50 段长合成对话、135 条遗忘请求，每条都配一份不含请求的对照），发现 6 种记忆系统 × 3 个基座模型里，端到端通过率没有一个超过 1.5%；收到遗忘请求后，面对角色扮演、是非题猜测等简单套话，最多也只拦下 35.7%。结论：如果你的 Agent 会记住用户，就要真正实现并测试删除路径——回一句「已删除」不代表数据没了。
