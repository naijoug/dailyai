## 科技新闻
1. **Claude helps decode Kurt Gödel's 1,009 pages of shorthand notebooks, surfacing hundreds of unknown theorems (HN ~340 pts)**: logician Jan von Plato, who had been reading Gödel's 1940s Gabelsberger-shorthand "workbooks" at about a page a week, gave Claude his transcriptions plus his own books and got a 650-page explanation within a couple of days. Claude reconstructed Gödel's near-complete proof of the independence of the axiom of choice, which passed the Coq proof assistant, and extracted 663 theorems: about 400 verified as stated, about 200 repaired and about 50 false. Notre Dame's Joel David Hamkins called it remarkable if it holds up, but noted that how much is Claude and how much is Gödel is a job for historians. https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/nov/13/kurt-godel-notebooks-ai-helps-unlock-hidden-discoveries-man-who-transformed-maths | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50486748
> [zh] **Claude 帮学者读完哥德尔 1009 页速记笔记，挖出数百条无人知晓的定理（HN 约 340 分）**：逻辑学家 Jan von Plato 研读哥德尔上世纪 40 年代用 Gabelsberger 速记写的「工作簿」，原本一周只能啃一页；他把自己的转写稿和几本相关著作交给 Claude，几天内拿到一份 650 页的解读。Claude 重建了哥德尔几乎完成的「选择公理独立性」证明，并通过了 Coq 证明助手校验；它提取出 663 条定理，约 400 条照原样验证通过、约 200 条经修补成立、约 50 条是错的。圣母大学的 Joel David Hamkins 认为若站得住会很了不起，但哪些归 Claude、哪些归哥德尔，要留给史学家判断。
