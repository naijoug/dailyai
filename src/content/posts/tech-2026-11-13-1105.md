## 科技新闻
1. **"Top Chinese frontier AI models carry the fingerprint of export-controlled Nvidia Blackwell GPUs" (HN ~190 pts)**: Will DePue's forensic analysis of published weights recovers traces of each model's initial values and finds hardware configurations that reproduce them. Six models released since April (DeepSeek-V4-Flash and V4-Pro, Kimi K3 and K3.1-Pro, MiniMax-M3, Ling-3.1-flash) match Blackwell-class B200/B300 or GB200/GB300 chips, while the same labs' earlier models match Hopper; DeepSeek-V4-Flash and Kimi K3 were confirmed on physical Blackwell hardware. The report says this shows access to the chips, not where they were or whether export controls were violated. https://unemploymentlabs.com/checkpoint-forensics | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50474883
> [zh] **《中国头部大模型带着受出口管制的英伟达 Blackwell GPU 指纹》（HN 约 190 分）**：Will DePue 对公开权重做取证：还原各模型初始化数值的痕迹，再找出能复现它们的硬件配置。4 月以来发布的 6 个模型（DeepSeek-V4-Flash 和 V4-Pro、Kimi K3 和 K3.1-Pro、MiniMax-M3、Ling-3.1-flash）与 Blackwell 级的 B200/B300 或 GB200/GB300 吻合，而同一批实验室更早的模型对应 Hopper；DeepSeek-V4-Flash 和 Kimi K3 还在实体 Blackwell 芯片上复核过。报告强调这只证明用到了这些芯片，不能说明芯片在哪、怎么拿到，也不能认定违反了出口管制。
2. **Microsoft 365 outage knocks out Outlook worldwide for hours (HN ~555 pts)**: starting just before 1PM ET on Nov 12, users around the world could not reach Outlook on the web or in apps, with some Teams issues; Microsoft blamed elevated load on part of its infrastructure across multiple regions, mainly hitting Exchange Online. By about 6PM ET it had returned some servers to service and shifted traffic, with no ETA for full recovery. https://www.theverge.com/tech/1032226/microsoft-outlook-is-down | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50471731
> [zh] **微软 365 故障，Outlook 全球中断数小时（HN 约 555 分）**：从美东时间 11 月 12 日下午 1 点前开始，全球用户无法通过网页或 App 访问 Outlook，Teams 也有部分问题；微软称多个区域的部分基础设施负载过高，主要影响 Exchange Online。到美东晚 6 点左右，一部分服务器已恢复、流量已改道，但仍没有完全恢复的时间表。
3. **The Verge: "No, AI agents did not discover a previously unknown Maya site"**: the site Pietro Miotti's 48 agents flagged in NASA lidar data had already been documented in an academic journal more than a year earlier. Archaeologists note that a site missing from Mexico's INAH register is not the same as unmapped, since researchers don't publish coordinates; Miotti says his agents missed it because checking means comparing images against figures in papers, and he has updated his post to credit the earlier work. https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1032125/ai-agents-maya-site-archeology-discovery
> [zh] **The Verge：「不，AI Agent 并没有发现未知的玛雅遗址」**：Pietro Miotti 的 48 个 Agent 从 NASA 激光雷达数据里标出的那处遗址，一年多前就已在学术期刊上发表过。考古学家指出，墨西哥 INAH 登记册里没有，不等于没被测绘过，因为研究者不会公开遗址坐标；Miotti 说他的 Agent 漏掉了它，因为核实需要拿图像去对比论文里的插图，他已更新博客注明前人的工作。
