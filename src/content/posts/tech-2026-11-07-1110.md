## 科技新闻
1. **BioBasicsBench tests AI on messy, everyday lab evidence**: OpenAI and Ligo’s November 6 release introduces 100 wet-lab analysis tasks using raw experimental data, including ambiguous images and failed controls. OpenAI reports 66.7% mean rubric credit for GPT-6 Astra at xhigh, versus 43.8% for GPT-5.6 Sol; this is not a task-pass rate. Remaining failures show assumptions overriding evidence, so improved scores do not establish reliable autonomous lab judgment. https://openai.com/index/measuring-how-ai-agents-handle-wet-lab-science/
> [zh] **BioBasicsBench 用真实实验中的模糊证据测试 AI**：OpenAI 与 Ligo 11 月 6 日推出 100 项湿实验分析任务，覆盖原始实验数据、模糊图像和失效对照。OpenAI 自报 GPT-6 Astra 在 xhigh 设置下获得 66.7% 的平均评分细则分数，GPT-5.6 Sol 为 43.8%；这不是任务通过率。模型仍会让假设压过证据，分数提高不代表已能自主作出可靠实验判断。
2. **Muse Spaces turns prompts into persistent personal tools**: Meta’s November 6 release lets users create and revise planners, trackers, games and dashboards inside Muse, then pin them or schedule when they appear. Meta says connected apps can keep spaces updated in the background. Spaces are available wherever Muse is available; sharing them with other people is still being explored. https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/introducing-spaces-customize-muse-app/
> [zh] **Muse Spaces 把提示词变成可持续使用的个人工具**：Meta 11 月 6 日发布的新功能允许用户在 Muse 内创建并修改规划器、追踪器、游戏和仪表盘，并固定展示或安排出现时间。Meta 称接入应用后，空间可在后台更新。功能覆盖所有 Muse 可用地区；与他人分享空间仍处于探索阶段。

## 开源
1. **淘宝 MNN-Pocket-0.3B：面向手机应用的轻量模型**：Alibaba 官方仓库记录 11 月 4 日发布，IT之家 11 月 6 日补充报道；模型支持中英文与 32K 上下文，采用 Apache 2.0 许可。团队称 INT4 QAT 的 MNN 部署包为 173MB，在 iPhone 18 Pro 上离线解码约 350 token/s；这些是指定设备与配置下的自报结果，不代表所有手机表现。https://www.ithome.com/1/023/212.htm | https://github.com/alibaba/MNN | https://huggingface.co/collections/taobao-mnn/mnn-pocket-03b
> [en] **Taobao’s MNN-Pocket-0.3B targets on-device mobile apps**: Alibaba’s repository dates the release to November 4, with IT Home reporting it on November 6. The Apache-2.0 model supports Chinese, English and 32K context. The team reports a 173MB INT4 QAT MNN deployment package and roughly 350 tokens/s offline decoding on iPhone 18 Pro; these device-specific results should not be generalized to every phone.
