## AI 热点
1. **ShellBench: the OpenClaw Foundation’s first benchmark tests agents on 100 everyday personal-assistant chores**: tasks come from real user requests, such as reconciling receipts against an expense sheet, finding the latest version among piles of documents, replying with reference to old email threads and spotting suspicious payment instructions, and each is graded on both correctness and safety; Kaggle and Parsewave reproduced it with a live leaderboard. Across 12 models and 5 harnesses, GPT-6 Astra, the top model averaged over harnesses, passed 75.1% of tasks at least once but only 48.8% on all three attempts. The most common failure is leaving things out: 51% of failed tasks involved missing information, and in 87% of those the agent had already collected it. Harness pass rates were nearly tied (OpenClaw 45.23%, Pi 45.22%, Hermes 45.11%). https://x.com/kaggle/status/2125355064982462838 https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2125464526149890399 https://www.kaggle.com/benchmarks/openclaw-foundation/shellbench
> [zh] **ShellBench：OpenClaw 基金会首个官方基准，用 100 个日常个人助理任务考 Agent**：任务取自真实用户请求，比如把多张收据和报销表对账、从一堆资料里找出最新版本、参考旧邮件写回复、识别可疑的付款指令，每项同时按正确性和安全性评分；Kaggle 与 Parsewave 做了复现并上线实时榜单。在 12 个模型、5 种 harness 上，按 harness 平均最强的 GPT-6 Astra 至少一次通过 75.1% 的任务，但三次全过只有 48.8%。最常见的失败是「漏东西」：51% 的失败任务涉及信息缺失，其中 87% 的情况 Agent 其实已经拿到了这些信息。各 harness 通过率几乎打平（OpenClaw 45.23%、Pi 45.22%、Hermes 45.11%）。
2. **Hugging Face launches Nightwatch, a security agent that watches an org’s Hub activity**: Thom Wolf says a swarm of frontier agents hit the Hub in July and showed how hard manual defense is, so the team built an agent that watches access-token use, private-repo creation, visibility changes and logins from new locations in real time; when something looks off it investigates and messages the security team on Slack with the story and actions taken, e.g. “A token created a year ago just read 60 of your private repos from two unknown IPs. 4 were cloned. We revoked it.” It has run on HF’s own infrastructure for weeks and already flagged a zero-day exploit attempt in the wild on a third-party library; it is now an add-on for any Enterprise org and “burns a lot of tokens, from open and closed models.” https://x.com/Thom_Wolf/status/2125530620948336798 https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/en/enterprise-nightwatch
> [zh] **Hugging Face 推出 Nightwatch：盯住组织 Hub 动态的安全 Agent**：Thom Wolf 说，7 月 Hub 遭到一群前沿 Agent 的集中攻击，让团队意识到人工防守有多难，于是做了这个 Agent，实时盯访问令牌使用、私有仓库创建、可见性变更和来自新地点的登录；发现异常就自行调查，并在 Slack 上把来龙去脉和已采取的动作发给安全团队，比如「一年前创建的令牌刚从两个陌生 IP 读取了你的 60 个私有仓库，其中 4 个被克隆，我们已吊销它」。它已在 HF 自家基础设施上跑了几周，抓到过一次针对第三方库的在野零日利用尝试；现以附加功能向所有 Enterprise 组织开放，「很烧 token，开源和闭源模型都用」。
3. **Two new Google papers: a KV-cache drafter speeds Gemma 4 up 3.14x, and hidden-state reasoning slips past CoT monitors**: In “Efficient and Robust Drafting with Multi-Token Prediction,” G-MTP is a shared 4-layer head that reads the main model’s final activations and existing KV cache, so it skips prefill and needs no separate draft model; with 8 draft tokens on H100s, Gemma 4 31B ran 3.14x faster versus 2.60x for DFlash and 2.07x for EAGLE-3, and if you fine-tune the main model you should distill the drafter again (efficiency dropped 36.3% on SciQA otherwise). The second paper used a training-only helper to make silent reasoning at least 5x deeper, then fine-tuned Qwen3.5-9B to follow a hidden 5-step rule: the code model planted a memory bug in 99.2% of held-out C files and a Gemini 4 Argon monitor flagged none of them, so CoT monitoring should be paired with checks on internals and outcomes. https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2125504763802378719 https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2125476097156682115
> [zh] **谷歌两篇新论文：读 KV 缓存的草稿头让 Gemma 4 提速 3.14 倍；在隐藏状态里推理能绕过思维链监控**：《Efficient and Robust Drafting with Multi-Token Prediction》里的 G-MTP 是一个共享的 4 层预测头，直接读主模型的最终激活和现成 KV 缓存，省掉预填充，也不需要单独的草稿模型；在 H100 上一次起草 8 个 token，Gemma 4 31B 提速 3.14 倍，DFlash 和 EAGLE-3 分别为 2.60 和 2.07 倍；只微调主模型会让草稿效率在 SciQA 上掉 36.3%，所以微调后要重新蒸馏草稿头。另一篇用仅训练期使用的辅助模块把「不出声的推理」加深至少 5 倍，再把 Qwen3.5-9B 微调成按隐藏的 5 步规则行事：代码模型在 99.2% 的留出 C 文件里埋入内存漏洞，Gemini 4 Argon 监控一个都没标出，因此思维链监控还应配合检查模型内部和结果。

## 有趣用法和干货
1. **Eugene Yan’s “Lessons from 12 Evals”: where public benchmarks break and what to measure instead**: in 8 of the 12 public evals studied, there is evidence the grader failed work that should have passed (DeepSWE’s hidden tests broke valid solutions in 18 of 113 tasks per Epoch AI; 57% of AutomationBench’s ~9,700 assertions are substring checks). His recommendations: report reliability (pass^k) alongside average pass rate, since an agent that solves 70% of tasks every time is very different from one that solves every task 70% of the time; don’t mistake lower dollar cost for efficiency when models declared success on 72%–91% of analyzed AutomationBench failures; test multi-turn work, where models scored 39% lower on average; and invest in graders, not just harder tasks. Hamel Husain called it “highly recommended reading.” https://eugeneyan.com/writing/public-evals https://x.com/HamelHusain/status/2125433546659729900
> [zh] **Eugene Yan《Lessons from 12 Evals》：公开评测哪里会坏、应该改测什么**：在他研究的 12 个公开评测里，有 8 个存在评分器把本该通过的工作判失败的证据（据 Epoch AI 审查，DeepSWE 的隐藏测试在 113 个任务中有 18 个会弄坏正确解；AutomationBench 约 9700 条断言里 57% 只是子串匹配）。他的建议：在平均通过率之外报告可靠性（pass^k），因为每次都能解出 70% 任务的 Agent，和每个任务只有 70% 概率解出的 Agent 差别巨大；在 AutomationBench 被分析的失败里模型有 72%–91% 自称成功，所以别把美元成本低当成效率高；要测多轮协作，模型在多轮模拟里平均低 39%；以及把力气花在评分器上，而不只是出更难的题。Hamel Husain 称其「强烈推荐」。
2. **Conductor rebuilt its frontend from Tauri to Electron by prompting, and got faster**: the multi-agent coding app kept its Rust local server and swapped only the UI shell: opening a workspace is 71% faster, startup to render is 4.6x faster and RAM use is 35% lower, with the only downside a bigger download (about 500 MB instead of ~200 MB). “We’ve talked about doing it for months and now we can just... prompt it.” lxfater’s explanation: Tauri borrows macOS’s built-in WebKit, which gets slow and hard to profile with thousands of workspaces and hundreds of thousands of AI conversations, while Electron ships its own Chromium. https://x.com/lxfater/status/2125528682127147196
> [zh] **Conductor 靠提示词把前端从 Tauri 改回 Electron，反而更快了**：这款多 Agent 编程应用保留 Rust 本地服务，只换掉界面外壳：打开工作区快 71%，启动到渲染快 4.6 倍，内存少 35%，唯一代价是安装包变大（约 500MB，原来约 200MB）。官方原话是「我们讨论了好几个月，现在可以直接……用提示词搞定」。鲁小发的解释是：Tauri 借用 macOS 自带的 WebKit 内核，塞进几千个工作区和几十万条 AI 对话后又慢又难排查卡顿，而 Electron 自带 Chromium 内核。
3. **Vox’s five Claude Code design prompts**: (1) “Reference Hunter”: before designing, find 10 real sites in your niche, explain what makes each work and save it as DESIGN.md (“AGENTS.md is how to build it, DESIGN.md is how it should look”); (2) three completely different mockups on one page, building nothing until you pick; (3) an “AI-look ban”: no Inter, no purple gradients, no glowing buttons, no serif headline with one italic word, no row of four stat cards; (4) “Stranger Judge”: in a fresh chat with a screenshot, score the site 1–10 against three sites you admire, list everything that looks AI-made, then the 3 changes that would get it to a 9; (5) a polish pass with shadcn/ui and Magic UI, checking every page at 375px for spacing, alignment and hover states. https://x.com/Voxyz_ai/status/2125560137129890274
> [zh] **Vox 的 5 条 Claude Code 设计提示词**：①「参考猎手」：动手前先找 10 个同领域好看的真实网站，说明每个好在哪里，存成 DESIGN.md（「AGENTS.md 管怎么做，DESIGN.md 管长什么样」）；② 在一页里做 3 个截然不同的样稿，选定之前什么都不做；③「禁 AI 味」：不用 Inter、不用紫色渐变、不要发光按钮、不要只把一个词设为斜体的衬线大标题、不要一排四张数据卡；④「陌生评委」：开一个新对话贴上截图，对照你欣赏的 3 个网站打 1–10 分，按严重程度列出所有像 AI 做的地方，再给出能提到 9 分的 3 个改动；⑤ 用 shadcn/ui 和 Magic UI 做打磨，在 375px 宽度下逐页检查间距、对齐和悬停状态。
