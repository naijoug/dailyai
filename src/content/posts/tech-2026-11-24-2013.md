5. **Event Horizon 浏览器 MCP**：V2EX 上的独立开发者作品，思路是不把裸 DOM 喂给 LLM，而是把整个可见页面「编译」成一套结构化契约再交给模型，作者实测比 Playwright MCP、browser-use 这类方案少用约 91.9% 的 token，能穿透 Shadow DOM、不需要写站点规则，窗口缩放也不太影响操作；目前纯文本驱动，纯 canvas 页面还不行。现在免费（单并发），客户端开源，但感知核心托管在云端、暂不开源，作者说是为了拦截银行、支付、医疗、凭据输入等高敏感站点，有回复对此提出疑问。https://ehbrowser.com/ https://github.com/Boundprogress/Event-horizon-MCP https://www.v2ex.com/t/1259480
> [en] **Event Horizon browser MCP**: an indie project on V2EX that, instead of feeding raw DOM to the LLM, “compiles” the whole visible page into a structured contract; the author measures about 91.9% fewer tokens than approaches like Playwright MCP or browser-use, says it pierces Shadow DOM without per-site rules and copes with window resizing, but is text-only for now and cannot handle pure-canvas pages. It is free (single concurrency) with an open-source client, but the perception core is hosted in the cloud and not open-sourced, which the author says is to block banking, payment, medical and credential-entry sites; a reply questioned that choice.

1. **OpenRouter drops Together AI, Phala and Novita after canary prompts leaked**: Dragos sent two million test prompts through OpenRouter, each with a unique tracking link or decoy credential, and saw data from at least three providers exposed, with some decoy credentials used to try to access its canary AWS accounts. OpenRouter says its own systems were not breached, removed the three providers, revoked Zero Data Retention eligibility for 125 endpoints and notified affected customers; Atlas Cloud withdrew voluntarily. Users are advised to rotate any secrets that may have been in their prompts, and OpenRouter will keep running Dragos-style tests before restoring ZDR. https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/openrouter-drops-ai-providers-over-leaked-prompts-and-secrets/ https://www.techmeme.com/261124/p2

2. **Anthropic study: most companies get AI value from doing more, not cutting costs**: in 66 interviews with 58 enterprise customers, Anthropic economists found most uses add value by increasing output, both by speeding up existing work and by enabling new products and services, with coding agents increasingly bringing software skills to nontechnical roles. They saw very few AI-driven domestic layoffs; more companies said they were hiring less than hiring more because of AI, and many have cut contractors, especially offshore ones, which the authors flag as an early warning for international business services. Bottlenecks shift to code review, sales and decision-making, top-down automation breeds resistance, and the highest-value cases involve reorganizing: broadening roles to own whole workflows and moving people to the tasks models cannot do. https://www.anthropic.com/research/working-with-ai
> [zh] **Anthropic 研究：多数公司从 AI 得到的价值是「做得更多」，而不是省钱**：Anthropic 的经济学家访谈了 58 家企业客户共 66 次，发现大多数用法通过提高产出创造价值，既加快现有工作，也带来新的产品和服务，编程 Agent 正越来越多地把软件能力带给非技术岗位。由 AI 自动化导致的本土裁员案例很少；说因 AI 少招人的公司多于多招人的，不少公司削减了外包，尤其是离岸外包，作者认为这可能是国际商务服务业受冲击的早期信号。瓶颈转向代码评审、销售和决策，自上而下的自动化容易引发抵触；价值最高的做法都伴随组织重构：拓宽岗位让个人负责完整流程，把人调到模型做不了的环节。

## 中文社区
1. **InfoQ：谷歌发现陈旧 TODO 注释会让编码智能体「分心」去做支线任务**：谷歌工程师 Sam Saccone 和 Madhuri Garikapati 分析了数千名工程师、数亿次智能体操作的跟踪数据：约 13% 的会话里智能体遇到了内联 TODO/FIXME 引用，其中 6% 会去处理被引用的缺陷，而提示词大多根本没提；这些「绕道」中 41% 处理的是已关闭的工单，57% 是仍开放但超出请求范围的缺陷。回归分析显示这类会话步骤多 70%、token 多 77%、等待时间多 105%。谷歌为此做了扫描智能体，必要时翻多年 Git 历史确认 TODO 已过时再提 PR 删除，6 个月清掉数千条，人工审核认可率超过 95%。https://www.infoq.cn/article/sFmubmDWkulIOTBLX0Dr
> [en] **InfoQ: Google finds stale TODO comments distract coding agents into side quests**: Google’s Sam Saccone and Madhuri Garikapati analyzed traces from thousands of engineers and hundreds of millions of agent actions: in about 13% of sessions agents hit inline TODO/FIXME references, and in 6% of those they acted on the referenced bug, usually without the prompt mentioning it; 41% of these detours targeted already-closed tickets and 57% open bugs outside the request’s scope. Regression analysis shows such sessions take 70% more steps, 77% more tokens and 105% more wall time. Google built scanner agents that, sometimes after digging through years of Git history, confirm a TODO is stale before sending a PR to remove it; they removed thousands in six months with over 95% reviewer approval.
