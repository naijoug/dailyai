> [en] **agent-desktop 1.2.0: check the MCP interface, not a universal “no cursor interference” claim**: Lxfater’s November 6 post describes background desktop control. The official November 5 changelog adds a stdio MCP server and stdin support for text commands. This CLI/C library locates controls through OS accessibility trees and contains no model; apps exposing no accessibility tree are invisible to it, while screenshots remain a separate feature. The multi-agent demonstration does not establish conflict-free focus handling in every app.
