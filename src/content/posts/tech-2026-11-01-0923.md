Its team-solve workflow (five Qwen3.8-27B roles talking through files) scored a mean 9.8/10 on the first ten LiveCodeBench Hard problems over five passes vs 8.0 for Fable 5 alone. Apache-2.0; macOS/Linux/Windows. https://memdoor.ai/ [GitHub](https://github.com/guregodevo/memdoor) [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50320995)

Open-source (Apache-2.0) and self-hostable, or Backenly Cloud; free tier available. Launched on Product Hunt Oct 31. https://backenly.com/

## 科技新闻
1. **Linux TCP handshake use-after-free CVE-2026-98432, CVSS 9.8**: a remote unauthenticated peer can hit a UAF in IPv4 TCP option handling during the handshake—before any app code runs—leaking heap memory, steering OOB writes or crashing the box. Fixed upstream Oct 11/15 (e.g. 6.12.113 / 6.18.56 / 7.2.10 lines); Amazon Linux and some distros have patched kernels, but Ubuntu and RHEL had not shipped one as of Oct 31. Reported by Anthropic’s Xinyang Ge; Anthropic says Claude produced a working root PoC. HN ~323 points. https://atlasum.net/blog/cve-2026-98432-linux-ipv4-tcp-uaf [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50319143)
> [zh] **Linux TCP 握手 Use-After-Free CVE-2026-98432，CVSS 9.8**：远程未认证对端可在握手阶段（应用代码尚未介入）打中 IPv4 TCP 选项处理里的 UAF——泄露堆内存、引导越界写或打崩机器。上游于 10 月 11/15 日修复（如 6.12.113 / 6.18.56 / 7.2.10 一线）；截至 10 月 31 日 Amazon Linux 等已有补丁内核，但 Ubuntu 与 RHEL 尚未放出。由 Anthropic 的 Xinyang Ge 报告；Anthropic 称 Claude 写出了可 root 的 PoC。HN 约 323 分。
