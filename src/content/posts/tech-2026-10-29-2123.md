## 科技新闻
1. **Claude Code burns ~15–20% of tokens on a 3-word status line**: every ~30s, for every background agent, it sends that agent's entire transcript to the agent's own model (usually Opus) just to refresh a 3–5 word status in the agents panel; there is no official off switch (on by default since 2.1.232). OpenTelemetry grouping by `query_source=agent_summary` showed 16.4% of spend and 19.7% of requests before a Bun `--preload` hook that wraps the summarizer timer; issue #86879 collects independent measurements of 18–26%. https://gist.github.com/flamerix-ai/c3905705cc4205e5de3011fa2e74efce [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50284255)
> [zh] **Claude Code 用约 15–20% 的额度只为刷一行三五个词的状态**：大约每 30 秒，对每个后台 agent，它会把该 agent 的整段 transcript 发给其自己的模型（多为 Opus），只为更新 agents 面板里 3–5 个词的状态行；没有官方开关（自 2.1.232 起默认开启）。按 OpenTelemetry 的 `query_source=agent_summary` 归类，关掉前约占花费 16.4%、请求 19.7%；一份 Bun `--preload` 钩子可包住摘要定时器，issue #86879 汇总了他人测到的 18–26%。
2. **SMS of Death: zero-click remote Android telephony DoS**: a single malformed 8-byte WAP Push SMS crashes `com.android.phone` (NegativeArraySizeException in `WapPushOverSms.decodeWapPdu`), dropping active calls including emergency calls with no tap or notification; carrier redelivery can keep re-crashing, and ~30 rapid messages can corrupt the SMS SQLite store so the phone crashes on every boot — recovery needs acknowledging the pending SMS on a non-Android device then clearing the DB. Reproduced on Pixel 9 and multiple Samsung models. https://smsofdeath.com/ [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50285452) [GitHub](https://github.com/MarkBlah/smsofdeath)
> [zh] **SMS of Death：零点击远程安卓电话 DoS**：一条畸形的 8 字节 WAP Push 短信即可让 `com.android.phone` 崩溃（`WapPushOverSms.decodeWapPdu` 里 NegativeArraySizeException），挂断包括紧急呼叫在内的通话，无点击、无通知；运营商重投会反复打崩，约 30 条连发还可损坏短信 SQLite，导致每次开机都崩——恢复需先在非安卓设备上确认待重投短信再清库。Pixel 9 与多款三星机型已复现。
3. **Deepgram acquires Daily, maker of the open-source voice-agent framework Pipecat**: deal size undisclosed; Pipecat stays open-source and vendor-neutral. Pipecat orchestrates voice/video streams with STT, LLMs and TTS at low latency; Daily had raised $62M+, Deepgram hit unicorn status earlier this year on a $130M Series C, and both were in the same 2016 YC batch. Deepgram is also launching Real-Time Context Standard (RCS), an open protocol for voice-agent context metadata co-developed with the Pipecat team. https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/deepgram-acquires-pipecat-maker-daily-to-bolster-its-voice-ai-infrastructure/
> [zh] **Deepgram 收购开源语音 Agent 框架 Pipecat 的开发商 Daily**：金额未披露；Pipecat 将继续开源且保持厂商中立。Pipecat 负责低延迟编排语音/视频流与 STT、LLM、TTS；Daily 累计融资超 6200 万美元，Deepgram 今年初以 1.3 亿美元 C 轮成为独角兽，两家同属 2016 年 YC 批次。Deepgram 还将推出与 Pipecat 团队共同开发的开放协议 Real-Time Context Standard（RCS），定义语音 agent 的上下文元数据。
4. **Forging a Microsoft Edge full chain with logic bugs only**: Orange Tsai (DEVCORE) documents his Pwn2Own Berlin 2026 winning chain — five bugs plus two Windows features, no memory corruption and no AI — for a sandbox escape that earned $175k and later a Pwnie Epic Achievement award; the write-up walks navigation/browsing internals and the handmade chaining process shared at HITCON and Hexacon. https://blog.orange.tw/posts/2026-10-the-last-proof-of-human-craft-browser-full-chain/ [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50285319)
> [zh] **只用逻辑漏洞打出 Microsoft Edge 完整利用链**：Orange Tsai（DEVCORE）公开其 Pwn2Own Berlin 2026 夺冠链条——五个漏洞加两个 Windows 特性，无内存破坏、无 AI——实现沙箱逃逸，奖金 17.5 万美元，后又获 Pwnie Epic Achievement；文章从导航/浏览内部讲起，并回顾在 HITCON、Hexacon 分享过的手工串链过程。
