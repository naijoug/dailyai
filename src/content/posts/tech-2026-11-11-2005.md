## 新产品
1. **Nod (n8n Labs)**: a standalone, open-source (Apache 2.0) personal agent from n8n Labs, released as a research preview for Mac and Windows or self-hosting. It has its own local sandbox VM and browser, works in connected apps like Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Slack and on sites with no integration (cancel a subscription, file monthly supplier invoices, watch prices), runs jobs in parallel and on schedules, and can be reached from Telegram or WhatsApp. A separate model check confirms before it sends, pays, posts or deletes anything you didn't ask for, saved logins and cards are entered through forms the model can't read, and every task shows its cost with a $5 check-in by default. You bring your own model provider (API key, ChatGPT plan or a local model); n8n reports 93.2% on LongMemEval. https://nod.n8n.io/ | https://blog.n8n.io/meet-nod/ | https://github.com/n8n-io/nod
> [zh] **Nod（n8n Labs）**：n8n Labs 推出的独立开源（Apache 2.0）个人 Agent，以研究预览形式发布，支持 Mac、Windows 或自托管。它有自己的本地沙箱虚拟机和浏览器，能在 Gmail、日历、Drive、Slack 等已连接应用里干活，也能操作没有集成的网站（退订、每月下载供应商发票、盯价格），多个任务并行、可定时，还能通过 Telegram 或 WhatsApp 对话。发邮件、付款、发帖、删除等你没明确要求的操作，会先由独立模型确认；保存的登录信息和银行卡通过模型看不到的表单填写；每个任务都显示花费，默认超过 5 美元会先问你。模型自带（API Key、ChatGPT 套餐或本地模型）；n8n 称其 LongMemEval 得分 93.2%。
2. **MiniMax H3-Image**: an open-weight image model built on H3 for text-to-image, reference-based generation (up to 16 reference images) and editing, generating native 4K (4096×4096) images in under 10 seconds on MiniMax's serving stack, up to 5× faster than mainstream alternatives by its own measure. Weights are on Hugging Face; it's also on the MiniMax API, MiniMax Code and Hailuo, priced at 50–70% of comparable offerings with 50% off 4K+ during launch week. https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3-image | https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3-Image
> [zh] **MiniMax H3-Image**：基于 H3 的开放权重生图模型，支持文生图、参考图生成（最多 16 张参考图）和图像编辑，在 MiniMax 自家推理服务上 10 秒内生成原生 4K（4096×4096）图片，官方称最高是主流方案的 5 倍速。权重已上 Hugging Face，也可通过 MiniMax API、MiniMax Code 和海螺使用，定价为同类的 50%–70%，首发周 4K 及以上五折。
3. **ElevenLabs Scribe v3 and v3 Live**: ElevenLabs' most accurate speech-to-text models; batch Scribe v3 cuts word error rate 27% versus Scribe v2 across 102 languages on its internal FLEURS benchmark, with better speaker diarization plus language hints, number formatting, keyterm prompting, entity detection and word-level timestamps, and v3 Live covers real-time transcription. https://elevenlabs.io/blog/scribe-v3
> [zh] **ElevenLabs Scribe v3 与 v3 Live**：ElevenLabs 目前最准的语音转文字模型；批量版 Scribe v3 在其内部 FLEURS 基准上覆盖 102 种语言，词错率比 Scribe v2 降低 27%，说话人分离更准，并支持语言提示、数字格式化、关键词提示、实体识别和逐词时间戳；v3 Live 负责实时转写。
4. **shadcn/improve**: an agent skill (`npx skills add shadcn/improve`) that uses your most capable model to audit a codebase and write self-contained implementation plans for cheaper models to execute; it never implements anything itself. Commands cover full or quick audits, security/perf-focused passes, branch-only review, plan critique, `/improve execute` (dispatches a cheaper executor in an isolated worktree and reviews the diff against the plan) and `/improve reconcile` to refresh the backlog. On GitHub Trending today with about 10.4k stars. https://github.com/shadcn/improve
> [zh] **shadcn/improve**：一个 Agent Skill（`npx skills add shadcn/improve`），用你最强的模型审计代码库，写出可独立执行的实施计划交给更便宜的模型去做，它自己从不动手实现。命令包括完整/快速审计、安全或性能专项、只审当前分支、评审计划、`/improve execute`（在隔离 worktree 里派更便宜的执行模型干活并对照计划审查 diff）和 `/improve reconcile` 清理待办。今日上 GitHub Trending，约 1.04 万星。

2. **Apple is reportedly building "Harbor," a Mac app that turns Macs into a private local-inference cluster**: leaker Aaron Perris found the app, currently in private preview under Apple's Claris brand: download and run open-weight models locally on Apple silicon with nothing reaching the cloud, and pool unified memory across two or more Thunderbolt 5 Macs that it auto-detects into a cluster for bigger models or more concurrency. HN commenters (295 points) were glad to see the Claris name return, while others noted running models headless on Macs is still painful and asked why local inference matters when hosted models are cheap. https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/10/apple-has-new-mac-app-coming-to-make-running-local-ai-models-easy/ | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50446520
> [zh] **据报苹果在做「Harbor」：把 Mac 变成私有本地推理集群的 App**：爆料者 Aaron Perris 发现了这款 App，目前以苹果旗下 Claris 品牌私测：在 Apple 芯片上本地下载并运行开放权重模型，数据不出机器；通过 Thunderbolt 5 自动发现两台以上的 Mac 并合并统一内存组成集群，跑更大的模型或承载更多并发。HN（295 分）评论里有人感慨 Claris 这个牌子回来了，也有人指出在 Mac 上无界面跑模型依然很折腾，还有人质疑云端模型这么便宜何必本地跑。
3. **Open Source Says No tracks OSS projects that ban AI-generated contributions**: the new catalog lists 22 projects with verified bans, including immich ("We ask you not to open PRs generated with an LLM"), Meilisearch (no generative-AI content in code, tests, docs, PR descriptions or issue comments), OkHttp and Servo, plus a broader index of AI contribution policies; anyone can submit a repo for an automated policy check. HN commenters (77 comments) noted most listed projects are only "restricted" rather than banned, that immich itself has an agents file and Claude co-authored commits, and that bans may just push contributors to hide AI use instead of taking responsibility for it. https://osssaysno.com | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50451102
> [zh] **Open Source Says No 汇总禁止 AI 生成贡献的开源项目**：这个新目录列出 22 个已核实禁止的项目，包括 immich（「请不要提交 LLM 生成的 PR」）、Meilisearch（代码、测试、文档、PR 描述和 issue 评论都不接受生成式 AI 内容）、OkHttp 和 Servo，另有更广的 AI 贡献政策索引；任何人都能提交仓库触发自动政策检查。HN（77 条评论）指出目录里多数项目只是「限制」而非禁止，immich 仓库本身就有 agents 文件和 Claude 共同署名的提交，还有人认为禁令只会让贡献者隐瞒 AI 使用，不如让贡献者自己对代码负责。
