## 新产品
1. **Turbo-Browser-MCP**: a Playwright MCP server (MIT, Node >= 20, playwright-core only) that batches browser actions so agents do not need one MCP call per click or keystroke. The author said he built it after multi-step browser agents hit three problems: full DOM/accessibility snapshots filling the context past 90k tokens, 10+ sequential turns per form (and freezes when agents passed uploads as Base64 tool arguments), and accidental clicks on Publish or Submit. It exposes 10 consolidated tools, including `inspect_compact` (main frame, iframes and open Shadow DOM, default cap 4000 characters, with `onlyChanged` deltas), `smart_fill_form` (React controlled inputs, contenteditable, selects, sliders, ARIA comboboxes and disk file uploads in one turn), and an `allowSubmit=false` guard by default. On the included headless Chromium benchmark (`tiktoken o200k_base`), the author reported a 140-button SaaS toolbar falling from 3,568 Playwright ARIA tokens to 1,135 compact tokens, or 56 tokens with `onlyChanged` (98.4% smaller than ARIA); a 10-field form plus snapshot going from 11 turns (574 ms, 12,197 direct / 72,614 cumulative replay tokens) to 1 turn (82 ms, 811 tokens); and a 48 KiB PDF upload falling from 44,728 tokens (inline Base64) to 85 tokens via a disk path. A Show HN post in this window had 39 points and 8 comments: the author posted the benchmarks there; commenters discussed tool design versus driving the DOM, and one asked about bot-detection cases that fail under remote control. The GitHub repo had 72 stars and 4 forks at fetch, was created 14 Nov, and has no separate marketing site. https://github.com/Tthanh2k6/turbo-browser-mcp | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50503930
> [zh] **Turbo-Browser-MCP**：一个 Playwright MCP 服务器（MIT 许可，需要 Node 20 及以上，只用 playwright-core），把浏览器操作打成批次，这样 Agent 就不必每次点击或按键都单独发一次 MCP 调用。作者说，他是在用 MCP 跑多步浏览器 Agent 时碰到三个实际问题后做的：完整 DOM/无障碍快照把上下文撑到超过 9 万 token、一张表单要连续 10 多轮（Agent 还把上传文件当成 Base64 工具参数时会卡住），以及偶尔误点 Publish 或 Submit。它提供 10 个合并后的工具，其中包括 `inspect_compact`（遍历主框架、iframe 和打开的 Shadow DOM，默认上限 4000 个字符，并支持 `onlyChanged` 增量）、`smart_fill_form`（一轮填完 React 受控输入、contenteditable、下拉、滑块、ARIA 组合框以及磁盘文件上传），以及默认关闭的 `allowSubmit=false` 提交保护。在附带的无头 Chromium 基准（`tiktoken o200k_base`）里，作者给出的数字包括：一个有 140 个按钮的 SaaS 工具栏，从 Playwright ARIA 的 3568 个 token 降到紧凑快照的 1135 个，若用 `onlyChanged` 则是 56 个（比 ARIA 小 98.4%）；一张 10 字段表单加快照，从 11 轮（574 毫秒，直接 12,197、累计回放 72,614 个 token）变成 1 轮（82 毫秒、811 个 token）；一份 48 KiB 的 PDF，内联 Base64 要用 44,728 个 token，改走磁盘路径则是 85 个。本窗口内的 Show HN 有 39 分、8 条评论：作者在帖里贴了基准；评论讨论了工具设计相对直接操作 DOM 的做法，也有人问到远程控制会触发机器人检测的情况。抓取时 GitHub 仓库有 72 个 star、4 个 fork，创建于 11 月 14 日，没有单独的产品官网。

1. **Australia’s central bank finds AI hitting young workers first, especially in software and customer service**: THE DECODER reported (15 Nov, 10:45 UTC) on an analysis by Mike Major at the Reserve Bank of Australia. The study built a monitoring system focused on where effects are likely to appear first: entry-level workers and highly AI-exposed occupations such as programming, clerical work and customer service. Job postings have fallen across groups since ChatGPT launched in late 2022, with the steepest drop in the highest-exposure group; in that group, workers aged 15 to 24 and young college graduates have weaker employment rates and higher unemployment than older workers. Major cautions that the study does not establish direct causation and measures theoretical exposure, not actual company use; post-pandemic business-cycle or remote-work shifts could also be driving the numbers. SEEK data are cited as showing AI both complementing existing work and creating demand for AI skills. The piece also recaps an August 2025 Stanford payroll study (employment among 22- to 25-year-olds in AI-exposed occupations down 13% in relative terms since late 2022, and nearly 20% among young software developers) and a March 2026 Anthropic study (job-finding rates for 22- to 25-year-olds in exposed occupations down about 14%, with no systematic rise in overall unemployment). As a company-level example, Anthropic’s internal review is cited: the share of junior hires in technical roles fell from 27% to 9% since early 2025, and by August 2026 AI agents’ cumulative task time was six times that of human employees; Anthropic itself said this is specific to a highly AI-dependent software firm and should not be read as the whole labor market.
