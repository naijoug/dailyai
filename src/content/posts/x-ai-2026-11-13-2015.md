2. **Liquid AI 开源 LiquidCodec：约 7400 万参数的流式语音编解码器，单核 CPU 编码 361 倍实时**：Gorden Sun 介绍，它是 LFM3-Audio-2B 和 LFM3-Omni-2B 背后的语音分词器，把 24kHz 语音压成每 80 毫秒 12 个 token，各码本之间没有顺序依赖，语音模型可以并行生成一帧的全部 token。官方称在 M4 Max 单核 CPU 上编码、解码分别约为实时的 361 倍和 87 倍，解码器 4 比特量化后体积缩小 3.5 倍、音质几乎不降；权重在 Hugging Face，许可为 LFM Open License（年营收 1000 万美元以下可商用）。https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2121140579572838573 | https://www.liquid.ai/blog/liquidcodec | https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/Liquid-Codec
> [en] **Liquid AI open-sources LiquidCodec, a ~74M-parameter streaming speech codec that encodes at 361x real time on one CPU core**: per Gorden Sun, it is the speech tokenizer behind LFM3-Audio-2B and LFM3-Omni-2B, turning 24 kHz speech into 12 tokens per 80 ms with no ordering dependency between codebooks, so a speech model can predict a whole frame in parallel. Liquid AI reports about 361x real-time encoding and 87x decoding on a single M4 Max CPU thread, and a 3.5x smaller decoder at 4-bit with almost no quality loss; weights are on Hugging Face under the LFM Open License (commercial use for organizations under $10M revenue).
3. **UniMate：一句英文描述就能驱动任意骨骼结构的 3D 动画**：Gorden Sun 推荐的这个模型不限于固定人体骨架，四足动物、人形角色和自定义骨骼的物体都能用同一个模型生成动作；每次输出 2 秒流畅的基础动作，可以往后续接，适合 3D 美术和游戏开发快速出角色动态、少手动 K 帧。https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2121187031522873414 | https://huggingface.co/Linzhan/UniMate
> [en] **UniMate: drive 3D animation for any skeleton from one English sentence**: the model Gorden Sun highlights is not tied to a fixed human rig; quadrupeds, humanoids and objects with custom skeletons all get motion from the same model. Each pass outputs 2 seconds of smooth base motion that can be extended, aimed at 3D artists and game developers who want varied character motion with less hand keyframing.

2. **smolmaxxing：让只有 1GB 可用内存的旧电视盒子和旧 iPad 本地跑大模型**：Gorden Sun 分享的开源项目基于 llama.cpp，针对老芯片重写底层计算，删掉模型里很少用到的词表项省空间，把暂时用不到的权重放在存储里按需读、并预读下一步要用的数据。作者实测第三代小米电视盒子 S 跑 2B 的 Qwen3.5 约 7.7 token/s、16B 的 Ling-mini-2.0 约 5.1 token/s；2013 年的初代 iPad Air 跑 1.7B 的 Bonsai 约 24–26 token/s。https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2121083287833870832 | https://github.com/hyperionaidev/smolmaxxing
> [en] **smolmaxxing: run LLMs locally on an old TV box and an old iPad with just 1GB of usable memory**: the open-source project Gorden Sun shared builds on llama.cpp, rewrites low-level compute for old chips, prunes rarely used vocabulary to save space, and keeps idle weights on storage, reading them on demand and prefetching what comes next. The author measured about 7.7 tokens/s for 2B Qwen3.5 and 5.1 tokens/s for 16B Ling-mini-2.0 on a 3rd-gen Xiaomi Mi Box S, and 24–26 tokens/s for 1.7B Bonsai on a 2013 first-gen iPad Air.
