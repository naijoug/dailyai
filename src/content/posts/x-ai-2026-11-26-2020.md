## 有趣用法和干货
1. **Recursive Collective Intelligence: when a team of agents actually beats spending the same tokens on independent tries**: a paper from Lixuan He with Li Fei-Fei, Yejin Choi, Leonidas Guibas and others grades agent groups on an “equal-budget ladder”: beat a single agent, beat a compute-matched majority vote, then beat compute-matched pass@n. Their findings: interaction helps when it moves information, since forwarding split facts lifts groups above pass@n at three model scales; identical 27B agents spontaneously form hubs (+18 points), and writing that structure into the prompt reaches level 3 with under half the tokens; and label-free generations trained on the group’s own output keep improving on PlanBench, HiddenBench and Hanabi for four to six generations. The practical takeaway: debate is not the point, information routing is. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32606 https://x.com/Chinazhidx/status/2125916608573149370
> [zh] **递归集体智能：多 Agent 协作什么时候真的比把同样 token 花在独立尝试上更强**：Lixuan He 与李飞飞、Yejin Choi、Leonidas Guibas 等人的论文用「等预算阶梯」给 Agent 团队打分：先超过单个 Agent，再超过算力相同的多数投票，最后超过算力相同的 pass@n。结论：交互有用的前提是传递信息，把分散在各 Agent 手里的事实转发出去，三种模型规模下都能超过 pass@n；相同的 27B Agent 会自发形成中心节点（准确率 +18 个百分点），把这种结构写进提示词后，不到一半 token 就能达到第三级；用团队自己的产出、无标签训练下一代，在 PlanBench、HiddenBench 和 Hanabi 上能连续进步四到六代。实用结论：重点不是辩论，而是信息路由。
2. **Terence Tao on reading a hard proof with AI: shrink it first, don’t one-shot it**: in a clip from a SAIR talk shared by Rohan Paul, Tao says that with a complicated argument he “would not just one-shot the proof. That doesn't teach you much of anything.” Instead, if a result holds in all dimensions, ask the AI to simplify the argument to the one-dimensional case and strip everything that only matters in high dimensions, then understand that shorter document first; much of the real work should still happen “at a whiteboard or blackboard with pen and paper”, with AI used for cleanup and nasty computations, and “knowing when not to use them is part of the challenge.” https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2125870382301495626
> [zh] **陶哲轩谈用 AI 读难证明：先把它变简单，别一把梭**：Rohan Paul 转发的 SAIR 演讲片段里，陶哲轩说面对复杂论证，他「不会让 AI 一次性给出证明，那样学不到什么」。更好的做法是：如果一个结论对所有维度都成立，就让 AI 把论证简化到一维情形，删掉只跟高维有关的部分，先读懂这份更短的文档；大部分真正的工作仍应「在白板或黑板上用纸笔」完成，AI 用来整理和算那些你不关心正负号细节的繁琐计算，而「知道什么时候不用它们也是挑战的一部分」。
3. **James Zhu: is it still worth building benchmarks when their half-life is 3.8 months?**: the former Kimi researcher and BenchLastExam founder analysed 86 benchmarks from 2020–2026 and found the time for models to eat half a benchmark’s headroom fell from 15.0 months to 3.8 months in 2026, and the average time to hit a 75% saturation ceiling is now 8.2 months, while building a solid benchmark takes 6 to 12 months. His three tests for a benchmark worth building: frontier (hard for current models, grounded in real high-value tasks), distribution (the task mix matches what you claim to measure), and correctness (solvable tasks, clean environments, no false positives or negatives in grading). https://x.com/JamesZhu1102/status/2125068120759185645 https://x.com/Chinazhidx/status/2125890762395259151
> [zh] **James Zhu：基准半衰期只剩 3.8 个月，还值不值得造 benchmark？**：这位前 Kimi 研究员、BenchLastExam 创始人分析了 2020–2026 年的 86 个基准，发现模型吃掉一半提升空间所需的时间从 15.0 个月降到 2026 年的 3.8 个月，平均 8.2 个月就会触到 75% 的饱和线，而做好一个扎实的基准要 6 到 12 个月。他给出值得做的三条标准：前沿性（对当前模型足够难、基于真实高价值任务）、分布（任务构成与你声称要测的东西一致）、正确性（题目可解、环境干净、评分不出现假阳性或假阴性）。

> [en] **Tiechuiren (lxfater): five open-source desktop tools for coding agents that all work with Claude Code and Codex subscriptions**: picked by pain point: Orca (139k stars, MIT) for desktop plus mobile with lossless switching between chat and terminal; Herdr (53k, Apache-2.0), a lightweight pure-Rust background process so closing a terminal doesn’t kill your agent; Paseo (25.6k) for privacy, with no telemetry, no forced login and a separate directory per agent; Happy (24.8k, MIT) to follow progress encrypted from your phone and pull teammates into the same session; and Paradev (16.7k, formerly Superset) to review diffs and preview in a built-in browser before opening a PR.
