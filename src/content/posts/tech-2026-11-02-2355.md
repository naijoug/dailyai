## 科技新闻
1. **Anthropic’s amended S-1: ~$17B Q3 revenue, growth slowing quarter-on-quarter**: nine-month revenue to Sept 30 was $33.24B (up ~1,200% YoY) with a $104B net loss mostly from fair-value changes on convertible notes and derivatives; Q3 revenue rose ~47% QoQ versus 144% in Q2 and 132% in Q1, with a $23.29B Q3 net loss, as the company prepares its IPO roadshow. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/02/anthropic-ant-s-1-quarter-financials.html [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50337439)
> [zh] **Anthropic 补充 S-1：三季度营收约 170 亿美元，环比增速放缓**：截至 9 月 30 日的九个月营收 332.4 亿美元（同比约 +1,200%），净亏损 1,040 亿美元，主要来自可转债与衍生负债的公允价值变动；三季度营收环比约 +47%，二季度为 144%、一季度为 132%，三季度净亏损 232.9 亿美元；公司正准备 IPO 路演。
2. **China’s MOFCOM Announcement No. 48 proposes export controls on frontier AI**: 40 technologies would join the restricted/prohibited export catalogue, 23 of them AI; model weights, checkpoints, source code and training/inference know-how are restricted if a model hits ≥10^26 training FLOPs, ≥2T parameters, or a global top-10 ranking; agent planning, multi-agent coordination and tool use are controlled separately above set benchmarks. The catalogue is a draft open for comment until Nov 15, while a pre-export registration requirement already applies. https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-china-ai-export-controls-announcement-48-2/ [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50334038)
> [zh] **中国商务部 2026 年第 48 号公告拟对前沿 AI 实施出口管制**：拟新增 40 项禁止或限制出口技术，其中 23 项属 AI；模型若满足训练算力 ≥10^26 FLOPs、参数 ≥2 万亿、或全球排名前十任一条件，其权重、检查点、源码与训练 / 推理优化技术即受限；Agent 规划、多智能体协同、工具调用等超过设定基准也单独受控。目录部分为征求意见稿（至 11 月 15 日），出口前登记要求已生效。
3. **Supermarket fridges and freezers in seven European countries hit by suspected cyber-attack**: chains in Belgium, Estonia, Ireland, Finland, Luxembourg, the Netherlands and Sweden reported cooling units heating to as high as 50°C overnight, echoing last month’s French attack on up to 700 stores; the EU food commissioner called it “a deliberate attempt to disrupt our food supply chain.” https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/02/supermarket-freezers-across-europe-hit-by-second-suspected-cyber-attack [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50335615)
> [zh] **欧洲七国超市冷柜疑遭网络攻击**：比利时、爱沙尼亚、爱尔兰、芬兰、卢森堡、荷兰、瑞典的连锁超市称冷柜夜间升温至最高 50°C，与上月法国多达 700 家门店遇袭类似；欧盟食品专员称这是「蓄意扰乱食品供应链」。
