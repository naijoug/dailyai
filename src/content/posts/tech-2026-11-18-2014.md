## 新产品
1. **Unsiloed AI**: turns complex enterprise documents (tables, charts, tricky layouts) into structured data AI agents can use, and shows where each extracted value came from in the source so businesses can check the output; it just raised a $7.5M seed led by Felicis with Y Combinator participating (TechCrunch). CEO Aman Mishra says it uses “dual-stream” vision models that process text tokens and image tokens separately, heat map-based chunking that spends more compute on figures, numbers and charts, and a domain-aware decoder. Customers include an unnamed bank, a law firm that runs it on its own servers for contracts and old case files, and voice-agent startup Bolna AI; competitors include Reducto, Unstructured, Microsoft and Amazon. https://www.unsiloed.ai/
> [zh] **Unsiloed AI**：把表格、图表和复杂版式的企业文档转成 AI Agent 能用的结构化数据，并标出每个抽取值在原文中的出处，方便企业核对；刚完成 Felicis 领投、Y Combinator 参投的 750 万美元种子轮（TechCrunch）。CEO Aman Mishra 称它用「双流」视觉模型分别处理文本 token 和图像 token，用热力图分块把更多算力花在图表、数字上，并配有懂行业术语的解码器。客户包括一家未具名银行、一家在自有服务器上处理合同和旧案卷的律所，以及语音 Agent 创业公司 Bolna AI；竞争对手有 Reducto、Unstructured、微软和亚马逊。
2. **Suno Visuals**: Suno added image and video generation on web, iOS and Android: pick up to 15 seconds of a song and a reference image (a Suno character preset, your own upload, or a prompt-generated image) to make a clip that can auto-lipsync to the vocals, with a timeline to stitch clips and add lyric text, plus images for thumbnails and album art (The Verge). It runs on “H3 Move,” based on MiniMax’s H3, with filters meant to block the likeness of artists, public figures and copyrighted IP, and uses separate “Visuals credits.” Some users under the launch video asked Suno to fix audio first. https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1036415/suno-visuals-ai-generated-video-images-feature
> [zh] **Suno Visuals**：Suno 在网页、iOS 和安卓上新增图像和视频生成：选一首歌里最多 15 秒的片段和一张参考图（Suno 预设角色、自己上传或用提示词生成），生成可自动对口型的视频片段，并提供时间线拼接片段、加歌词字幕，还能生成封面和专辑图（The Verge）。它由基于 MiniMax H3 的「H3 Move」模型驱动，配有过滤器，用于阻止生成特定艺人、公众人物和受版权保护 IP 的形象，并使用单独的「Visuals 积分」。发布视频下有用户呼吁先把音频修好。
3. **FastFrames**: Apple researchers released autoregressive video diffusion models that generate 480p video at interactive latency, with code and weights. Profiling showed operations usually treated as fixed details, such as VAE decoding and KV cache updates, ate over half of end-to-end latency, so they swapped in lightweight alternatives. The 1.3B model runs at 36 ms per frame on one B200 while matching a state-of-the-art model nearly 8x slower; the 14B model hits 45 ms per frame on an 8x B200 node with better quality, and an optimized LingBot 1.3B gets a 16x speedup for camera-controlled generation (trending on Hugging Face Papers). https://apple-aiml-research.github.io/ml-fastframes/
> [zh] **FastFrames**：苹果研究人员发布一组自回归视频扩散模型，能以交互级延迟生成 480p 视频，并公开代码和权重。他们剖析推理流程后发现，VAE 解码、KV 缓存更新这类通常被当作固定实现细节的操作，占了端到端延迟的一半以上，于是换成轻量替代方案。1.3B 版本在单张 B200 上每帧 36 毫秒，质量与慢近 8 倍的最先进模型相当；14B 版本在 8 卡 B200 节点上每帧 45 毫秒，画质更好；经它优化的 LingBot 1.3B 在镜头控制生成上提速 16 倍（Hugging Face Papers 热门）。
4. **mitmAny：让 AI 通过 MCP 分析真实抓包流量**：V2EX 分享创造区开源的桌面抓包工具，内置 MCP 服务，在网站或 App 上操作并捕获请求后，可让 Codex 等 MCP 客户端读取真实请求与响应，分析接口、登录和分页流程（例如让它解释一组请求的分页逻辑、引用请求编号并标出不确定的推断）。支持 Windows、macOS 和安卓 USB 抓包，带 JSON 查看、HAR 导入导出和 cURL 导出，MIT 开源、全部免费。作者说明 MCP 只读取已捕获流量，不重放请求、不绕过证书固定，安装包暂未签名。https://github.com/wonderfulsuccess/mitmAny
> [en] **mitmAny: let AI analyze real captured traffic over MCP**: an open-source desktop capture tool shared on V2EX with a built-in MCP server. After you use a site or app and capture its requests, MCP clients like Codex can read the real requests and responses to work out APIs, login and pagination flows (e.g. explain a set of requests’ pagination logic, cite request IDs and flag uncertain inferences). It supports Windows, macOS and Android USB capture, with a JSON viewer, HAR import/export and cURL export, MIT-licensed and fully free. The author notes the MCP only reads captured traffic, doesn’t replay requests or bypass certificate pinning, and the installers aren’t signed yet.
5. **江山时序：中国历史交互地图时间轴**：GitHub 今日热门的开源项目，把战国（前 403 年）到今天、18 个历史时期的疆域放进一张可拖动时间轴的羊皮纸风地图，含 198 个疆域快照、古城与关隘的古今地名沿革和诗词路线。拖动时间轴能看各政权此消彼长、当年在位君主和年号，悬停可查古代州郡并叠加现代省界，黄河改道、古湖泊、长城和运河也随时间变化，还支持语音朗读当年大事。https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map
> [en] **Chronicles of the Realm (jiangshan-map): an interactive timeline atlas of Chinese history**: an open-source project trending on GitHub that puts 18 periods from the Warring States (403 BCE) to today on one parchment-style map with a draggable timeline, 198 territory snapshots, ancient-to-modern place-name genealogies for cities and passes, and classical poetry routes. Scrubbing shows realms rising and falling plus the reigning monarch and era name; hovering reveals ancient prefectures with a modern-province overlay, Yellow River shifts, ancient lakes, Great Walls and canals change over time, and it can read each year’s events aloud.

## 科技新闻
1. **Microsoft’s agent-framework PyPI package was poisoned with a credential stealer, and ONNX Runtime’s repo vanished from GitHub**: versions 1.26.1 and 1.26.2 of agent-framework, the meta-package for Microsoft Agent Framework (about 190,000 downloads a month), were published 33 minutes apart on Nov 17 and drop a .pth startup hook that runs on every Python start, even without an import. It harvests env vars matching TOKEN/SECRET/KEY/AZURE/OPENAI/HF_ and similar, other processes’ environments, credential files and Azure managed-identity tokens (1.26.2 adds GitHub Actions OIDC), then posts them to a webhook.site endpoint; both are quarantined, 1.26.0 is the last clean release, and anyone who installed them should rotate every reachable credential (GitHub advisory GHSA-764m-3ppc-5m73). On HN, microsoft/onnxruntime and microsoft/agent-framework both went offline the same day (354 points); with no official word, commenters split between a compromised admin account and Ignite-week repo consolidation, and others pointed out ONNX Runtime’s telemetry can be disabled with ORT_DISABLE_TELEMETRY=1 (HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50539610). https://socket.dev/blog/agent-framework-pypi-package-compromised-with-credential-stealer
> [zh] **微软 agent-framework 的 PyPI 包被植入凭据窃取程序，ONNX Runtime 仓库也从 GitHub 消失**：Microsoft Agent Framework 的元包 agent-framework（月下载约 19 万）在 11 月 17 日 33 分钟内先后发布了 1.26.1 和 1.26.2，两者都会安装 .pth 启动钩子，每次启动 Python 都会运行，哪怕没有 import。它会收集名称含 TOKEN/SECRET/KEY/AZURE/OPENAI/HF_ 等的环境变量、其他进程的环境、凭据文件和 Azure 托管身份令牌（1.26.2 还加上 GitHub Actions OIDC），再发到一个 webhook.site 地址；两版都已被隔离，1.26.0 是最后的干净版本，装过的人应轮换所有可触及的凭据（GitHub 公告 GHSA-764m-3ppc-5m73）。HN 上，microsoft/onnxruntime 和 microsoft/agent-framework 同一天都下线了（354 分）；官方尚未说明，评论区有人怀疑管理员账号被盗，也有人认为是 Ignite 期间合并仓库，还有人提醒 ONNX Runtime 的遥测可用 ORT_DISABLE_TELEMETRY=1 关闭。
2. **An experiment finds Google’s AI Overviews cut clicks to websites by about 40%**: RevScope cites a randomized test that hid AI Overviews from some US desktop users and found an Overview cuts clicks to outside sites by about 40%; in DataForSEO’s 20-million-keyword panel, results with an Overview rose from 5.93% in Sept 2024 to 61.43% in Aug 2026, while featured snippets fell to 0.44% and forum blocks to 7.42%, and GEO hasn’t shown a way to make up the loss (HN, 459 points: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50540178). The same day Nicolas Bouliane wrote that All About Berlin, the guide site he has lived off since 2020, lost 70–75% of its traffic to AI Overviews and he’s preparing for another way to pay rent (HN, 429 points: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50542705). https://revscope.co/blog/search-seo-geo-2026/
> [zh] **实验显示谷歌 AI 摘要让网站外链点击减少约四成**：RevScope 引用一项随机实验：对部分美国桌面用户隐藏 AI Overviews，对比发现出现摘要时外部网站点击减少约 40%；在 DataForSEO 的 2000 万关键词样本中，带 AI 摘要的结果占比从 2024 年 9 月的 5.93% 升到 2026 年 8 月的 61.43%，精选摘要跌到 0.44%，论坛讨论区块跌到 7.42%，GEO 也还没找到弥补损失的办法（HN 459 分）。同一天，Nicolas Bouliane 写道，他自 2020 年起靠它谋生的柏林指南站 All About Berlin 因 AI 摘要流失了 70%–75% 的流量，他已在为换个方式交房租做准备（HN 429 分）。
3. **KPMG ran a parallel audit of a FTSE 250 company with 100+ AI agents**: per the FT via Business Standard, “Project Exponential” used agents across the whole audit rather than single tasks: they collected and analysed data, documented it, drafted conclusions and suggested next steps while working with one another, and in some cases asked better questions and dug deeper than the human team. A person still had to step in when an agent failed to match regulations to company documents, and KPMG didn’t rely on the findings to approve the accounts; it plans to extend the trial to as many as 20 larger audits and eventually sell AI-based audits, without saying whether fees would fall. https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/kpmg-finds-ai-agents-ask-better-questions-than-human-auditors-in-trial-126111800471_1.html
> [zh] **KPMG 用 100 多个 AI Agent 平行审计一家富时 250 公司**：据 Business Standard 转述《金融时报》，这个代号「Project Exponential」的试验让 Agent 覆盖整个审计流程而不是单项任务：收集、分析和记录数据，起草结论并相互协作提出后续步骤，有时比人类团队问得更好、挖得更深。不过有一次 Agent 没能把法规和公司文件对上，仍需审计师介入，KPMG 也没有依据这次结果批准账目；它计划把试验扩大到多达 20 个更大的审计项目，最终向客户提供 AI 审计，但没说审计费会不会降。
4. **Energy-Based Reasoners: looped reasoners that know when to stop**: the paper learns an energy function over latent reasoning states and turns looped updates into gradient descent on it, so the energy scores a state, its gradient gives the direction to improve, a small gradient says when to stop, and the lowest energy picks among candidate answers. On Sudoku-Extreme and graph tasks, EBRs give the best single-sample accuracy among the reasoning models compared, reach 99.9% and 88.3% within five passes, and stopping early per trajectory lets them beat EqR with 345x fewer FLOPs (HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50543989). https://energy-based-reasoners.github.io/
> [zh] **Energy-Based Reasoners：知道何时停下的循环推理模型**：论文在潜在推理状态上学习一个能量函数，把循环更新变成对它做梯度下降：能量给状态打分，梯度给出改进方向，梯度足够小就停止，多条候选答案中取能量最低者。在 Sudoku-Extreme 和图任务上，EBR 在参与对比的推理模型中单样本准确率最高，5 次推理内达到 99.9% 和 88.3%，按轨迹提前停止后以少 345 倍的 FLOPs 超过 EqR。
5. **Cloudflare: Quad9 leaving France is a warning about DNS-level blocking**: Quad9 is withdrawing its free public resolver from France after a court imposed daily penalties for not blocking domains tied to alleged copyright infringement; Cloudflare notes Cisco’s OpenDNS left France, Portugal and Belgium under similar orders, and a Belgian court now threatens penalties if Cloudflare, Google and Cisco don’t block too, arguing such demands harm users’ privacy, security and the open Internet (HN, 661 points: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50538994). https://blog.cloudflare.com/quad9-withdrawal-from-france/
> [zh] **Cloudflare：Quad9 退出法国，是 DNS 层封锁的警钟**：法国法院因 Quad9 未屏蔽涉嫌侵权的域名对其按日处罚，Quad9 决定撤出在法国的免费公共 DNS 解析服务；Cloudflare 指出思科 OpenDNS 此前因类似命令退出过法国、葡萄牙和比利时，比利时法院现在也要求 Cloudflare、谷歌和思科屏蔽，否则重罚，并认为这类要求会损害用户隐私、安全和开放互联网（HN 661 分）。
