## 有趣用法和干货
1. **Hightouch’s model-cascade case shows why calibration matters**: its November 4 identity-resolution study routes uncertain Jev decisions to Opus. On the easier synthetic test, the team reports 98.8% accuracy at $0.88 per 1,000 identities, versus Opus alone at 99.0% and $3.86. On harder cases the hybrid cost rises to $2.94 versus $5.39, so the headline “4x lower cost” is not universal. These are the authors’ task-specific results, not independent general-model rankings. https://hightouch.com/blog/llm-judges-identity-resolution
> [zh] **Hightouch 的模型分级案例说明置信度校准为何重要**：11 月 4 日的身份记录匹配研究把 Jev 不确定的判断交给 Opus。团队在较简单的合成测试中报告 98.8% 准确率、每千个身份 0.88 美元；单用 Opus 为 99.0%、3.86 美元。更难测试中混合方案成本升至 2.94 美元，单用 Opus 为 5.39 美元，因此标题中的“四倍降本”不能普遍套用。这是作者在特定任务上的结果，不是独立通用模型排名。

## 科技新闻
1. **OpenAI partners with Hacktron on system-boundary security testing**: the November 6 announcement says the teams will select high-priority targets, especially interactions with third-party services, and expand Hacktron’s frontier-model access through Daybreak Red. This is a scoped research partnership, not a general public release of those models or permission to test arbitrary systems. https://openai.com/index/partnering-with-hacktron/
> [zh] **OpenAI 与 Hacktron 合作测试系统交界处的安全问题**：11 月 6 日公告称双方将选择高优先级测试目标，特别关注与第三方服务的交互，并通过 Daybreak Red 扩大 Hacktron 的前沿模型访问。这是有范围的研究合作，不是相关模型全面公开，也不代表可以任意测试其他系统。
