## 有趣用法和干货
1. **卡兹克：最近两个月用 Agent 干活最爱说的 10 句提示词**：包括「从第一性原理出发找到最本质原因再修」「先问不清晰的问题对齐偏好，再出 3 版方案」「不要默认我的问题定义是对的，先判断有没有把症状当原因、把手段当目标」「推演改动的二阶、三阶影响」「跨领域找类似方案」「先假设当前最优方案是错的，站在最强反对者角度找反例」「分析 Git 历史和关键 PR，识别可以安全移除的历史包袱」「假设明天来一个不了解项目的强工程师，评估他的认知成本并提出简化」「找出最近最慢的 PR」「复盘近期 Bug、返工和回滚的共同根因，沉淀成项目规则、自动化或 Skill」。抓取时约 850 赞、890 收藏。https://x.com/Khazix0918/status/2122268349707219333
> [en] **Khazix: the 10 prompts he has used most with agents over the past two months**: they include “start from first principles, find the root cause, then fix it”; “ask clarifying questions to align on my preferences, then give 3 options”; “don’t assume my problem definition is right; check whether I mistook a symptom for the cause or a means for the goal”; “trace second- and third-order effects of the change”; “look for analogous solutions in other fields”; “assume your best plan is wrong and attack it as its strongest critic”; “study the Git history and key PRs to find legacy baggage that can be safely removed”; “imagine a strong engineer new to the project joins tomorrow, estimate their cognitive cost and propose simplifications”; “find the slowest recent PRs”; and “review recent bugs, rework and rollbacks for shared root causes and turn them into project rules, automation or Skills.” About 850 likes and 890 bookmarks at fetch.
2. **Matt Pocock: a brief history of AI coding since Nov 2025**: month by month, from Opus 4.5 and OpenClaw (Nov 2025), Ralph loops (Jan), cognitive-debt fatigue (Feb) and the Skills explosion (Mar), through “The unreasonable effectiveness of HTML” and richer review surfaces (May), “Loop Engineering” (Jun), every talk becoming “here is my software factory” (Jul), long-running “manager” threads (Sep) and unmet speed gains (Oct), to the No Slop manifesto (Nov). His TL;DR: “no-one is writing code anymore, and we are still figuring out what that means.” About 530 likes at fetch. https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2122287575557706226
> [zh] **Matt Pocock：2025 年 11 月以来的 AI 编程简史**：按月梳理：Opus 4.5 发布、OpenClaw 诞生（2025 年 11 月），Ralph 循环流行（1 月），对「认知债」的疲惫（2 月），Skills 爆发（3 月），《HTML 不讲理的有效性》带火更丰富的审查界面（5 月），「Loop Engineering」（6 月），每场大会都在讲「我的软件工厂」（7 月），长期运行的「经理」线程（9 月），承诺的提速没兑现（10 月），直到 No Slop 宣言（11 月）。他的总结是：「已经没人在写代码了，而我们还在弄清这意味着什么。」抓取时约 530 赞。
3. **Berryxia 开源豆仓 BeanPod：本地优先的 macOS 录音整理工具**：原本为 Anker 录音豆写的原生 macOS 应用，录完连上 Mac 自动下载、本机转写，整理成纪要和待办，录音不出电脑；没有录音豆也能自录、导入音频或贴小宇宙、Apple 播客、喜马拉雅链接。文字稿点一句跳回原音、可单句循环，「英语学习模式」会拆出分级单词、短语和难句；知识库可直接提问，并通过 MCP 让 Claude、Codex 调用录音内容；中英双语界面，MIT 协议。作者说明只在固件 04.92 上测过，得到录音卡支持尚未真机测试。https://x.com/berryxia/status/2122262173842042936
> [en] **Berryxia open-sources BeanPod, a local-first macOS recording organizer**: a native macOS app first written for Anker’s recorder; plug it into a Mac and it downloads recordings, transcribes them on-device and turns them into notes and to-dos without the audio leaving the computer. It also works without the device: record on the Mac, import audio, or paste Xiaoyuzhou, Apple Podcasts or Ximalaya links. Clicking a transcript line jumps back to the audio with single-sentence looping, an “English learning mode” extracts graded words, phrases and hard sentences, and the knowledge base can be queried directly or exposed to Claude and Codex over MCP; bilingual UI, MIT license. The author notes it was only tested on firmware 04.92 and support for the Dedao recorder has not been tested on real hardware.
