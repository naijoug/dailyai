2. **Mistral releases the open weights of Mistral Large 4 ("Le Chonk")**: a ~1T-parameter multimodal MoE with 1M context, shipped as FP8 and NVFP4 on Hugging Face with day-0 Transformers, vLLM and llama.cpp support; license is a modified MIT that requires Model-as-a-Service providers to get a separate commercial license. API price drops to $0.70 / $0.07 cached / $2.10 per M tokens, and Unsloth says its 1-bit quant keeps 91% of top-1 accuracy at 223 GB vs 1.1 TB. https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/ | https://huggingface.co/blog/mistral-large4 | https://x.com/danielhanchen/status/2115814087498305811
> [zh] **Mistral 开放 Mistral Large 4（「Le Chonk」）权重**：约 1T 参数的多模态 MoE，1M 上下文，Hugging Face 提供 FP8 和 NVFP4 两个版本，首日支持 Transformers、vLLM 和 llama.cpp；许可证为修改版 MIT，做 Model-as-a-Service 的企业需另购商业授权。API 降到每百万 token 输入 0.70 / 缓存 0.07 / 输出 2.10 美元；Unsloth 称其 1-bit 量化保留 91% 的 top-1 准确率，体积 223 GB（原版 1.1 TB）。
3. **宝玉解读 Anthropic「自我评估」防滥用机制：Claude 每答完一轮就自查这段对话是否安全**：系统在回答后追加一句反思指令，Claude 输出简短评估（危害类别、是否拒绝、1–10 审查优先级和一句理由）；利用现成 KV cache，整体推理算力增加不到 2%，不向用户收费；Fable 5 以来的模型都在跑，零数据保留流量不跑；单条标记不封号，同一组织攒多条高优先级标记才转人工。在 1% 误报率下覆盖强分类器标记的 78%、高优先级的 94%。https://safeguards.anthropic.com/2026/self-assessment/ | https://x.com/dotey/status/2115923359149998490
> [en] **Baoyu explains Anthropic's "self-assessment" misuse detection: Claude checks each conversation for safety right after it answers**: a short reflection instruction is appended after each reply and Claude outputs a compact assessment (harm category, whether it refused, a 1–10 review priority and a one-line reason); reusing the KV cache, it adds under 2% inference compute and is not billed to users; it runs on every model since Fable 5 but not on zero-data-retention traffic; a single flag does not mean a ban, only multiple high-priority flags per org go to human review. At a 1% false-positive rate it catches 78% of what a stronger classifier flags, and 94% of high-priority cases.

> [zh] **Google 将关停 Jules 并并入 Antigravity**：团队称会把 Jules 的经验带进 Antigravity，结合「自主远程后台任务执行和 agent 优先的开发环境」；同日 Antigravity 上线插件市场，提供 Jira、Confluence、Linear、Notion、Figma、Stripe、Vercel、Datadog 等连接器。
5. **OpenAI updates its Model Spec around five values**: faithfulness to human intent, harm minimization, honesty and transparency, human agency, and care; the revision renames the subject from "the assistant" to "GPT", adds guidance on context, memory, oversight and side effects, and removes most detailed style rules. https://openai.com/index/updated-model-spec | https://github.com/openai/model_spec/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
> [zh] **OpenAI 围绕五项价值重写 Model Spec**：忠于人类意图、尽量减少伤害、诚实透明、人的自主权、关怀；这一版把主语从「the assistant」改成「GPT」，新增关于上下文、记忆、人类监督和副作用的指导，并删掉大部分细节风格规定。
6. **Claude API raises max request size from 32 MB to 128 MB**: applies to Messages and Token Counting on the first-party API (cloud platforms stay at 20–32 MB), aimed at long computer-use sessions and big PDFs that hit 32 MB with context left; requests with more than 20 images now allow 3000 px per image instead of 2000 px. https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2115923971279614171 | https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2115923973188034637
> [zh] **Claude API 单次请求上限从 32 MB 提到 128 MB**：适用于官方 API 的 Messages 和 Token Counting（云平台仍是 20–32 MB），解决长时间 computer use 和大 PDF 在上下文还有余量时就撞上 32 MB 的问题；超过 20 张图的请求，单图上限从 2000 px 放宽到 3000 px。
