## 科技新闻
1. **Tailworm: researcher publishes 11 unpatched Tailscale / headscale flaws; Tailscale SSH unix-socket forwarding can yield root on another node**: Erica Windisch (Cyberdione Labs) reproduced findings on Tailscale v1.104.2 and headscale v0.29.5 (tested Oct 22–24), including High-severity issues where `ssh -L`/`-R` unix-socket forwarding dials or replaces sockets as root, TOCTOU races past path allowlists, serve-to-localapi write access, and conn25 egress privilege gaps; she recommends disabling Tailscale SSH and App Connectors for now. Tailscale says it was already reviewing the same areas and will share more; no bulletin yet. https://tailworm.com/ [pwn.watch](https://pwn.watch/i/tailscale-tailworm-ssh-forwarding-privesc)
> [zh] **Tailworm：研究者公开 11 个尚未修补的 Tailscale / headscale 漏洞；Tailscale SSH 的 unix socket 转发可在另一节点拿到 root**：Erica Windisch（Cyberdione Labs）在 Tailscale v1.104.2 与 headscale v0.29.5 上复现（测试窗口 10/22–24），高危项包括 `ssh -L`/`-R` unix socket 转发以 root 拨号或替换 socket、TOCTOU 绕过路径白名单、serve 打到 localapi 的写权限，以及 conn25 出口权限缺口等；她建议暂时关闭 Tailscale SSH 和 App Connectors。Tailscale 称已在审查相同区域并会补充说明，目前尚无安全公告。
