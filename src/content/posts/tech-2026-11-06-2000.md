## 科技新闻
1. **Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash adds 15-second video and reaches general availability**: Google’s November 5 announcement adds multi-turn editing and extends native clips from 10 to 15 seconds. The stated 720p price is $0.07 per second, 30% below Omni 1.1. Google says it is generally available through the Gemini API and Gemini for business; improved consistency is a vendor claim, and fast motion and complex physics remain limitations. https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/
> [zh] **Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash 支持 15 秒视频并正式开放**：Google 11 月 5 日公告新增多轮编辑，将原生片长从 10 秒延至 15 秒；720p 标价每秒 0.07 美元，比 Omni 1.1 低 30%。官方称已通过 Gemini API 和 Gemini for business 正式提供；一致性提升属厂商主张，快速运动和复杂物理仍有限制。
2. **Mistral Studio expands managed agents, workflows and enterprise apps**: the November 5 update adds agents with skills, sandboxes, subagents and hooks; resumable workflows gain event triggers and visual inspection. Developers can deploy through a new MCP server, inspect OpenTelemetry traces and set human approvals, with cloud, VPC and on-premises deployment options. These are platform features, not a new model announcement. https://mistral.ai/news/build-with-studio/
> [zh] **Mistral Studio 扩展托管 Agent、工作流和企业应用**：11 月 5 日更新让 Agent 配备 skills、沙箱、子 Agent 和 hooks；可恢复的工作流新增事件触发和可视化检查。开发者可经新 MCP 服务器部署、查看 OpenTelemetry 轨迹并设置人工审批，支持云、VPC 和本地部署。这是平台功能更新，并非新模型发布。
3. **Copilot enters Xcode’s built-in coding assistant in public preview**: GitHub’s November 4 announcement supports all Copilot plans starting with Xcode 27.3 beta on macOS Tahoe 26.6 or later. Setup lives under Xcode’s Intelligence settings; the existing Copilot for Xcode extension remains a separate option. https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-04-github-copilot-is-now-available-in-xcodes-coding-assistant/
> [zh] **Copilot 接入 Xcode 内置编程助手，进入公开预览**：GitHub 11 月 4 日公告称支持所有 Copilot 套餐，需 Xcode 27.3 beta 和 macOS Tahoe 26.6 或更新版本，在 Intelligence 设置中配置。既有 Copilot for Xcode 扩展仍是独立选项。
