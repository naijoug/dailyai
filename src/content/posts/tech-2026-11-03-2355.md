## 新产品
1. **Subvocal (YC F26): smart glasses that let you talk to your computer without making a sound**: antennas in the frame emit RF that passes through your face and is modulated by silent mouthing of the tongue, lips and soft palate; foundation models trained on that signal decode it to text at the speed of speech, and it can be tuned to mouthing with lips closed. The founders moved from EMG prototypes to RF after sweat, interference and placement problems. Show HN this window (~810 points / 380+ comments). https://subvocal.company/ [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50340088)
> [zh] **Subvocal（YC F26）：不出声也能和电脑说话的智能眼镜**：镜框内天线发出射频穿过面部，舌头、嘴唇和软腭的无声口型会调制信号，团队用这类数据训练基础模型，以说话的速度解码成文字，还能微调到闭唇默念。创始人起初用 EMG 电极，因汗水、干扰和贴放位置问题改走射频。本窗口 Show HN（约 810 分 / 380+ 评论）。
2. **AgentKit by Scalekit: an MCP gateway so production agents act as the user who asked**: 30,000+ tools across 600+ apps (Salesforce to GitHub) behind one gateway; per-tenant AES-256 token vault with auto-refresh so tokens never touch your code or prompts, every call runs on the end user’s own scopes, Virtual MCP Servers compose a small tool set per agent role, custom connectors for internal APIs, Triggers to start an agent when an email lands, and full call logs to Datadog/Splunk. Free tier: 10,000 tool calls/month. #1 on Product Hunt for Nov 3. https://www.scalekit.com/agentkit [PH](https://www.producthunt.com/products/scalekit)
> [zh] **Scalekit 的 AgentKit：让生产环境 Agent「以提问用户的身份」行动的 MCP 网关**：一个网关接入 600+ 应用的 3 万+ 工具（从 Salesforce 到 GitHub）；按租户隔离的 AES-256 令牌库自动刷新，令牌不进代码和提示词，每次调用都用终端用户自己授权的范围；Virtual MCP Server 按 Agent 角色组合小工具集，可把内部 API 做成自定义连接器，Triggers 在邮件到达时唤起 Agent，全部调用日志可推到 Datadog / Splunk。免费档每月 1 万次工具调用。Product Hunt 11/3 日榜第 1。
3. **MachineMail: real email inboxes for humans and AI agents**: agents receive and send mail over MCP or API, on your own domain, with a trust report on every inbound message; the free plan covers 3,000 emails a month (100/day) and one custom domain, paid plans start at $9/month. On Product Hunt this window. https://machinemail.dev [PH](https://www.producthunt.com/products/machinemail)
> [zh] **MachineMail：给人和 AI Agent 用的真实邮箱**：Agent 可通过 MCP 或 API 收发邮件，支持自有域名，每封来信附信任报告；免费档每月 3,000 封（每天上限 100）并支持一个自定义域名，付费 9 美元/月起。本窗口登陆 Product Hunt。
4. **One Roof: open-source “chorus coding” hub for many agents at once**: several sessions of Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Kimi and Antigravity each run in a real terminal, with one Inbox for every question they ask (the agent’s own options as buttons, nothing auto-approved), a morning page of what ran or broke overnight, Linear-backed projects and ~25 ready-made loops with limits and gates. MIT; macOS 14+ on Apple silicon. On Product Hunt this window. https://gaurav8936.github.io/oneroof/ [PH](https://www.producthunt.com/products/one-roof)
> [zh] **One Roof：同时调度多个 Agent 的开源「合唱编程」工作台**：Claude Code、Codex、Gemini CLI、Kimi、Antigravity 多个会话各跑在真实终端里；统一收件箱汇总它们的提问（以 Agent 自带选项为按钮，绝不代你批准），晨报页展示夜里跑了什么、坏了什么，项目可对接 Linear，内置约 25 个带限额和关卡的循环任务。MIT 协议，需 Apple 芯片 macOS 14+。本窗口登陆 Product Hunt。
5. **Wavefunction: a minimal, open-source coding agent desktop app for Mac**: deliberately not a super app—keep chatting while it runs several tasks in parallel (subagents show as separate, steerable sessions), bring your ChatGPT subscription, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter or a local model, and computer use runs on its own sandboxed desktop instead of yours; no built-in browser. Free. On Product Hunt this window. https://wavefunction.si [PH](https://www.producthunt.com/products/wavefunction)
> [zh] **Wavefunction：极简、开源的 Mac 编程 Agent 桌面应用**：刻意不做超级应用——它并行处理多个任务时你可继续对话（子 Agent 以独立会话呈现、可单独干预），可用 ChatGPT 订阅、Anthropic、Google、OpenRouter 或本地模型，电脑操作跑在独立沙箱桌面而非你的桌面；不内置浏览器。免费。本窗口登陆 Product Hunt。

## 科技新闻
1. **Google will switch off AI for K-12 students by default—but schools that restrict AI entirely lose Search**: starting next year, Gemini, NotebookLM, Gemini in Classroom and Search AI features are off unless administrators enable them; because “Search and AI are deeply integrated,” schools that block AI altogether will see Search replaced by a not-available message (~570 points on HN). https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/google-will-let-schools-disable-its-ai-tools-but-not-without-sacrificing-search/ [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50353192)
> [zh] **Google 明年起默认对 K-12 学生关闭 AI，但彻底禁 AI 的学校也将失去搜索**：Gemini、NotebookLM、Classroom 中的 Gemini 及搜索 AI 功能需管理员主动开启；由于「搜索与 AI 深度整合」，完全限制 AI 的学校，学生打开搜索只会看到不可用提示（HN 约 570 分）。
2. **Ask HN: OpenAI seems to be silently re-enabling model training on Codex chats**: a team that had disabled data sharing found “Improve the model for everyone” switched back on in Codex without anyone opting in; the poster links an earlier similar report and the privacy portal as a second opt-out path—worth re-checking chatgpt.com/settings/data-controls (~450 points). [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50352757)
> [zh] **Ask HN：OpenAI 似乎在悄悄重新开启 Codex 对话的训练授权**：一家关闭了数据共享的团队发现，Codex 的「为所有人改进模型」被重新打开，没人主动同意；发帖人附上此前类似报告以及隐私门户的第二个退出入口——建议到 chatgpt.com/settings/data-controls 复查（约 450 分）。
3. **Fal doubles its valuation to $16B as inference demand surges**: the AI video/image/audio model host raised $1.3B in a Series F led by Bessemer with Nvidia, Sound Ventures and T. Rowe Price ($1.65B across Series E and F), up from $8B in April; it passed $800M annualized revenue earlier this year, though inference providers’ gross margins stay thin on compute costs. https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/ai-inference-startup-fal-doubles-its-valuation-to-16b-as-demand-surges/
> [zh] **推理需求暴涨，Fal 估值翻倍至 160 亿美元**：这家托管 AI 视频 / 图像 / 音频模型的公司完成 13 亿美元 F 轮，由 Bessemer 领投，Nvidia、Sound Ventures、T. Rowe Price 参投（E、F 两轮合计 16.5 亿），4 月估值为 80 亿；今年早些时候年化收入超 8 亿美元，但推理厂商因算力成本毛利仍薄。
4. **CrowdStrike to pay ~$500M for two AI security startups as securing agents takes off**: it is buying Fiddler AI (~$400M), which monitors AI agents inside enterprises, and Dreadnode (~$95M), which automates security testing of AI models, apps and agents before launch, per the WSJ. https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/crowdstrike-to-shell-out-nearly-500m-for-2-ai-security-startups-as-the-market-for-securing-agents-takes-off/
> [zh] **CrowdStrike 约 5 亿美元收购两家 AI 安全初创，Agent 安全市场升温**：据《华尔街日报》，以约 4 亿美元收购监控企业内 AI Agent 的 Fiddler AI，约 9,500 万美元收购在上线前自动化测试 AI 模型、应用和 Agent 安全的 Dreadnode。
