## AI 热点
1. **DeepMind’s AI-assisted cryptanalysis targets legacy RSA, not a break of modern encryption**: Pushmeet Kohli’s November 7 post at 01:07 Beijing links two working papers. One reproduces a known 1024-bit modular-root experiment using 1/3.5 of the CPU time; the other gives a leading-order estimate of roughly 30× lower per-key cost than MNFS for additional 1024-bit integers after precomputation costing about one factorization, under standard number-field-sieve heuristics. The authors explicitly say these results do not threaten deployed RSA with 2048-bit or larger keys. https://x.com/pushmeet/status/2118752095859794390 | [Modular-root paper](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/papers/Faster_Affine_Root_and_a_1024bit%20Computation.pdf) | [Batch-factorization paper](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/papers/Fan_Factories_Multiple_Number_Fields_in_Factorization_Factories_and_Batch_NFS.pdf)
> [zh] **DeepMind 的 AI 辅助密码分析针对旧 RSA，并非破解现代加密**：Pushmeet Kohli 11 月 7 日 01:07 北京时间原帖附两篇工作论文。一篇以原实验约 1/3.5 的 CPU 用时复现已知的 1024 位模根实验；另一篇在标准数域筛启发式假设下，先支付约一次分解的预计算成本，以主导项估算后续 1024 位整数的单个分解成本比 MNFS 低约 30 倍。作者明确表示，这些结果不威胁现有 2048 位及更长 RSA 密钥。

Network secrets only support publicly reachable APIs in Anthropic-hosted environments and do not apply during setup scripts. https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2118795192090386454 | https://code.claude.com/docs/en/cloud-environments#add-network-secrets
> [zh] **Claude Code 简化云端仓库配置**：ClaudeDevs 于 11 月 7 日 03:58 北京时间宣布为 Pro/Max/Ultra 提供依赖、网络和 API Key 配置辅助，并保存环境复用。官方文档区分由虚拟机外部注入的网络密钥，以及 Claude 可读取的普通环境变量；网络密钥仅支持 Anthropic 托管环境中的公网 API，不适用于启动配置脚本。

## 有趣用法和干货
1. **meng shao 解读 Anthropic：Agent 记忆需要纠错，不能只堆笔记**：11 月 7 日 09:25 北京时间原帖指向 11 月 6 日工程文。Anthropic 在 25 题内部语料实验中，自报知识库加搜索满足 61% 评分细则，单用搜索为 53%，并非答对题目比例；部分单文档任务没有提升。实践重点是回查原始记录、保存已验证规则、保持注入内容精简；后台整理仍有计算与维护成本。https://x.com/shao__meng/status/2118877430320418999 | https://www.anthropic.com/research/components-of-agent-memory
> [en] **meng shao on Anthropic: agent memory needs correction, not just more notes**: the November 7 post at 09:25 Beijing links to the November 6 engineering article. Anthropic reports 61% of rubric criteria met with wiki plus search versus 53% with search alone on 25 internal-corpus questions, not question accuracy; some single-document tasks showed no gain. Practical lessons include checking original records, keeping validated rules and limiting injected context, while accounting for background computation and maintenance.
