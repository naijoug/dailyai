## 新产品
1. **StateWeave**: runs your AI agent against stateful clones of the apps it works in (Slack, Gmail, Salesforce, Shopify, Zendesk, Google Calendar, Sheets, Drive, WhatsApp, Teams, with Stripe and more coming), each with the same APIs, MCP servers and its own UI. You describe a scenario to fill every app with consistent data, inject faults such as outages, expired tokens, 401s and cut-off responses, and get graded runs showing which checks failed and the exact calls behind them, with a fresh copy every run; swapping your production URL for the sandbox URL is the integration. The founder says he built it after a test run in real Salesforce marked a live deal Closed Lost; asked about writes that time out but actually succeed, he pointed to a `hang_after_commit` fault that makes agents create duplicates. It was #9 on Product Hunt’s day rank at fetch. https://stateweave.net/ | https://www.producthunt.com/products/stateweave

## 科技新闻
1. **Universal XSS in uBlock Origin, found mostly by an AI model**: Smitty van Bodegom disclosed a one-click universal XSS in uBlock Origin (the MV2 Firefox version; a similar but less serious issue hit uBO Lite) where a malicious site could trick the extension into adding arbitrary trusted filters, which can inject JavaScript, and so run code in the context of any site. The attack embeds the element picker’s extension page in an invisible iframe, drives it to the desired filter, and positions it so the user clicks the hidden “Create” button; on Firefox the per-install extension UUID was leaked through a default filter’s fetch hook. It is fixed, with a GitHub advisory. He writes that “GPT-5.6 Sol did most of the hard work of finding the issue,” which he then verified, and Claude wrote most of the PoC code. The HN thread had about 416 points and 120 comments. https://iter.ca/post/ubo-uxss/ | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50513160
> [zh] **uBlock Origin 曝通用 XSS，主要由 AI 模型发现**：Smitty van Bodegom 披露了 uBlock Origin（Firefox 的 MV2 版本；uBO Lite 也有类似但较轻的问题）的一键通用 XSS：恶意网站能诱使扩展添加任意「可信过滤规则」，而这类规则能注入 JavaScript，进而在任意网站上下文中执行代码。攻击方式是把元素拾取器的扩展页面嵌进一个透明 iframe，驱动它填好想要的规则，再摆好位置诱导用户点中隐藏的「创建」按钮；Firefox 上每次安装随机的扩展 UUID 则通过一条默认规则的 fetch 钩子泄露。问题已修复并发布 GitHub 安全公告。作者写道「找漏洞的大部分硬活是 GPT-5.6 Sol 干的」，他负责验证，PoC 代码大部分由 Claude 编写。HN 讨论约 416 分、120 条评论。
2. **Old Bitly links hijacked in years-old tweets from brands and officials**: Ransomnews found old, inactive Bitly links in posts from Burger King, McDonald’s and Donald Trump re-created to point at unrelated content; later updates add CNN reports of redirected links in old posts from Bill Gates, Justin Trudeau, Mike Pence, the FBI and the US Department of Defense. Bitly confirmed it had found an issue that “allowed bad actors to repurpose certain old, inactive links,” suspended the accounts involved, is disabling the affected links and is working on protections. HN: about 312 points and 158 comments. https://ransomnews.com/burger-king-tweet-bitly-link-hijack-2026/ | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50511395
> [zh] **旧 Bitly 短链被劫持，波及品牌和政要多年前的推文**：Ransomnews 发现，汉堡王、麦当劳和特朗普旧帖里早已失效的 Bitly 短链被重新创建，指向无关内容；后续更新提到 CNN 报道，比尔·盖茨、特鲁多、彭斯、FBI 和美国国防部旧帖里的链接也被重定向。Bitly 确认发现了一个「让不法分子能重新利用某些旧的、失效链接」的问题，已封禁相关账号、正在禁用受影响链接，并在加固防护。HN 约 312 分、158 条评论。
3. **IT之家：IMF 总裁警告全球经济「靠 AI 这条腿支撑」，AI 循环融资超 1 万亿美元**：据 CNBC，格奥尔基耶娃在东盟峰会期间表示，若市场对 AI 投资信心骤降、资金撤离，全球增长可能被削弱，「这条腿出现摇晃，就可能引发金融稳定问题」。IMF 估计 AI 企业之间的循环融资（投资方注资、资金再以采购订单等形式回流）已超过 1 万亿美元，约三分之二来自债务融资，「规模还算不上特别庞大，但增长速度非常快」；报道还提到英伟达过去一个月市值蒸发超 1 万亿美元，瑞银估计若英伟达收缩融资，AI 产业链资金供给可能减少 500 亿美元以上。https://www.ithome.com/1/027/861.htm
> [en] **IT Home: IMF chief warns the world economy is “standing on one leg — AI,” with AI circular financing above $1 trillion**: per CNBC, Kristalina Georgieva said at the ASEAN summit that a sudden loss of confidence in AI investment could weaken global growth, and if that leg wobbles it could become a financial-stability problem. The IMF estimates circular financing among AI companies (investors fund AI firms and the money flows back through purchase orders) now exceeds $1 trillion, about two-thirds of it debt, “not especially large yet, but growing very fast”; the report also notes Nvidia lost more than $1 trillion in market value over the past month and cites a UBS estimate that if Nvidia pulled back, AI supply-chain funding could shrink by $50 billion or more.
