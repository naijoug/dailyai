1. **Lovable apps leaked passports, 200,000+ chat messages and other sensitive data**: researchers at Crissy Field GmbH used Common Crawl to identify and analyze 159,041 Lovable projects (Dec 2025–Sep 2026). 34,545 used Supabase; 24,664 sites sent Supabase records on first load without login. Additional read-only requests found email addresses in 10,560 tables across 7,403 of those 24,664 sites (30%). The authors explicitly say 30% is not a confirmed leak rate and the Common Crawl sample is not representative of all Lovable apps. Serious examples they actually retrieved include a German lifestyle forum that exposed 80,000+ profiles (names, emails, dates of birth, gender) plus more than 200,000 chat messages; event and restaurant operators that exposed ticket and food-sale records including payment information and ticket codes; a property manager that exposed AI summaries of internal tenant/owner emails; a service provider that exposed 1,000+ identity-document records (passport numbers, expiration dates, nationalities); and one case that made 280,000 customer emails accessible. They could not establish misuse. On HN (686 points, 283 comments) the author said he had submitted the same post two weeks earlier and it got little traction. Lovable’s Head of Security, posting as igorandri, thanked the researchers and said the root cause in these apps was insufficient Row-Level Security, “exactly what our scanning is built to catch.” The company says it auto-scans every app before publish, offers a deeper SAST scan free for all users, a “Fix” action, optional auto-fix, and can block publishing on critical findings. https://www.crissyfield.de/research/lovable-en/ | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50499017 | https://lovable.dev/blog/find-it-fix-it-ship-it

3. **Google Research on Antigravity `/teamwork` results in math, chemistry and systems**: Vahab Mirrokni’s official post (13 Nov) describes `/teamwork` as a multi-agent pattern — coordinators, specialized agents with partitioned context, critics/auditors, declarative “patterns,” plus `/boost` — used with Gemini 4 Argon, Carbon and Hydrogen, “done in collaboration with human experts,” with more features coming to external Antigravity users “in the coming weeks.” This is different from the already-covered `/run-workflow` launch. In mathematics, Google plus CUHK, USC and CMU used `/teamwork`’s Long Proof pattern (Stellar Colosseum) to prove the 2013 Courtade–Kumar conjecture in full generality; the entire proof was formalized in Lean 4, “producing 50 million lines of code.” Gemini first found counterexamples to a generalized conjecture; later work extended the proof from balanced to unbalanced functions “without human input.” The authors also say humans verified, simplified or edited other claimed resolutions, including near-optimal sample bounds in adaptive data analysis (open since 2014). In chemistry, 30 specialized agents plus a physics-informed spectral simulator reached 95% top-1 exact match and 100% top-3 accuracy on complex benchmark molecules, “nearly doubling single-agent performance.” In systems, `/teamwork` plus Argon was used to build FarArray; claimed figures include lock-free Connected Components scaling to 1-trillion-edge graphs with 60x–90x throughput and 40x–180x speedups at 6x lower RAM versus TCP KV stores. The blog landed in the previous tech window but was not used then; an HN discussion in this window had 173 points and 39 comments. https://research.google/blog/advancing-science-engineering-with-antigravitys-teamwork-feature/ | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50496811
> [zh] **Google Research 公布 Antigravity `/teamwork` 在数学、化学和系统问题上的结果**：Vahab Mirrokni 的官方文章（11 月 13 日）把 `/teamwork` 写成一种多 Agent 模式——协调者、上下文分区的专职 Agent、批评者/审计者、声明式「patterns」，再加上 `/boost`——与 Gemini 4 的 Argon、Carbon 和 Hydrogen 一起使用，「与人类专家合作完成」，并说未来几周还会有更多功能向外部 Antigravity 用户开放。这和此前已经报道的 `/run-workflow` 不是同一件事。数学方面，Google 与香港中文大学、南加州大学和卡内基梅隆大学用 `/teamwork` 的 Long Proof 模式（Stellar Colosseum）完整证明了 2013 年的 Courtade–Kumar 猜想；整份证明用 Lean 4 形式化，「写出了 5,000 万行代码」。Gemini 先找到了一个推广猜想的反例；后来又把证明从平衡函数扩展到非平衡函数，「没有人工介入」。作者还写到，其他一些据称已解决的开放问题经过了人工核实、简化或编辑，其中包括自 2014 年以来一直开放的自适应数据分析近最优样本界。化学方面，30 个专职 Agent 加上一个有物理约束的光谱模拟器，在复杂基准分子上达到 95% 的 top-1 精确匹配和 100% 的 top-3 准确率，「几乎是单 Agent 表现的两倍」。系统方面，`/teamwork` 加 Argon 被用来构建 FarArray；文中给出的数字包括无锁 Connected Components 可扩展到万亿边图，吞吐量是 TCP KV 存储的 60–90 倍，速度提升 40–180 倍，内存只相当于其六分之一。这篇博客其实出现在上一期科技汇总的时间窗口里，当时没有采用；本窗口内的 HN 讨论有 173 分、39 条评论。
