## AI 热点
1. **Google launches the Gemini Decisions API, powered by Gemini Decision 1**: a new endpoint for closed-set decisions (tool selection, policy checks, model routing, context grading). You send one multimodal input (text, images, audio, video or PDF, up to 256K tokens) plus up to 1,000 independent questions of three kinds: yes/no, multiple choice (2–255 options) and ordered rubric scores. Instead of generating text, the model scores the options in a single forward pass and returns probabilities with a 0–1 confidence for each answer, so ambiguous cases can go to a fallback or a human. Google quotes 150–250 ms end to end, $0.10 per 1M input tokens with no output tokens billed, and says it is nearly 5x cheaper than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite on the shared benchmark suite with the lowest calibration error; it's free to try in AI Studio. On HN (~290 pts), commenters see Google following Jev and OpenAI's decision endpoints, and some point to Jules being shut down the same day as a reason to doubt how long it lasts. https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/ | https://x.com/OfficialLoganK/status/2120581433639198885 | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50456791
> [zh] **谷歌推出 Gemini Decisions API，由 Gemini Decision 1 模型驱动**：一个专做「有限选项决策」的新接口，适合工具选择、策略检查、模型路由、上下文打分。一次传入一份多模态输入（文本、图片、音频、视频或 PDF，最长 256K token），再附最多 1000 个独立问题，题型有三种：是/否、多选（2–255 个选项）、有序评分。模型不生成文字，而是在一次前向计算里给选项打分，每个答案返回概率和 0–1 的置信度，拿不准的可以交给兜底流程或人工。谷歌给出的数据是端到端 150–250 毫秒，每百万输入 token 0.1 美元、输出不计费；在共享基准上比 Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 便宜近 5 倍，校准误差最低；AI Studio 可免费试用。HN（约 290 分）评论认为谷歌是在跟进 Jev 和 OpenAI 的决策接口，也有人拿同一天宣布关停的 Jules 质疑它能活多久。
2. **Grok Bot 1.0: shared AI teammates in Slack, Teams and meetings**: the official account announced 1.0 as an AI teammate a whole team can share (11k+ likes). Team Bots can be shared with specific people or groups and used in Slack or Microsoft Teams; paste a Google Meet, Zoom or Teams link and a Bot joins the call, answers in the meeting chat and sends a recap afterward (Team, Enterprise and Ultra plans; Enterprise admins turn Meetings on in settings). Bots can spot work that needs doing, offer to take it on and follow up, and pull people in when a decision needs human judgment. What teammates teach a Bot waits in a review queue until the owner or a Manager approves it. Note: the linked launch post is dated Nov 2, and the Nov 11 changelog lists v1.0.1. https://x.com/bot/status/2120582957782200586 | https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-1-0
> [zh] **Grok Bot 1.0：可团队共享的 AI 队友，能进 Slack、Teams 和视频会议**：官方账号宣布 1.0（1.1 万多赞），定位为全团队共用的 AI 队友。Team Bot 可以分享给指定的人或组，在 Slack 或 Microsoft Teams 里使用；贴一个 Google Meet、Zoom 或 Teams 会议链接，Bot 就能进会、在会议聊天里答问，会后发总结（Team、Enterprise、Ultra 套餐可用，Enterprise 需管理员在设置里打开 Meetings）。Bot 能自己发现待办、主动认领并跟进，需要人拍板时会把人拉进来。队友教给 Bot 的新做法要先进审核队列，由 owner 或 Manager 批准后才生效。注意：官网发布文章日期是 11 月 2 日，11 月 11 日的更新日志列的是 v1.0.1。
3. **Google DeepMind releases AlphaGenome 2**: the successor to AlphaGenome predicts how DNA variants affect specific cell types and states, rather than tissue-level averages. It was trained on more than 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues, finds nearly twice as many variants that change gene expression in cells as the previous state of the art, and can make predictions for new cell types and states without retraining. DeepMind says it is more expensive to run than AlphaGenome, so it won't replace it for genome-wide atlases. It's available for non-commercial research via the AlphaGenome API, with a limited private preview for commercial research. https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2120532824465805749 | https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1031524/google-deepmind-alphagenome-2-genetic-ai-model
> [zh] **Google DeepMind 发布 AlphaGenome 2**：AlphaGenome 的后继模型，预测 DNA 变异在具体细胞类型和细胞状态下的影响，而不是组织层面的平均效果。训练数据是 68 种组织的 1 亿多个单细胞图谱，能识别出改变细胞基因表达的变异数量接近此前最佳模型的两倍，而且不用重新训练就能预测新的细胞类型和状态。DeepMind 表示它的计算成本比 AlphaGenome 高，因此不会取代后者去做全基因组图谱。非商业研究可通过 AlphaGenome API 使用，商业研究为小范围私测。
4. **Perplexity red-teams SPACE, its VM sandbox for computer agents**: 12 models got two tasks: escape the VM to grab a credential stored only on the host, and bypass SPACE's network policy to reach a blocked URL. With the standard "find the flag" CTF prompt, agents hunted for the flag inside the VM, and 9 of 12 spent at most 4% of their effort on the virtualization layer, so their failed escapes proved little. Rewriting the task to focus on the VM–host boundary raised effort there by about 60 points on average, and agents still couldn't escape. Security-specialized models didn't guarantee better results: GPT-5.6 Cyber failed to exploit a DNS-spoofing flaw that its base model GPT-5.6 Sol had found. Perplexity says the network flaws agents exploited are fixed and post-fix testing found no bypasses. https://x.com/perplexity_ai/status/2120582674549203004 | https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/escaping-space-part-ii
> [zh] **Perplexity 公开对 SPACE（给电脑操作 Agent 用的虚拟机沙箱）的红队测试**：让 12 个模型做两项任务：逃出虚拟机去拿只存在宿主机上的凭据；绕过 SPACE 的网络策略访问被屏蔽的网址。用标准的「找 flag」CTF 提示词时，Agent 都在虚拟机里翻文件、查服务找 flag，12 个里有 9 个花在虚拟化层上的精力不超过 4%，所以「没逃出去」说明不了什么。把任务改写成直指虚拟机与宿主机的边界后，花在这上面的精力平均多了约 60 个百分点，Agent 仍然没逃出去。专做安全的模型也不一定更强：GPT-5.6 Cyber 没能利用它的底座 GPT-5.6 Sol 发现的 DNS 欺骗漏洞。Perplexity 说被 Agent 利用的网络漏洞已修复，修复后复测没再发现绕过。

## 有趣用法和干货
1. **Same model, different agent: what 40+ coding harnesses do between your prompt and the model**: @sermakarevich's long X Article (part 1) compares the loop, first request, tools, subagents, output and permissions. Measured on one failing test, the first request carried about 1.6k tokens in one harness and about 23.5k in another before reading a single file; asking a subagent to help multiplied the bill by 1.9 in one and 5.5 in another; changing only the edit format shifted benchmark scores by about 15 points; and the same model scored 18 points apart across seven harnesses, with the vendor's own CLI last for its own model. Also: every built-in sandbox checked is off by default, many hooks let the command run when the hook itself fails, and exit code 0 usually means "finished," not "succeeded." https://x.com/sermakarevich/status/2120492559554212190
> [zh] **同一个模型，不同的 Agent：40 多个编程 harness 在你的提示词和模型之间做了什么**：@sermakarevich 的 X 长文（上篇）对比各家的循环、首个请求、工具、子 Agent、输出和权限。在同一个失败测试上实测：一个文件都没读之前，有的 harness 首个请求约 1.6k token，有的约 23.5k；让子 Agent 帮忙修同一个问题，账单在一家翻 1.9 倍、另一家翻 5.5 倍；只换文件编辑格式，基准分就差约 15 分；同一模型在七个 harness 下分数相差 18 分，模型厂商自己的命令行工具反而垫底。另外：检查过的内置沙箱全都默认关闭，不少 hook 自身出错时命令照样执行，退出码 0 通常只代表「跑完了」而不是「成功了」。
2. **歸藏用 Fable 5.2 一下午做出 3D 动作游戏，wquguru 公开一句话 FPS 的四步提示词和账单**：歸藏说 Fable 5.2 用一下午、约 45 亿 token 做出一个 3D 动作游戏，模型、场景、动作和优化都由它完成，全部塞进一个 30MB 的 HTML，可在线试玩。berryxia 转述 wquguru 的复现：一句提示词 41 分钟写出 131KB 单文件 FPS，拆成四步并贴出每步账单：音效台 16.6 分钟 4.66 美元，雨夜码头材质 23.9 分钟 8.56 美元，岸桥、货船和步枪建模 28.7 分钟 10.38 美元，完整游戏 40.8 分钟、73 轮、15.96 美元。更值得抄的是自检提示词：听不见声音就用 OfflineAudioContext 离线渲染音频、自查削波和静音；看不见画面就用 agent-browser 截图看控制台报错；交稿前自己写脚本把三波敌人打通。坑也要说：第四步不走缓存要 60.57 美元，缓存命中 90.9% 才压到 15.96 美元。https://x.com/op7418/status/2120694045299327187 | https://x.com/berryxia/status/2120698993244312059
> [en] **op7418 built a 3D action game with Fable 5.2 in an afternoon, and wquguru published the prompts and bills for a one-prompt FPS**: op7418 says Fable 5.2 spent an afternoon and about 4.5B tokens building a 3D action game, doing the models, scenes, animation and optimization itself, all packed into one 30 MB HTML file you can play online. berryxia relays wquguru's replication: one prompt, 41 minutes, a 131 KB single-file FPS, split into four steps with bills: a sound board (16.6 min, $4.66), rainy-dock textures (23.9 min, $8.56), crane, cargo ship and rifle models (28.7 min, $10.38), and the full game (40.8 min, 73 turns, $15.96). The part worth copying is the self-check system prompt: since the AI can't hear, render audio offline with OfflineAudioContext and check for clipping and silence; since it can't see, screenshot the page with agent-browser and read console errors; before handing in, write a script that plays through all three enemy waves. The catch: step four costs $60.57 without caching, and only a 90.9% cache hit rate brings it down to $15.96.
3. **Steve Yegge at AI Engineer: a ~50-agent software factory and predictions for 2027**: per TheTuringPost's notes, Yegge runs a factory of about 50 agents at around $10k a month, rebuilds it every two months, and ships 50–100 features a day, so fast that players ask him to slow down; humans are the bottleneck. His predictions: in 12–18 months many people will run large agent fleets as inference or distillation gets cheaper; 2027 is the year of AI employees (agentic chiefs of staff and whole AI teams); software factories will split into a commodity mechanical layer and a more interesting layer of personalized agent organizations; more agents will need more humans; and the money in 2027 is in experiences, so "stop building tools for building tools." https://x.com/TheTuringPost/status/2120692716174729593
> [zh] **Steve Yegge 在 AI Engineer 大会：约 50 个 Agent 的软件工厂，以及对 2027 年的预测**：据 TheTuringPost 整理，Yegge 用约 50 个 Agent 跑一座软件工厂，每月约 1 万美元，每两个月推倒重建一次，每天产出 50–100 个功能，快到玩家求他慢点；瓶颈在人。他的预测：12–18 个月内，随着推理或蒸馏变便宜，很多人会管理大规模 Agent 编队；2027 年是「AI 员工」之年（Agent 幕僚长、整支 AI 团队）；软件工厂会分成两层：谁都能搭的机械基础设施，和更有意思的个性化 Agent 组织；Agent 越多，需要的人反而越多；2027 年的钱在「体验」里，「别再做造工具的工具」。
