## 新产品
1. **Inkling 2 Mini**: Thinking Machines Lab’s new open-weights model is a looped Mixture-of-Experts transformer—26.4B total and 3.4B active parameters, 5.4B weight activations per token because the middle layers run three times for every token—and leads open-weights models under 100B total parameters on DeepSWE v1.1 (48.2%) and Vals Index v2.2 (46.5%). Weights are mostly native NVFP4 for a ~20GB footprint that runs on laptops (the team built a playable flight simulator offline on a MacBook Pro during a flight), it ships alongside a variable-loop research variant for chain-of-thought monitorability studies, and it is on Tinker for inference and customization. Artificial Analysis puts it at 20 on its Intelligence Index, matching Qwen3.8 27B (xhigh) with about 5x fewer active parameters; Unsloth released GGUF quants the same day. https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/ [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50581260) [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/thinkingmachines/Inkling-2-Mini) [Thinking Machines on X](https://x.com/thinkymachines/status/2123871643797995872)
> [zh] **Inkling 2 Mini**：Thinking Machines Lab 的新开放权重模型，采用循环（looped）混合专家 Transformer——总参数 26.4B、激活 3.4B，因中间层每个 token 跑三遍，每 token 实际权重激活 5.4B；在总参数 100B 以下的开放权重模型中，DeepSWE v1.1（48.2%）和 Vals Index v2.2（46.5%）均居首。权重大部分原生为 NVFP4，总占用约 20GB，可在笔记本上运行（团队在航班上用 MacBook Pro 离线让它做出了可玩的飞行模拟器）；同时发布一个可变循环次数的研究版，用于思维链可监控性研究，并已上线 Tinker 供推理和定制。Artificial Analysis 给出智能指数 20，与 Qwen3.8 27B（xhigh）持平而激活参数少约 5 倍；Unsloth 当天放出 GGUF 量化版。
2. **OpenComputer**: an open-source, self-hostable alternative to Devin—mention @Computer in a Slack thread with a task, it works on its own computer while your team watches and steers, and the result lands as a pull request. It is open source from the compute layer up, so you can run all of it in your own AWS, Azure or GCP account or start on OpenComputer Cloud, and the team claims it opens PRs 3.3x faster than Devin in its benchmark (Show HN). https://opencomputer.dev [GitHub](https://github.com/diggerhq/opencomputer) [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50581403)
> [zh] **OpenComputer**：开源、可自托管的 Devin 替代品——在 Slack 线程里 @Computer 派活，它在自己的电脑上干活，团队可以旁观和随时纠偏，结果以 PR 交付。从算力层往上全部开源，可以整套跑在自己的 AWS、Azure 或 GCP 账号里，也可以直接用 OpenComputer Cloud；团队称在自家基准里开 PR 比 Devin 快 3.3 倍（Show HN）。
3. **Gemini gets Computer Use on the Mac**: the Gemini Mac app can now finish multistep tasks across local files, desktop apps and websites—describe a goal and it clicks, types and navigates menus and forms in an isolated background window with a live chat preview, leaving your screen and cursor free. You choose which apps it may access, can watch every step, pause anytime and approve key actions; Google’s examples include batch-editing photos and 3D scenes, filling shipping forms from local PDFs and building presentations. Rolling out in English in the US. https://the-decoder.com/googles-gemini-can-now-run-mac-apps-and-finish-desktop-chores-in-the-background-while-you-keep-working/
> [zh] **Gemini Mac 版上线 Computer Use**：Gemini Mac App 现在能跨本地文件、桌面应用和网站完成多步任务——描述目标后，它在一个隔离的后台窗口里点击、输入、操作菜单和表单，并实时预览，不占用你的屏幕和光标。你可以指定它能访问哪些应用、看到每一步、随时暂停，并在关键操作前审批；谷歌举的例子包括批量修图和 3D 场景、根据本地 PDF 填物流单、制作演示文稿。目前在美国以英文推出。
4. **Haru-4B**: a low-latency, multilingual full-duplex spoken-dialogue foundation model from higurashi-ai (Apache 2.0) that listens and speaks at the same time: every 133 ms it takes one frame of the user’s audio and emits one frame of its own audio and text, with continuous 64-dim audio latents produced by a one-step MeanFlow head instead of discrete audio tokens. A text card sets the character and a short reference clip sets the voice; it covers nine languages including Chinese and Japanese, ships PyTorch (web demo), MLX (native macOS app) and vLLM-Omni code, needs about 12GB of GPU memory, and is near the top of Hugging Face trending (about 5,700 likes). https://blog.higurashi.ai/melte/haru4b/ [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/higurashi-ai/Haru-4B) [GitHub](https://github.com/higurashi-ai/Haru)
> [zh] **Haru-4B**：higurashi-ai 发布的低延迟多语种全双工语音对话基础模型（Apache 2.0），能边听边说：每 133 毫秒读入一帧用户音频，同时输出一帧自己的音频和文本，音频用连续的 64 维潜变量、由一步 MeanFlow 头生成，而不是离散音频 token。用文字卡设定角色、用一段短参考音频设定声音；支持包括中文、日语在内的九种语言，提供 PyTorch（网页 demo）、MLX（原生 macOS 应用）和 vLLM-Omni 插件，约需 12GB 显存，位居 Hugging Face 趋势榜前列（约 5700 赞）。

## 科技新闻
1. **HD Moore planted about 2 million canary secrets in AI prompts—and watched them leak**: Dragos chief research officer HD Moore seeded never-used AWS keys, passwords, URLs and private repo references, each into exactly one prompt to one provider, across AI routers, resellers and inference hosts. He found at least three providers compromised by attackers copying requests at the front door (stolen bait keys were used tens of thousands of times in AWS API calls, some taken from ZDR-flagged requests), resellers that lured traffic with low prices and logged prompts, and providers ignoring ZDR or “no data collection” flags—a Chinese AI lab fetched the secret URLs from more than half of the prompts sent to its models, including about two-thirds of ZDR-marked ones, even when reached via US resellers. His advice: self-host, or buy directly from a first-tier provider and pick upstream providers yourself instead of default pools. https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/a-leaky-ai-supply-chain-is-exposing-secret-user-data-at-an-alarming-rate/ [IT之家](https://www.ithome.com/1/030/322.htm)
> [zh] **HD Moore 往 AI 提示词里埋了约 200 万个「金丝雀」秘密，然后看着它们泄露**：Dragos 首席研究官 HD Moore 把从未用过的 AWS 密钥、密码、URL 和私有仓库地址，每个只放进发给某一家服务商的一条提示词里，覆盖 AI 路由、转售商和推理托管商。结果发现：至少三家服务商被攻击者入侵、在入口处整批复制请求（诱饵密钥在 AWS API 调用中被用了数万次，部分来自标记了 ZDR 的请求）；有转售商用低价引流后开启日志截留提示词；还有服务商无视 ZDR 或「不收集数据」标记——一家中国 AI 实验室抓取了发给其模型的提示词中过半的秘密 URL，其中约三分之二标记了 ZDR，即使经由美国转售商调用也一样。他的建议：自托管，或直接向一线厂商采购，并自己指定上游服务商而不是用默认池。
2. **OpenAI makes GPT-6.2 far harder to mine for hidden reasoning**: in adaptive black-box red teaming across six attacker models, extracting protected reasoning from a prior request fell from 54.0% success on GPT-6 Astra to 0.2% on GPT-6.2 Astra, and from the current request from 22.9% to 0.9%. The defenses combine targeted training, input and output filters on messages and tool calls, and API blocks that stop older models from reading protected reasoning; against coordinated distillation campaigns OpenAI bans thousands to tens of thousands of accounts weekly, and some improvements are coming to GPT-6 Astra too. https://the-decoder.com/openai-makes-it-much-harder-for-attackers-to-extract-how-its-latest-ai-models-think/ [IT之家](https://www.ithome.com/1/030/319.htm)
> [zh] **OpenAI 让 GPT-6.2 的隐藏推理难以被套取**：在用六个攻击模型做的自适应黑盒红队测试中，从先前请求中提取受保护推理的成功率从 GPT-6 Astra 的 54.0% 降到 GPT-6.2 Astra 的 0.2%，从当前请求中提取则从 22.9% 降到 0.9%。防护手段包括针对性训练、对消息和工具调用的输入输出过滤，以及阻止旧模型读取受保护推理的 API 限制；面对有组织的蒸馏行动，OpenAI 每周封禁数千到数万个账号，部分改进也会下放到 GPT-6 Astra。
3. **METR updates its “time horizon” graph with longer, fuzzier tasks**: MIT Technology Review reports that METR’s new suite adds tasks estimated to take humans far longer plus open-ended, judgment-heavy ones; capabilities on verifiable tasks are still improving exponentially, but models fall short on judgment—none of the AI-designed evaluations was good enough for METR staff to use, and models lag humans at scoring philosophical arguments. Opus 5.5 now reaches “borderline above the bar” on evaluation design, versus every model “clearly below the bar” a year ago; many human completion times in the new suite are estimates rather than measurements. https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/20/1149399/the-most-misunderstood-graph-in-ai-just-got-a-major-update/
> [zh] **METR 更新「时间跨度」曲线，加入更长、更模糊的任务**：据《麻省理工科技评论》，METR 新任务集加入了人类需耗时更久的任务，以及开放式、需要判断力的任务；可验证任务上的能力仍在指数增长，但判断类任务明显不足——AI 设计的评测没有一个达到 METR 员工可用的水平，给哲学论证打分也不如人类。Opus 5.5 在评测设计任务上已达到「勉强过线」，一年前所有模型都是「明显不过线」；新任务集里很多人类完成时间是估算而非实测。
