5. **fast-anything: “Don’t rewrite it in Rust. Rewrite the 5% that’s slow.”**: one command profiles your app, ranks functions by time × portability, records up to 500 real calls, has your coding agent (`--agent claude`, `codex` or any stdin command) port just that function into a one-function Rust crate, then replays every recorded call plus derived edge cases (NaN, ±infinity, -0.0, non-ASCII, empty and 16x inputs) before swapping it in—fully reversible, removed byte for byte. It ports the outermost portable loop so the app crosses into Rust once; in one example a port that compared bytes instead of characters was caught by 148 of 500 recorded calls. Works with Python, Node, Ruby, Java, Go, PHP and C. HN 52 points; commenters shared doing the same by hand with PyO3, and noted the repo itself is 62% Rust. https://github.com/EL4CTEO/fast-anything [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50596155)
> [zh] **fast-anything：「别用 Rust 重写整个应用，只重写慢的那 5%」**：一条命令先剖析应用，按「耗时 × 可移植性」给函数排序，录下最多 500 次真实调用，让你的编程 Agent（`--agent claude`、`codex` 或任何从 stdin 读提示的命令）只把这个函数移植成单函数 Rust crate，再回放全部录制调用和衍生的边界用例（NaN、±无穷、-0.0、非 ASCII、空输入和 16 倍大输入）后才替换进去——完全可回退，按字节原样移除。它会移植最外层可移植的循环，让应用只跨进 Rust 一次；示例里一个按字节而非字符比较的移植被 500 次录制调用中的 148 次抓出。支持 Python、Node、Ruby、Java、Go、PHP 和 C。HN 52 分；评论里有人分享用 PyO3 手工做过同样的事，也有人调侃这个仓库本身 62% 是 Rust。

## 科技新闻
1. **Together AI discloses a prompt leak that started when an AI coding agent committed a credential**: prompt samples from its serverless inference—requests to MiniMax M3, GLM-5.2 and Kimi K2.6, mainly on June 24, July 4, July 8 and July 14—were stored in a private Hugging Face repo for testing; while developing its public `xorl` repo, “an AI coding agent committed a credential” to that repo, and although scanning caught and reverted it, an unauthorized party used it to download the samples, then searched them for credentials and tested them—five were Dragos canary credentials. Together has made zero data retention the default for new accounts, moved to scoped or short-lived credentials and changed its rules for AI coding agents. The Decoder adds that OpenRouter removed three providers and revoked ZDR approval for 125 endpoints, and emailed users that third parties may have read their MiniMax requests; Novita and Phala have also published preliminary reports. HN commenters pushed back on blaming the agent: “No, ‘xorl’ did not do this— you did.” https://www.together.ai/blog/security-event-prompt-leakage-for-requests-in-june-and-july-of-2026 [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50595816) [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/security-researchers-show-how-cheap-ai-inference-providers-steal-keys-and-snoop-on-users/)
> [zh] **Together AI 披露提示词泄露事件，起因是 AI 编程 Agent 误提交了凭据**：其无服务器推理的提示词样本（发往 MiniMax M3、GLM-5.2 和 Kimi K2.6 的请求，主要来自 6 月 24 日、7 月 4 日、7 月 8 日和 7 月 14 日）被存进一个私有 Hugging Face 仓库用于测试；在开发公开仓库 `xorl` 时，「一个 AI 编程 Agent 提交了」能访问该私有仓库的凭据，虽然自动扫描发现并回滚了提交，未授权方仍在暴露期间拿到凭据下载样本，再从中搜刮其他服务的凭据并逐一测试——其中五个是 Dragos 的诱饵凭据。Together 已把零数据保留（ZDR）设为新账号默认，改用限定范围或短期凭据，并修改了使用 AI 编程 Agent 的开发流程。The Decoder 补充：OpenRouter 下架了三家供应商、撤销 125 个端点的 ZDR 资格，并邮件提醒用户发往 MiniMax 模型的请求可能被第三方读取；Novita、Phala 也已发布初步报告。HN 评论反对把锅甩给 Agent：「不，不是『xorl』干的，是你们自己。」
