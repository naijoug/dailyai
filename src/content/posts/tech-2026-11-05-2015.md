## 科技新闻
1. **OpenSSH 10.6p2: critical workaround for password-auth bypass on musl Linux without PAM**: Damien Miller warns that on Alpine-like musl builds with PAM disabled, set `PasswordAuthentication=no` or take SSH offline immediately; not an issue on non-Linux, glibc, or PAM password auth. Root cause sits outside sshd; fast release after AI rediscovery concerns. Reporter: Özgün Kültekin (Wakefield). https://www.openssh.com/releasenotes.html#10.6p2 | https://hachyderm.io/@damienmiller/117563026759098135 [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50377221)
> [zh] **OpenSSH 10.6p2：紧急缓解 musl Linux 无 PAM 时的密码认证绕过**：Damien Miller 提醒，在类似 Alpine、未启用 PAM 的 musl 构建上，应立刻设 `PasswordAuthentication=no` 或停掉 SSH；非 Linux、glibc 或 PAM 密码认证不受影响。根因在 sshd 之外；因担心 AI 复现而快速发版。报告人：Özgün Kültekin（Wakefield）。
